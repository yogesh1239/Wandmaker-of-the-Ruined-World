Four years ago, the Gremlin Disaster stole electricity from the world.

It stole lives. It stole peace.

The biggest, worst disaster in human history had stolen so much. It even stole online shopping from me.

This was an extremely serious, grave problem.

I couldn't browse the internet like before, click on a product that caught my eye, and buy it. It couldn't have been more inconvenient.

These days, product catalogs were just endless rows of text on paper—dry, lifeless things without a single photo. How was that supposed to make anyone want to buy anything?

That was going to be the sticking point when I pitched my magic wands to the mages from the Tohoku Hunting Association who were coming to help rebuild after the mushroom pandemic.

The Blue Witch barely knew the Tohoku Hunting Association's mages, and they barely knew her. Everyone in Tokyo knew her name, but the moment you crossed a prefectural border, hardly anyone had heard of her.

It was hard to say whether my old strategy of using the strongest witch in the Tokyo Witches' Council as my poster girl to build name recognition would work on them.

I heard the mages in the aid delegation would go home in a week or so. I needed a fast, impactful, easy-to-understand way to sell them on my magic wands.

While I was wondering how to pitch them, the Blue Witch, who handled advertising and publicity for my magic wands, came up with a good idea.

Give them samples.

Written catalog specs wouldn't mean much to them.

Even telling them, “This is the brand the Blue Witch herself uses,” probably wouldn't mean much.

I'd give them sample wands and let them see, hold, and use the real thing so they could feel for themselves how amazing they were.

Then orders would definitely come pouring in. We have absolute confidence in our magic wands' performance and quality.

Early on the morning the Tohoku Hunting Association's mages were due in Tokyo, the Blue Witch came to Okutama to pick up the samples. When she found out they still weren't finished, she started lecturing me.

“Listen. This is the kind of thing you make by the day before. Plan ahead better.”

“I just want to squeeze out a little more quality. The meeting isn't until this afternoon. There's still time, right?”

“I'm the one who has to make up for your delay. Don't get cocky.”

“Sorry about that.”

When I apologized, the Blue Witch gave a resigned sigh and sat down in a chair at the edge of the workshop.

After all that, she was still willing to wait for me. She really was a good person. Sorry I'm always so demanding.

“But is there really that much to refine? The quality looks good enough.”

As she spoke, the Blue Witch picked up the general-purpose, mass-produced magic wand that had been lying on my workbench.

Well, yeah. I get what she's saying. That would probably go over well as a sample too.

Multilayer processing for massive magic amplification.

Spherical polishing.

Backlash-prevention mechanism.

With all three classic modifications for basic magic-wand functions, you can't really go wrong.

But this time, I wanted to go one step further. If I was handing out samples, it had to be a new product. That would get people talking and catch their interest.

“What I'm making now is a new type. Magic-school customization.”

“Magic-school customization?”

The Blue Witch parroted the words back to me. I stopped working, showed her the half-finished wand, and explained.

“Magic backlash happens because magic power flows back into the caster, right? Your freezing magic leaves the caster shivering, foresight magic wrecks the brain, eyeball magic causes blindness, and fire magic causes burns.”

“That's why the backlash-prevention mechanism reduces the backlash.”

“Right. So here's another twist—one extra step. You know personal-color Gremlins? I'm using one in the backlash-prevention mechanism.”

Personal-color Gremlins were a type of melt-recast Gremlin made by mixing components extracted from blood into Gremlins melted at high temperatures. Once they cooled and hardened, the blood components dyed them the individual's own color.

If I used my blood, they were gold. If I used the Blue Witch's blood, they were blue.

With a hundred people, you'd get a hundred different Gremlin colors.

“These days, we only use personal-color Gremlins in amulets, but I kept running experiments to see whether they had other uses. Then I found a pretty useful property last night, so I'm hurrying to build it into the samples. I'll add it to Cyanos later too.”

“So what does all that mean? Spare me the theory. Keep it short.”

“In short, I found that a personal-color Gremlin refined from your blood reduces freezing magic's backlash even further, since you're good at freezing magic. Compare them.”

When I handed her the half-finished wand, the Blue Witch gave me a dubious look, held the conventional wand in one hand and the new-type wand in the other, then cast magic.

“<ruby>Vaa-ra<rt>Freeze</rt></ruby>. <ruby>Vaa-ra<rt>Freeze</rt></ruby>... Huh. The new type really does have slightly less backlash.”

“Right?”

Cold mist billowed twice from the wand tips, and the Blue Witch nodded, convinced.

When the Blue Witch became a witch, she awakened to freezing magic. She was also resistant to it.

That same resistance carried over to the backlash-prevention mechanism made from the Blue Witch's personal-color Gremlin, allowing it to reduce freezing-magic backlash even further.

“I haven't had many samples to work with, so I haven't done much testing, but I confirmed it with eyeball magic too. You know how casting eyeball-familiar magic makes your eyes feel squeezed tight until they hurt? Using the Eyeball Witch's personal-color Gremlin reduces that backlash too.”

“Oh. Then what about fire magic and foresight magic?”

“We should see similar backlash reduction.”

“Are you a genius...? Put Ori on it and technology leaps a hundred years ahead.”

“That's because I'm a genius Wand Maker.”

When I puffed out my chest, the Blue Witch laughed.

Bwahahaha! I'm a genius. Call me the ultimate, perfected form of “you get good at what you love.” I'll improve wands every chance I get.

Still, this new mechanism wasn't necessarily a complete upgrade over the old one.

For example, customizing it to reduce freezing magic's backlash made the backlash from every other school worse.

An eyeball-magic customization did the same thing: backlash from every other school got worse.

The backlash-reduction rate for one specific school of magic went up, but the backlash-reduction rate for other schools went down.

“There are downsides, sure, but you use almost nothing but freezing magic, right, Blue Witch? And I hear the Eyeball Witch uses almost nothing but eyeball magic. I think the benefits outweigh the downsides.”

“Right. Witches and mages find their own school of magic easiest to use and most stable. Other people's schools are hard to control, so they only use them as support. So this... magic-school customization? It should be well received. I'm grateful for it too.”

Magic-school customization for wands was basically attribute specialization. It offered big benefits to Transcendents and wizards who fought mainly with one school of magic.

For people who used a variety of schools equally, the downsides were probably greater, but they could choose the right setup for the situation.

With the Blue Witch's seal of approval, I confidently moved on to the samples' finishing touches.

I don't know what kind of magic the Tohoku mages use, but whatever it is, you can never have too much backlash reduction. I really want them to enjoy the samples' custom options to the fullest and discover how good Ori-made custom magic wands are.

The one I'm making now is customized for freezing magic, but of course I can customize a wand for any other school too. If you need a custom job, please place an order. Heh heh heh.

Once I finished the custom wand, the samples were finally ready. I carefully set one general-purpose magic wand and one magic-school-customized wand in my best paulownia box, packed snugly with cushioning material.

I put in the specification sheets, closed the lid, and attached a noshi[^1] marked “Gift.” Done.

“All right. I have a little time left. Might as well give Cyanos magic-school customization too.”

“If you can do it quickly, do it.”

“I can do it quickly. It won't even take ten minutes.”

“That really is quick.”

I took Cyanos from the Blue Witch and immediately got to work improving it.

As a freezing-magic user, the Blue Witch naturally used freezing magic a lot, so I took the handle apart and replaced its default milky-white backlash-prevention mechanism with one made from blue personal-color Gremlin. That improved its freezing-magic reduction rate even further.

As I worked briskly, the Blue Witch watched with her elbow on the workbench and her cheek in her hand, then spoke in awe.

“Every time I see it, your dexterity is on another level. It's practically magic.”

“It's normal to me, though.”

“What part of that is normal? You really aren't using magic, right?”

“I'm just naturally like this. It's my constitution. You know how there are short sleepers who are fine even with ridiculously little sleep? Or people who can wiggle their ears on command. I'm just another one of those freaks of nature.”

The Blue Witch still looked skeptical after I explained it, but that was the only explanation I had.

Humans are more amazing than they realize. Some people have survived seven lightning strikes. Others have been bitten by venomous snakes hundreds of times and ended up immune to venom that should normally be lethal. These true stories sound completely fake.

Some people go beyond human limits. So is it really that strange for someone to match electronic precision machinery by hand?

“You're static-prone too. You became a witch because of your constitution, right? Constitutions are amazing.”

“When you put it that way, I start to think so. Then is your tactlessness a constitution too?”

She said it sarcastically, so I gave a big nod and puffed out my chest.

“Of course it's a constitution. How do I put it? I was born without much interest in other people. Even pretending to be interested is a pain. My own analysis is that I don't understand the subtleties of people's emotions because I don't care to get to know them. That's probably why people call me tactless.”

“You understand that much about yourself, and you still won't fix it...?”

“I'm telling you, it's a constitution. You can't handle spicy food, but would you train yourself until you could? Huh? You wouldn't, right? You get by just fine as long as you don't eat spicy food.”

I couldn't change who I was.

Both my dexterity and my poor social skills were part of me. Fighting my own nature would only make life harder. Grandpa had told me to be myself in a place where I could be myself.

“You're a pain. If you aren't interested in people, I bet you've never liked anyone either.”

“That's not true. I like the Blue Witch.”

“...”

I'd only said something completely obvious, but the Blue Witch quickly turned away and fell silent.

Huh? Why all of a sudden?

Why? Did I say something that upset her?

We're friends, so naturally I like her. In the first place, I wouldn't become friends with someone I didn't like.

If I were friends with someone I hated, that wouldn't be real friendship. It would be nothing more than playing at fake friendship.

Therefore, friends = liking the other person. The equation holds. It should.

There shouldn't be any logical inconsistency, yet the phenomenon of an unnatural interruption in the conversation has, in fact, occurred. That means some profound mystery of communication beyond my understanding is probably at work.

“Why did you go quiet? Did I say something weird? If you tell me what's weird, I'll consider improving it.”

“...No. When I talk to you, sometimes I feel like I'm the one who's wrong. You have no tact or hesitation, but I don't hate that no-guard, one-hundred-percent-honest way you talk. Stay as you are, Ori.”

“Uh. Okay...?”

I couldn't follow her logic, but apparently I was fine as I was. Fine by me, then.

My hands never stopped while we talked, so the upgrade was done just as our conversation wound down.

The Blue Witch took the upgraded Cyanos Ver. 2.2 and the paulownia box for the Tohoku Hunting Association, then left. At a witch's speed, getting from Okutama to the meeting in the city center would take no time at all.

If I could sell magic wands in Tohoku, I'd open up a much wider market and gain more customers.

I had put everything I had into that product.

All that was left was for the Blue Witch, my Minister of Sales and Promotion, to do some proper selling.
