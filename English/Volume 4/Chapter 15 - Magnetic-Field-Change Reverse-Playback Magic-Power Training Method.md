The nice stoat who understood my tastes had sent not only the paper on the magic-power training method to Okutama, but also solid supplementary information on how it had been discovered.

I read it gratefully, and it was pretty interesting.

Tokyo Magic University's Department of Mutation Studies researched mutations in monsters and humans.

Right after the Gremlin Disaster, people with electrically charged constitutions became witches and mages. Some animals also began turning into monsters. If they could figure out the principles behind those mutations, they might be able to artificially create witches and mages, or prevent animals from becoming monsters. Maybe.

There was also a chance they could return people like the Spider Witch, who suffered because she had become a witch, to normal. The possibilities were endless.

But until now, that promising Department of Mutation Studies had stayed in the background.

The Department of Gremlin Engineering had produced major results, making general-purpose magic wands and improving backlash-prevention mechanisms.

The Department of Magic Linguistics had also been doing great work. Not to mention all kinds of bypass incantations. The Nameless Epic was another huge and important research topic.

The Department of Combat Studies gave military training based on real combat data from security forces around the country. It refined wizard combat and produced dozens of skilled wizards every year.

The Department of Monster Studies had devised the Class A, B, and C monster classifications, pushed forward research into monster ecology, and dug into magic-beast handling brought by the Hokkaido Magic Beast Farm. It was important.

The Department of Magic Medicine was super important. It mostly researched mushroom disease, but it also made response manuals in case an unknown magical disease outbreak occurred. I had experienced the results of its research into medical uses for magic and monster products when I had appendicitis.

The Department of Mutation Studies had not put out any especially noticeable results, but that was only because its work had not paid off yet. It had been steadily building up data the whole time.

Discovering and putting the magic-power training method into practical use was the product of that steady data collection.

The magic-power training method had been derived from three phenomena that reduced magic-power capacity.

First was the reduction from magic-depletion fainting.

The Department of Mutation Studies professor was pretty eccentric. A little strange.

He was normal during lectures. But when a student said they wanted to join his laboratory, he gave them a trial: serve as a test subject once.

“Anyone without the resolve to become a test subject themselves has no right to experiment on other living things!” he said.

It was kind of like bushido.[^1] Not something a researcher should say, but he said it, so there was nothing to be done.

The Department of Mutation Studies was normal if you only took its lectures, but the bar was high if you wanted to join the laboratory.

The Department of Mutation Studies laboratory used volunteer students who wanted to join it to research the principles of magic-depletion fainting.

When you used up all your magic power, your magic-power capacity decreased.

What exactly happened then?

Witches and mages gained huge amounts of magic power when they mutated. Then the reverse, a decrease in magic power, had to be a kind of mutation too.

After they investigated in every way they could think of, they found that a change in the magnetic field accompanied magic-power reduction from magic-depletion fainting. Weak magnetic force was emitted, centered on the head.

Magic power and Gremlins were closely related to electricity.

And electricity was closely related to magnetism.

It was entirely plausible that changes in magic power would come with magnetism.

Witches and mages could sense and control magic power, but they could not sense magnetism. Without the Department of Mutation Studies' human experiments, they probably would not have discovered magnetic-field changes when magic-power capacity decreased.

At the Department of Mutation Studies laboratory, they tried reverse-playing those magnetic-field changes.

They thought that if they reproduced around the head, in reverse, the magnetic-field changes that occurred when magic power decreased, magic power might increase.

They immediately built a dedicated laboratory, gathered magnets, created a magnetic field in the room, and experimented on one student by giving them reverse-playback magnetic-field changes.

As a result, that student went through something awful.

They got the full set of brain damage: severe headaches, nausea, dizziness, hallucinations, reduced attention, aphasia, agnosia, memory impairment, and so on.

The experiment had been short, which was partly why it did not kill the student, but they ended up suffering from aftereffects.

It was a negative result, but they had definitely observed a change.

The Department of Mutation Studies wanted more data!

But it was way too dangerous. The fact that the student had not died in the first experiment was just luck. The Department of Mutation Studies professor used himself for a follow-up experiment and ended up half-paralyzed from the aftereffects.

Professor Ohinata, the university president, was not happy about this either and issued an order not to conduct overly dangerous experiments. Apparently she had not forbidden them too strongly because the Department of Magic Linguistics had also produced a huge number of deaths from failed experiments early on. She could not exactly criticize other people.

The second magic-power-capacity reduction phenomenon the Department of Mutation Studies handled was Gremlin implantation.

If you implanted a monster Gremlin in your body, your magic-power capacity decreased by an amount corresponding to that Gremlin.

Unlike magic-depletion fainting, the decrease from this happened slowly over several days, so it was easier to observe what was going on.

Magnetic-field changes were also observed during Gremlin implantation, just like with magic-depletion fainting.

But the way they changed differed from what happened during magic-depletion fainting.

The Department of Mutation Studies used a condemned prisoner it had obtained after a tug-of-war with the Department of Magic Medicine as a living human specimen for a magnetic-field reverse-playback experiment. The prisoner suffered numbness on one side of the body, visual impairment, fainting, brain hemorrhaging, and more, and died.

Living human specimens were hard to obtain, and they could not do dangerous experiments without them. The troubled Department of Mutation Studies spent a while analyzing the data it had gathered, building theories, and testing hypotheses.

The Department of Mutation Studies' view was that something in the head was deeply involved in increases and decreases in magic power. There was no doubt about it.

Its evidence was “magnetic-field changes in the head,” “magnetoreception,” and “mushroom disease.”

First, both magic-depletion fainting and Gremlin implantation showed magnetic-field changes centered on the head.

Next, humans had a sense called magnetoreception in their heads that let them detect that field.

According to a paper published in 2019 by a joint research team from the University of Tokyo and the California Institute of Technology, all humans had the ability to sense magnetism.

Magnetoreception, which sensed the Earth's magnetic field, was something many animals that traveled long distances had, such as migratory birds and salmon.

Humans had magnetoreception too, though it was extremely weak. According to the paper, an experiment that magnetically stimulated the heads of 34 men and women found that their brain waves reacted differently depending on the direction of the magnetic field.

This reaction happened completely unconsciously. It was so slight that you could not even notice you were sensing magnetism.

But humans definitely had a sixth sense in their heads, different from taste, touch, hearing, sight, and smell: magnetoreception.

Finally, mushroom disease.

The mushrooms in the mushroom pandemic had always grown on people's heads.

Without a single exception, on their heads.

There had to be a reason for that. During incantations, magic power was always emitted from the mouth. In other words, from the head.

There had to be something invisible but important to magic power in the head.

The Department of Mutation Studies carefully checked its data, worked hard on building theories, and came up with several hypotheses, but there were just too few living human specimens! Magnetic-field reverse-playback experiments were too dangerous to do very often.

Because of that, the Department of Mutation Studies' research moved at a turtle's pace.

Then the magic drug and five hundred rank-and-file Arataki Group prisoners were dumped in its lap.

The Department of Mutation Studies went wild.

A third magic-power-capacity reduction phenomenon!

A huge number of living human specimens they would not feel any guilt about using up!

Using this insanely exciting new research material, the Department of Mutation Studies pushed through dozens of experiments it had only worked out in theory, all at once.

It used up a huge number of living human specimens to compare the three magic-power-capacity reduction phenomena and work out the pattern behind brain damage during magnetic-field reverse playback.

That let it establish a magic-power training method that gave the head appropriate magnetic-field changes, increased magic-power capacity, and did not cause brain damage.

According to the rules the Department of Mutation Studies discovered after tireless effort, the magic-power training method needed two things: magnetic-field changes and meditation.

Magnetic-field changes were made with magnets. They put a person inside a device consisting of an upright coffin covered in magnets, then slowly rotated the coffin at a fixed speed. This gave the head appropriate magnetic-field changes.

But magnetic-field changes alone were not enough. They increased magic power, but you got hit with awful brain damage.

That was why meditation was needed.

Roughly put, if you thought about unnecessary things while the magnetic field was changing, you went crazy and died.

There were various neuroscience explanations for this, but apparently there were also unresolved phenomena that neuroscience could not explain. The Department of Mutation Studies had only figured out the part that went, “We don't know why, but if you do it this way, it's okay.”

That was meditation.

You could not move while the magnetic field was changing. The point emitting the magnetic field would shift.

And you could not think about anything.

You had to empty your mind. You had to enter a state where you thought about nothing and calmed your mind, called things like “clear mirror, still water” or “the state of no self.”

If you disturbed your mind even a little or thought about something, it showed up in the brain and caused brain damage.

It sounded like a pretty strict requirement, but anyone could learn this meditation through training, and some people could do it without training.

A high-ranking monk invited to the university as a meditation instructor entered a meditative state in one shot, increased his magic power without taking any brain damage at all, and came out totally fine. The Department of Mutation Studies professor also succeeded in one shot.

There were individual differences among the students in the Department of Mutation Studies laboratory, but they could enter a meditative state after around 3 to 20 days. All of them increased their magic power without taking any brain damage.

Even if the meditative state broke during magnetic-field reverse playback, your headache gradually got worse as a warning sign before brain damage set in. If you stopped right after you felt a headache, there was no harm.

However, meditation did not let you avoid damage perfectly, so the medical view was that it was best to leave 24 hours between each session of magic-power training. Doing sessions back-to-back increased the risk of brain damage.

So magic-power training was once a day.

Magic-power training required building and operating an appropriate magnetic field, and meditation skill was also essential.

It was not something anyone could do easily.

But if you had the equipment and learned how to meditate, anyone could do it, even if it was difficult.

A single magnetic-field reverse playback during magic-power training could increase magic-power capacity by 0.2 K.

There were no individual differences in that number. It was still unknown whether witches and mages could increase their magic power too, because nobody at the Tokyo Witches' Council could meditate. Some had already started training, though.

They still did not know whether there was a limit to how much magic power could increase. In other words, there might be a cap where you could grow up to a certain point but no further.

For now, all the Department of Mutation Studies students had completed five meditation sessions, and nobody's growth had stopped yet. It looked safe to think everyone could increase by at least 1 K.

As a special case, beast handlers were known to be unable to increase their magic-power capacity with this magic-power training method. Instead of their magic-power capacity increasing, the Gremlin they had implanted swelled just a tiny bit. Interesting phenomenon.

But by that logic, I could not do magic-power training. I would need to go through a complicated process if I wanted to.

I had a <ruby>lizard<rt>fire salamander</rt></ruby> Gremlin implanted in the back of my left hand. I had carefully chosen the best spot so it would not interfere with my fingers. If it swelled and changed size, it would affect my dexterity.

In theory, I could extract the Gremlin I had implanted, increase my magic power through magic-power training, then implant the Gremlin again to increase my magic power. In fact, a beast handler who was one of the Department of Mutation Studies students, a pouch sparrow keeper, had apparently increased their magic power that way.

But while I had the Gremlin pulled out, my bond with the fire salamanders would be cut off.

I had lost around 45 K of magic power through the fire-salamander Gremlin implantation. Just restoring the magic power I had lost through magic-power training at 0.2 K a day would take more than half a year.

There was no way I could go half a year without spending time with the fire salamanders.

Who would feed them in the meantime? Who would polish their scales? I could not squish their bellies or go for walks with them.

I could not take it. No way.

At least I wanted to learn how to meditate.

Using the materials Professor Ohinata sent me as a reference, I should make seven wands, buy up magnets with my extra money, and try making a meditation room in my house. The lines of magnetic force with magical meaning created by complex magnetic fields looked like they could teach me something useful for making magic wands too.

But I probably would not increase my magic power through magic-power training.

Humans had little magic power. There were many spells they could pronounce but could not use because they did not have enough magic power.

The magic-power training method was an unbelievably important discovery that broke through that limit on how much magic power people had.

Now that this research had been published, magnetic-field reverse-playback mechanisms would be mass-produced, and the number of people who learned meditation would increase.

At that point, safety had only been confirmed and data was only being gathered at the laboratory level.

If more than two million Tokyo residents began magic-power training, the amount of data would increase dramatically, and they would surely learn new things.

Even if they learned nothing, it was almost certain that anyone could increase their magic power by at least 1 K.

In other words, people who had not even been able to use the shooting-magic core spell, “<ruby>![gaiji-0002.png](images/gaiji-0002.png)-<rt>Fire</rt></ruby>,” until now would become able to use magic.

As long as they trained, all of humanity could now use magic.

The dawn of a new age.

If they kept up magic-power training, maybe there would even be former ordinary people who got insane magic power of around 10,000 K! I got fired up.

In practice, I could not do magic-power training, so I could not ride this big wave.

But it looked like I could do something for the people riding that big wave.

I should think about whether I could apply the magic-power training theory and make some new kind of magic wand...
