The bat-faced mage staying close to Ohinata and keeping watch was sensitive to magic power, so the Blue Witch could not approach. Even if she tried to snipe him with magic from a distance, he would sense her magic power moving and dodge. His senses were that sharp.

But Handa Sakunosuke's dynamite suicide bombing had nothing to do with magic power. It was purely physical.

That was why the explosion that blew through the room's wall caught the bat-faced mage completely by surprise and badly distracted him.

The Blue Witch, who had shed her disguise and hidden behind the big clock in the university's central tower, moved quickly at this once-in-a-lifetime chance.

She aimed Cyanos at the bat-faced mage. But though he had finally let his guard down, he was too close to Ohinata. What if she caught Ohinata in the blast?

That hesitation was swept away by someone else's intervention.

Something small, almost impossible to see or sense, flew in through the hole the explosion had opened in the wall, slammed into the bat-faced mage, and sent him flying.

The Blue Witch clenched her fist without thinking.

She had not been the only one lying in wait and watching for a chance.

The bat-faced mage crashed into a pile of projectors and printers. The Blue Witch released the cold killing intent she had held back and cast her lethal spell.

“<ruby>Do ××× Vaa-raa zu Aaraa kunnei shiseruu<rt>That spear was made of ice, snow, and sorrow</rt></ruby>”

Magic power compressed the cold it had created into a pure-white trident. The lethal spear formed from nothing in an instant and shot straight down so fast that it left an afterimage and sound itself behind.

The next instant, it pierced through four floors and skewered the bat-faced mage straight from head to crotch. Pinned to the ground, the mage died instantly.

The university suddenly grew noisy.

Her position had been exposed by that first shot. Until she finished off the user of the magic-rampage spell, she would temporarily be unable to use Cyanos.

More than anything, the Blue Witch wanted to go save Ohinata first, but she noticed a small figure dragging Ohinata down the hallway at quite a speed, away from the fiercest fighting.

Gratitude welled up from the bottom of her heart. The little figure had done such a wonderful job that she could kiss whoever it was. Once she had wiped out the enemy, she definitely wanted to thank them.

But cleaning up came first.

She quickly checked the overall situation. The Arataki Group's gang boss had taken the dynamite blast at close range, but he was not dead yet, and a shadow was trying to rise from the smoke at the blast's center.

“<ruby>Do Vaa-ra<rt>Freezing Javelin</rt></ruby>”

The Blue Witch tucked Cyanos at her waist and rapidly fired suppression magic barehanded, slamming the gang boss to the ground. Then she leaped from behind the big clock and jumped high into the air. The Tokyo Witches' Council's strongest trump card, finally unleashed, flew through the air.

Her destination was the campus store on the first floor of the south building. That was where the user of the magic-rampage spell and her two guard witches were.

“!? Nee-san, get back! <ruby>Nautetsuddo<rt>We are a fortress</rt></ruby>!”

She had made a flashy entrance, so of course they noticed her.

When the red-haired guard witch cast a spell, a shining, sturdy, translucent barrier appeared in midair and blocked the Blue Witch's path.

From the incantation and its appearance, the Blue Witch guessed how the unknown magic worked. She turned in midair, kicked off the barrier, and shot down to the ground.

“<ruby>××××× De-nitsu<rt>Boil, my blood</rt></ruby>”

Then she used the crouch of her landing like a spring. From that low, beastlike stance, she burst forward faster than a bullet and kicked straight through the barrier.

She did not stop at breaking the barrier. Without losing any momentum, she accelerated again, knocked the red-haired witch down, and straddled her.

The battle changed with every blink, leaving the enemy completely on the back foot.

The witch beneath her desperately tried to cast something. The Blue Witch shoved the muzzle of her sidearm into the witch's mouth and pulled the trigger while firing barehanded magic at the back of the panicked magic-rampage witch trying to flee.

“<ruby>Do ××× Vaa-raa zu Aaraa kunnei shiseruu<rt>That spear was made of ice, snow, and sorrow</rt></ruby>”

After another blink, the magic-rampage witch had been pierced through the heart from behind and turned into a grotesque object of death thrust up from the ground.

There was a reason she had guards assigned to her: she had been fragile for a witch. The magic power the Blue Witch had worked up to fire a second shot would go to the guard instead, who was coughing up blood and barely conscious.

“<ruby>Do ××× Vaa-raa zu Aaraa kunnei shiseruu<rt>That spear was made of ice, snow, and sorrow</rt></ruby>”

The guard, who had been shot five times at point-blank range inside the mouth and could not speak, had her head crushed by a white trident and died.

In barely more than ten seconds, three witches and mages had died.

Two people remained in the university.

The Blue Witch gripped Cyanos again, cold killing intent and magic power surging within her.

An enormous amount of magic power overflowed around the Blue Witch, and waves of white frost spread through the university.

The dark-red pools of blood soaking into the ground and the abandoned bodies were all wrapped in white. Winter swallowed spring and took over.

The Ice Queen reigned.

The Arataki Group underlings who had gathered around the campus panicked before the terrifying witch and tried to flee ahead of one another.

But several of the underlings trying to escape in a clump were sent flying into the air together.

The shaved-headed mage called the Young Boss shouldered through the wall of underlings frozen in fear and strode out.

Maybe he thought it was a magic wand. He pointed the cheap weapon—nothing but a magic stone stuck to a wooden rod—at the Blue Witch and shouted with a demonic look on his face.

“You bitch! Don't think you'll get out of here alive! <ruby>Puurapugiya ga booroo<rt>Come from the great hole in the earth</rt></ruby>—”

“<ruby>Do Vaa-ra<rt>Freezing Javelin</rt></ruby>”

The Blue Witch accurately aimed and rapidly fired to interrupt the Young Boss's incantation. He had stupidly made a threat and shown himself.

Ice-spear magic was extremely useful, with a short incantation, power, efficiency, range, and speed all balanced at a high level.

The Blue Witch's superb magic-power control raised every one of those qualities even higher, including its accuracy.

The Young Boss tried to dodge the ice spear, but could not get clear. It struck his cheek and cut off his incantation.

The Young Boss looked stunned. This time, he cast a short spell.

“<ruby>××× Puuraputsuta<rt>How dare you step on me</rt></ruby>!”

“<ruby>Do Vaa-ra<rt>Freezing Javelin</rt></ruby>”

The Blue Witch overlaid the Young Boss's spell with a short spell of her own.

As a bed of swords thrust up from the ground to pierce anyone above it, the Blue Witch's ice spear appeared at her feet.

The Blue Witch stepped on the ice spear as it fired and used it as a catapult, reaching top speed in an instant and appearing before the Young Boss. The bed of swords pierced empty air.

Short incantations tended to have low power and could not deal fatal wounds to a Transcendent.

She had no intention of humoring a long-range exchange of suppression fire.

The Blue Witch always crushed lives by the shortest, fastest route.

The Blue Witch grabbed the Young Boss's face in one hand, slammed him into the ground, and mercilessly cast magic.

“<ruby>Do ××× Vaa-raa zu Aaraa kunnei shiseruu<rt>That spear was made of ice, snow, and sorrow</rt></ruby>”

The magic, raised in power by Cyanos, made another skewered corpse.

The underlings who witnessed the one-sided slaughter completely lost their will to fight.

Some wet themselves. Others prostrated themselves and began begging for their lives as they cried in fear.

The small fry could be dealt with later.

The Blue Witch walked toward the last mage remaining.

After Handa Sakunosuke's suicide bombing, the Blue Witch had fired one suppression shot at the gang boss, who had stubbornly survived. But while ice-spear magic was effective for suppression, it could not deal a fatal blow to a mage.

It would not have been strange if he had stood up long ago and attacked her with magic or his fists.

Facing him again, she saw how badly the gang boss was wounded.

The front of his body was black with burns and swollen red, and his jaw was mangled.

No wonder no magical support had come. Accurate pronunciation was impossible with that jaw.

The gang boss showed no intention of running from the Blue Witch as she approached. Dragging his wrecked body, he instead came toward her step by step on legs that twitched irregularly.

The Young Boss had been the same, but Arataki Group members had plenty of fight in them—at least the witches and mages did. The gang boss strode proudly despite being the commander of a defeated force, pointed at the Blue Witch, then himself, and raised his fists.

Since he could not speak, he had no choice but to convey his intent with his hands.

The Blue Witch understood that intent exactly.

He seemed to be saying, Fight me one-on-one. Fists only.

But she had no reason to accept that demand. The Blue Witch walked into the guaranteed-hit range of her trident magic, pointed Cyanos at the half-corpse, and cast a spell to finish him off.

“<ruby>Do ××× Vaa-raa zu Aaraa kunnei shiseruu<rt>That spear was made of ice, snow, and sorrow</rt></ruby>”

The ice spear shot out too fast for the eye to follow and struck the gang boss.

But the spear did not pierce the enemy. It only lodged shallowly.

The gang boss staggered badly, but pulled the spear from his chest and threw it aside. Then he came at the Blue Witch again, step by step, with frightening intensity.

“…………!?”

The Blue Witch became alert and frowned.

Something about the hit had felt wrong.

It was not just that he was tough.

It was as if magic did not work well on him. As if he had resistance to it.

As the Blue Witch watched the approaching gang boss closely, she felt something strange, then realized it.

Most likely, the gang boss was the same kind as her.

He knew different magic, but like her, he had no heart and had high resistance to cold. That was the kind of species he belonged to.

Even strengthened by Cyanos, freezing magic worked far too poorly on him.

His resistance could not nullify the magic entirely, so she could probably overpower it and kill him.

But the Blue Witch chose another method.

She walked up to the half-corpse, an irritating, old-fashioned fossil who wanted a one-on-one fistfight to end his life, and grabbed both his hands.

The gang boss grinned ferociously with his burned face, tensed up, and tried to overpower the Blue Witch.

His physical strength was impressive, enough to match or surpass the Dragon Witch. He exerted tremendous force, and the ground beneath the Blue Witch's planted feet caved in.

But she had no intention of playing along fairly.

While grappling with the gang boss, the Blue Witch cast magic.

A spell that required contact, a long incantation, and a great deal of magic power in exchange for overwhelming strength.

“<ruby>××××× De-nitsu<rt>Boil, my blood</rt></ruby>, <ruby>×× Giyuna<rt>my rage</rt></ruby>. <ruby>Hatobato De-nitsu Dawa-tagu Mi![gaiji-0003.png](images/gaiji-0003.png)n<rt>Wringe every last drop of blood from you</rt></ruby>, <ruby>Rasotefu ×× Ri × Kuwawa<rt>Let it atone to your ancestors</rt></ruby>”

Blood magic was cast, and a horrifying red mist wrapped itself around the gang boss.

The gang boss groaned as he spat blood and stubbornly tried to resist with magic-power control.

But he had no chance against the Blue Witch.

His meager resistance was torn apart like paper. Magic power and blood burst from every hole in his body and every wound, and he dried out like a dead tree and died.

After taking a breath, the Blue Witch noticed the students and professors watching her from a distance, holding their breath. The crowd still did not seem to grasp that it was over.

That did not matter. Only Ohinata mattered.

Thinking that, the Blue Witch was about to go to the little figure who had laid Ohinata at the base of the water tower and was hopping up and down to get her attention. But then she remembered Associate Professor Nanase's words.

If she was going to move forward now instead of looking to the past, she could not just be friends with Ohinata and Ori.

This time, Magic University had worked together as one.

Not one person had been a traitor.

Everyone had done their best in their own way.

They could be trusted.

The Blue Witch stopped, turned around, and raised Cyanos to answer the watching crowd.

“Celebrate! Magic University is free! You made this happen!”

As a huge cheer erupted behind her, the Blue Witch eagerly headed for Ohinata.

The Arataki Group's main witches and mages who had attacked the heart of the Tokyo Witches' Council had been wiped out.

Only several witches attacking different parts of Tokyo remained.

But the Dragon Witch would probably happily deal with those remnants for their magic stones. If she had trouble, the Blue Witch could help her.

The top of the Arataki Group was dead. There was no one left to hold the organization together.

With the Blue Witch and the others retaking Magic University, the Arataki Group's attack on Tokyo had effectively come to an end.
