It was a hot, muggy night.

Okutama was nearing the height of summer, and even with the windows wide open, the air was hot and sticky. Lately, I'd been making an ice pack with freezing magic and using it as a pillow.

The heat woke me from a nightmare where I was slurping piping-hot ramen at a kotatsu[^1] while surrounded by lava.

Even for summer, this was hot. So hot, so hot. As I sat up, blearily rubbing my eyes, the source of the insane heat came into view.

“Mii.”

“Mi.”

“Mimimi!”

The three fire salamanders that had invaded my bedroom cried happily when they saw I was awake.

The three had carried six-inch nails, heating wire, and a monkey wrench into the room in their mouths and were busily welding metal reinforcements onto the walls and floor.

“Y-You guys...!”

“Miimi!”

As I clutched my head, Mokutan ran over, wagged his tail, and handed me a rusty razor blade.

The three didn't doubt for a second that their rampage was an act of kindness. They kept breathing fire, melting metal, and cluttering up the room with it.

No wonder it was hot. The whole room was ringed with hot metal that smelled a little scorched. It was like a sauna. No, an oven.

Fire salamanders had a habit of reinforcing their nests with metal. It seemed they'd seen that my nest (the bedroom) didn't have enough metal and thoughtfully decided to help me build it.

Thanks, you idiots!

“Talk about help I didn't ask for... Anyway, the window. Open the window... It won't open! The window frame's welded shut! The door... The door too! I'm trapped!”

“Miimimi.”

“Mimimiimi.”

“Mimiimi!”

The three crawled up to the ceiling along an iron pipe welded to the wall and happily got to work reinforcing the ceiling with metal.

The sealed-off bedroom kept getting hotter and hotter, and sweat poured off me like a waterfall. You've got to be kidding. At this rate, my pets were going to steam me alive!

“Hey, stop it, stop it. Tsubaki, Sekitan, Mokutan, assemble! Wait! Stay!”

I called them to assemble and stopped them from wrecking the bedroom any further.

The fire salamanders lined up on the low writing desk they'd haphazardly welded crushed empty cans onto. Since I'd raised my voice angrily, they looked surprised and confused.

“Mii~?”

“Mimi...”

“Mimimi...?”

Even if they couldn't understand my words, I understood how they felt. Their faces said, “We thought you'd praise us, so why are you mad?”

I knew they had done all this out of kindness.

I knew that, but...

“Could you stop? I'm going to die.”

“Mi?”

“No breathing fire in the bedroom.”

“Mimi?”

“My nest is fine the way it is. You don't need to reinforce it with metal.”

“Mimimi?”

“Yeah, you don't get it, do you? Let's make this simple. If you breathe fire here, this is what happens!”

When I pulled the fire extinguisher out from under the bed and aimed its nozzle at them, the three fire salamanders jumped in shock, then panicked and scrambled away.

They charged the door and rammed their heads into it. After realizing they'd welded it shut themselves, they floundered for a moment, then breathed fire on the welded spot to make a small hole.

After the fire salamanders crawled out through the hole and scattered away, I threw myself against the door and smashed through it, somehow escaping the bedroom.

Even the warm, muggy night air felt like a blast from an AC set to 18°C after I'd nearly been steamed alive.

What a night. That was one hell of an accident.

For now, I'll go get some water. I've sweated way too much.

That glass of water is sure to taste like the water of life itself, better than anything else...
