The Blue Witch had received Foresight's warning and killed the Arataki Group witch watching her in an instant. Now she was sprinting all-out for Bunkyo Ward.

As she ran, she used an eyeball familiar to give Ori a strict order, “Do not leave Okutama under any circumstances,” hammering it in far more thoroughly than she needed to. All the while, she was racing to save Ohinata Kei, who was in the very middle of Bunkyo Ward—a special-class danger zone that was said to be at risk of being razed flat.

If it came to the worst, she had to be ready to save only Ohinata and run.

Hitting several places at once to swamp Foresight's ability to respond had certainly worked. The enemy weren't idiots who would charge the Witches' Council without a plan.

There was at least one clever enemy who had drawn up the plan of attack. She couldn't tell what they would try next, or what they were already doing.

She wondered regretfully if she should have gotten information out of her watcher instead of killing her, but there was no undoing that now. Besides, she didn't know how to interrogate anyone, and if getting information took too long, she might not reach Ohinata in time.

The Blue Witch was taking the shortest route, leaping across the roofs of buildings and apartment blocks and scanning for trouble from up high, when she saw a huge, pitch-black hemispherical dome spread out in the distance.

It was the Night Witch's magic, and it was right over Bunkyo Ward. The Arataki Group hadn't waited for the Blue Witch to arrive; their attack had already begun.

All she could see was the pitch-black dome—no mist spreading around it, no spiderwebs. It seemed the Spider Witch had refused to join the defense. That meant the only ones defending Bunkyo Ward, the heart of Tokyo, were the Eyeball Witch, the Night Witch, and the Tobacco Witch.

Honestly, they weren't very dependable.

The Night Witch would have been stronger at night, but it was broad daylight. How long could three ordinary witches hold off the Arataki Group, whose attackers supposedly all carried magic stones?

Her fears came true. Before long, the pitch-black dome vanished as if melting into the air.

The Night Witch had no reason to dismiss her magic in the middle of this crisis. The sensible conclusion was that she had been forced to—in other words, that she had lost.

Even if none of the three were combat witches, that was too fast. Just as she'd thought, the Arataki Group had attacked Bunkyo Ward with some real chance of winning, and it had worked.

Still, there was one mercy: Bunkyo Ward didn't look like it had been razed. At least, not yet.

The Blue Witch had been running flat out, but once she judged that Bunkyo Ward had fallen, she changed plans. She dropped to street level, kicked in the door of a house, and forced her way inside.

A mother and child had barricaded themselves in under the emergency declaration. The mother stood shaking with a kitchen knife, and the boy, apparently her son, with a length of lumber.

“Eek! ...B-Blue Witch-sama?”

“Sorry for the rough entrance, but I'm in a hurry. I want to disguise myself as an ordinary person. Can you lend me some clothes?”

“Huh? Ah, y-yes!”

The mother hurriedly tossed her knife aside, pulled some clothes from a chest of drawers, and handed them over. The Blue Witch struggled with how tight they were across the chest and how loose around the waist, but for now she changed into a plain outfit that wouldn't look out of place. With a pair of nonprescription glasses on as well, she looked like some schoolgirl.

Her mask, tattered black coat, and Cyanos were famous as the Blue Witch's identifying marks. Put the other way, without those three things, the Blue Witch looked entirely human and could blend in with ordinary people.

“U-um...”

“What is it?”

“If I may say so, if you're going to pass as an ordinary person, I think you should dirty your face a little.”

“Why?”

“Well, Blue Witch-sama, you're beautiful. Like a crane in a garbage heap, I suppose. I mean, your face alone stands out a great deal.”

“O-Oh.”

Feeling oddly awkward, the Blue Witch gratefully dirtied her face with ash, dust, and a little soil, then let the woman muss up her long, glossy black hair, which had been neatly tied back. It wasn't pleasant, but it was necessary.

The Blue Witch thanked the mother for helping with her disguise and the son for cheering her on with “Beat up the bad guys!” then set off briskly for the center of Bunkyo Ward once more. To hide Cyanos, she had borrowed a bamboo sword bag that had apparently belonged to the woman's late husband. She kept her magic power firmly suppressed too, taking every care to pass as an ordinary person.

If Foresight was unconscious and the Eyeball Witch had been defeated, then the Witches' Council had effectively lost the head it needed to move as a group.

The Witches' Council was short on teamwork at the best of times, and without a leader, it was just a mob of crows. The one dragon mixed in among the crows would probably cause a fair amount of havoc, but she wasn't all that reliable.

If the violent but disciplined Arataki Group had seized the Witches' Council's nerve center, the Blue Witch couldn't just rampage with brute force anymore either. It wasn't as though she could freeze all of Bunkyo Ward solid the way she had with the giant kaiju.

So what the Blue Witch needed was guerrilla warfare: move in secret, dig up information on the Arataki Group, find the one spot that mattered, and blow it away in a single stroke.

She didn't know how much inside information on the Witches' Council the Arataki Group had.

Judging by how smoothly the attack had gone, the Dragon Witch had evidently let quite a lot slip. But since the Blue Witch's own watcher had been a cocky small-fry witch, the Arataki Group's intelligence had to have some big holes.

The Blue Witch prayed that Ohinata had managed to slip away from the Arataki Group and was lying low.

If they knew how much the Blue Witch doted on Ohinata, it wasn't hard to imagine them taking her hostage.

And then the Blue Witch wouldn't be able to defy the Arataki Group. Of course, that would depend on the finer details of the situation...

The Blue Witch stubbornly reminded Ori once more, “Do not leave Okutama under any circumstances,” then dismissed the eyeball familiar. It would be a problem if Ori suddenly called while she was sneaking around.

Given how little sense of danger Ori had, even if she told him not to call, it seemed possible he would call anyway at the worst possible moment for one reason or another. She wanted to eliminate any uncertainty.

Even under the emergency declaration, a few people were still out on the streets, so no one stopped the Blue Witch as she headed for central Bunkyo Ward, casually taking in her surroundings.

According to what she overheard from some anxious, confused security force members, the Eyeball Witch, the Night Witch, and the Tobacco Witch had apparently been defeated after all.

But the Arataki Group had accepted the three witches' surrender and apparently hadn't killed them.

Foresight hadn't been able to take command since his initial orders, but there was no talk of him being captured or killed either. It seemed he had managed to lie low.

Walking briskly and picking up every scrap of information she could, the Blue Witch reached the gates of Tokyo Magic University, the place Ohinata was most likely to be.

Inside the university, men and women who were clearly neither students nor faculty prowled around, dressed like typical thugs. Killing them all at once would be easy, but then the source of the magic-power fluctuations farther in—a mage or a witch—would come flying.

She was confident she could kill every enemy in the university, witches and mages included.

But while Ohinata's whereabouts and safety were still unknown, she couldn't carelessly start a fight.

She was hiding in the bushes behind the statue of Professor Ohinata, the father, wondering how to go about scouting, when someone suddenly spoke to her in a low voice.

“Witch-sama. You're the Blue Witch-sama, aren't you?”

“!?”

The Blue Witch nearly screamed at the presence and voice that had suddenly sprung up beside her.

On reflex, she moved to club the newcomer with the bamboo sword bag, then realized just in time that it was Nanase Nanami, associate professor in the Department of Magic Linguistics, and stopped.

With the weapon stopped at the tips of her eyelashes, Nanase broke into a cold sweat and raised both hands.

“S-Sorry...”

“You startled me. I couldn't sense you at all.”

“I have been hiding the whole time using suspended-animation magic. Then you came close, Witch-sama.”

“I see. That was excellent judgment.”

Impressed, the Blue Witch nodded. Just what you'd expect from an associate professor at elite Magic University.

Suspended-animation magic was one of the Spider Witch's spells, and it made the user hard for monsters, witches, and mages to detect by sight or smell. It broke the moment you spoke or moved, and if you entered someone's field of vision in an obvious way, they would naturally notice you, so it was no good for surprise attacks. For letting enemies pass by, though, it was ideal. In a situation like this one, it was arguably the best possible way to escape danger.

Even under suspended animation, you could still see and hear normally. Thankfully, Associate Professor Nanase knew nearly everything that had happened or been said inside the university, even the full course of the battle at Bunkyo Ward Office. She had apparently been scouting with an eyeball familiar too (though it had been destroyed partway through).

Crouching in the bushes behind the statue, the Blue Witch listened as Associate Professor Nanase whispered the whole story.

Associate Professor Nanase had been on campus when the emergency declaration went out, so she hadn't actually seen every part of the battle at Bunkyo Ward Office. But her eyeball familiar had arrived in time for her to see how it ended.

According to what she'd seen, an Arataki Group witch had apparently used a magic-rampage spell.

Three enemies had attacked the ward office, all of them carrying magic stones. One had already died before the familiar arrived, which showed how fiercely the three Witches' Council members had fought.

But the instant the Tobacco Witch cast a major spell to take out the other two in one sweep, the enemy witch recited an unknown incantation. At once, the Tobacco Witch's magic-power control was badly disrupted, and her magic nearly ran out of control.

The Tobacco Witch's wand was a low-performance mass-produced model. The Eyeball Witch hurriedly pressed her own custom wand, made by 0933, into the Tobacco Witch's hands, which let her barely regain control of her magic power and averted the disaster of the major spell slipping completely out of control.

But in all the confusion, the Night Witch had her wand taken, arm and all, and was knocked unconscious. Her night magic came undone.

The Tobacco Witch was worn out from nearly losing control of her magic.

With no chance of winning left, the Eyeball Witch accepted the call to surrender and gave up.

The Blue Witch had thought they'd lost awfully fast, even considering who they were up against, but now it made sense.

She'd heard that at worst, the Arataki Group would raze Bunkyo Ward. Their hidden trump card wasn't large-scale, wide-area magic after all—it was runaway magic.

Throwing off a witch's or mage's magic-power control to make a major spell run out of control was as good as turning the enemy into an extremely powerful bomb.

![gaiji-0004.png](images/gaiji-0004.png)Katsushika Ward had been razed flat when a mage let his magic run out of control and blew himself up, and the Hell Witch had also destroyed her own administrative district when her magic ran wild. That bomb's power had already been proven.

With magic like that in play, she couldn't use Cyanos carelessly.

Magic abnormally amplified by Cyanos was hard to control. If someone forced it to run wild, not just Bunkyo Ward but half of Tokyo would sink beneath a glacier.

Right around when the three witches were defeated at the ward office, two mages called the “gang boss” and the “Young Boss” had led their troops in an attack on Magic University.

Magic University had been split between the all-out resistance faction and the bloodless surrender faction, and the all-out resistance faction brushed off Professor Ohinata's attempts to stop them and went out to intercept the two mages.

The professors and students, mainly from the Department of Combat Studies, managed to pin down the enemy and hit them with a concentrated barrage of magic, despite one mishap: the Monster Trap was nullified by a magic stone used as a ground.

But nothing could make up for how fundamentally weak their magic was.

The Young Boss ended up covered in bruises and down on one knee, but without any serious injury. In the end, a spell cast by the gang boss forced the entire all-out resistance faction into submission, and they were defeated.

“What do you mean by ‘submission’? Don't tell me it was puppetry magic?”

The Blue Witch tensed. If he could use the same magic as Iruma, the Witches' Council's destruction was starting to look like a real possibility.

But Associate Professor Nanase shook her head.

“No. The effect looked more like ‘they can no longer disobey orders.’ When they tried to defy an order, their mouths and bodies would stop moving, and so on. They clearly still had the will to disobey, so their thoughts were not being twisted. There was also a grace period of around 30 seconds between casting the magic and it taking effect. And it did not contain the core word of puppetry magic.”

“If the core word is different, it's not puppetry magic. Good...”

“They used a different incantation on the three witches brought to the university than the one they used on the professors and students, and they separately demanded consent to a contract. It seems the magic they have to use on witches requires consent. All three refused, and they were talking about preparing to torture them into giving it.”

“That magic has a lot of holes. Seems safe to think of this gang boss as a lesser Iruma.”

The Blue Witch relaxed at the thought of whom she was comparing him to, and Associate Professor Nanase glared at her reproachfully.

“What are you relieved about? Foresight-sama has collapsed, and the Eyeball Witch-sama has been captured and is about to be tortured. Blue Witch-sama, you are the only one who can turn this situation around.”

“R-Right. Sorry. But as far as I'm concerned, as long as Kei-chan is saved...”

The Blue Witch had barely started to say it when she reflexively dodged a fist flying at her face.

She dodged the second one too and blinked in bewilderment. Associate Professor Nanase was quietly seething, her voice still hushed, her fist clenched tight.

“You need to cut this nonsense out, or I really will hit you, you know.

“What do you mean, ‘as long as Kei-chan is saved’? Do you think Professor Ohinata would be happy to be the only one saved and see Tokyo in this state?

“You are Tokyo's savior, the one Professor Ohinata trusts completely.

“You are the strongest witch, the one who will absolutely beat the Arataki Group and bring back peace, aren't you?

“And yet what have you been doing?

“You. Because you were off protecting some ruins, Professor Ohinata tried to kill herself with poison!”

“...What did you say?”

“See! You did not even know! If I may say so, you are only playing at being a guardian. You have not protected anything at all.”

As the blood drained from the Blue Witch's face, Associate Professor Nanase clicked her tongue and laid out the situation for her in painstaking detail.

“Professor Ohinata is smart. She knew that if she fell into enemy hands, she would be used as a hostage against you. So she kept trying to negotiate with the Arataki Group right up to the last moment, and the instant negotiations broke down, she drank poison.

“The gang boss noticed and made her throw up the poison, so she survived, but she is unconscious.

“You made Professor Ohinata, a girl who is still only fifteen, go that far!

“Because you dote on her, she became valuable as a hostage.

“And yet, instead of staying by her side to protect her, you clung to an empty city. Because you were not beside her, the Professor's life is in danger in enemy hands right now.

“If you had been beside the Professor. If you had been in Bunkyo Ward from the beginning. The university would not have fallen, and the Eyeball Witch-sama, Night Witch-sama, and Tobacco Witch-sama would not have had to surrender to the enemy. Foresight-sama surely would not have needed to push himself until he collapsed, either.

“I understand. Your trauma and grief must be beyond anything I can imagine. You were still a high school senior back then.

“But how long are you going to wallow in sentimentality and shut yourself away in Ome?

“Because you keep clinging to something that is over and done with! That is why you keep letting the present slip away!”

Nanase had lost her temper, and this time the Blue Witch did not dodge her third punch. The fist slammed into her face, but she didn't budge an inch.

Taken to task so harshly by an outsider in the middle of an obvious crisis, the Blue Witch was racked by heartache and fierce self-blame.

The Hell Witch had lost what she was meant to protect, just as the Blue Witch had, yet she had still kept her eyes forward and moved on.

The Blue Witch should have followed her example and done the same. Much sooner.

Of course, the ones most to blame were the Arataki Group, who had invaded and were spreading violence everywhere.

But next to blame was the Blue Witch herself, and she berated herself for it.

The Blue Witch had let the past hold her back until she nearly let something precious slip away once again.

Seeing the change in the Blue Witch's expression, Associate Professor Nanase lowered the fist she'd raised.

Though traces of anger still showed on her face, she passed on even more valuable information.

Nanase had watched her allies fall and be taken captive, holding back the urge to rush out and devoting herself to gathering information. Thanks to her, the Blue Witch now had everything she needed to know.

“The Arataki Group has gathered the influential people in the courtyard, and they are making them submit with magic and pulling information out of them. Professor Ohinata has been hidden away, so I do not know where she is. But rather than search blindly, it would be better to check the courtyard first.”

“All right. Got it.”

“I do not even have 1 K of magic power left. I am no use to you, so I leave the rest to you, Blue Witch-sama. Do you understand? Save Professor Ohinata, even if it kills you. That is the bare minimum responsibility you must fulfill. And if you have any human conscience left, save Tokyo.”

Nanase all but kicked the Blue Witch out of the bushes where they had been hiding.

Under these extreme circumstances, Nanase had obviously lost her usual calm and gone hysterical. But what she had said was right.

The Blue Witch didn't think protecting her memories of the past had been wrong. But losing Ohinata because of it—losing the future—was something she could never allow.

The Blue Witch set off at once to check the courtyard as she'd been told. But thugs who seemed to be Arataki Group men were prowling the route from the gate to the courtyard, so she made a big leap onto a roof from behind a building, then crossed the rooftops to a spot where she could see into the courtyard.

If she was spotted and they threatened her with Ohinata's safety, she was confident she would cave without a fight. For everyone's sake, she could not be found.

Luckily, no one spotted her, and the Blue Witch got into a good position overlooking the courtyard.

In the courtyard, professors and local influential figures (witches excluded) knelt in a single row with their hands tied behind their backs, like prisoners lined up before a firing squad.

A hard-faced man of about fifty went down the line, laying a hand on each one's shoulder and casting magic on them. Once he had cast it on all of them, he began the interrogation. When she strained her ears, she could just make out the conversation.

“I'll ask you again. Tell me the truth. Where is the Wand Maker?”

“I don't know. I heard they are in Ome City.”

“Their name? Not 0933. Their real name.”

“I don't know.”

“Their sex? Their age?”

“Male or female. Most likely between 5 and 150 years old.”

The mage, presumably the gang boss, groaned at the hopelessly vague answer.

“Damn it, you're done. Next, you. Where is the Wand Maker?”

“I don't know. I believe the story that the Wand Maker is currently being sent to Hokkaido in secret aboard a trade ship to provide technical assistance.”

“Name? Sex? Age?”

“I don't know any of it.”

Yet another empty answer came back, and the mage scratched his head irritably.

“You bastard...! Next, you! Where is the Wand Maker!? Who are they!?”

“I don't know. I support the theory that the Wand Maker and Foresight-sama are the same person.”

“Are you fucking with me...? Every one of you is saying something different. What the hell is going on?”

The Blue Witch couldn't help smiling as she listened in. She had never been more grateful for Ori's poor social skills than she was right then.

Because he had kept refusing to show his face in public, the Arataki Group had no idea what to make of anything.

If Ori had been sociable, respectable, and actively involved in society, they would have dug up his whereabouts long ago, and he would have been in danger.

The situation was still serious.

But watching the enemy flounder because of Ori's stubborn social anxiety took some of the tension out of her shoulders.

Ori was so spaced-out, and yet, strangely, just hearing his name come up made her feel like things might work out somehow.

The Blue Witch clutched the amulet Ori had given her through her clothes and swore to herself that she would find Ohinata and rescue her, no matter what.
