After receiving the foresight warning, the Blue Witch instantly killed the Arataki Group witch who had been watching her, then raced toward Bunkyo Ward.

As she ran, she used an eyeball familiar to repeatedly order Ori, “Do not leave Okutama under any circumstances,” taking more care than necessary to drive the point home. Then she raced to save Ohinata Kei, who was in the middle of Bunkyo Ward, a special-class danger zone that might be completely razed.

At worst, she had to keep open the option of saving only Ohinata and escaping.

Simultaneous attacks that overwhelmed foresight's ability to respond had certainly worked. The enemy was not stupid enough to charge the Witches' Council without a plan.

At least one smart enemy had drawn up the operation. She did not know what they might do or what they were already doing.

She regretted not pulling information from the watcher instead of killing her, but what was done could not be taken back. To begin with, she did not know how to interrogate someone, and if extracting information took too long, she might not make it in time to rescue Ohinata.

The Blue Witch took the shortest route, leaping across the roofs of buildings and apartment blocks while searching for anything strange from up high. Then she saw a huge, pitch-black hemispherical dome appear in the distance.

It was the Night Witch's magic. The location was right in Bunkyo Ward. Without waiting for the Blue Witch to arrive, the Arataki Group's attack had begun.

All she could see was the pitch-black dome. There was no mist spreading around it and no spiderwebs in sight. It seemed the Spider Witch had refused to join the defense. That meant only the Eyeball Witch, Night Witch, and Tobacco Witch were defending Bunkyo Ward, the heart of Tokyo.

Honestly, they weren't very dependable.

The Night Witch would be stronger at night, but it was broad daylight now. How long could three ordinary witches hold off the Arataki Group's attack when every one of them had a magic stone?

Her fears came true. Not long after, the pitch-black dome vanished as if melting into the air.

There was no reason for the Night Witch to dismiss her magic in the middle of this crisis. It was reasonable to think it had been forced down—in other words, she had lost.

Even if none of the three were combat-oriented witches, that was too fast. The Arataki Group really had attacked Bunkyo Ward with some kind of winning plan, and it had succeeded.

But luckily, Bunkyo Ward did not look like it had been razed. At least, not yet.

Seeing that Bunkyo Ward had fallen, the Blue Witch changed plans. She dropped to street level, kicked down the door of a house, and barged inside.

The mother and son who had barricaded themselves indoors under the emergency declaration were shaking. The mother held a kitchen knife, and the boy who looked like her son held a length of lumber.

“Eek! ...B-Blue Witch-sama?”

“Sorry for being rough, but I'm in a hurry. I want to disguise myself as an ordinary person. Can you lend me some clothes?”

“Huh? Ah, y-yes!”

The mother hurriedly threw down her knife, pulled some clothes from a chest of drawers, and handed them to the Blue Witch. They were tight around the chest and loose around the waist, but for now she changed into an ordinary outfit that did not look out of place. With a pair of nonprescription glasses, she looked like an ordinary schoolgirl.

Her mask, tattered black coat, and Cyanos were well-known markers of the Blue Witch. In other words, without those three things, the Blue Witch—whose appearance was entirely human—could blend in with ordinary people.

“U-um...”

“What is it?”

“If I may say so, I think you should dirty your face a little if you want to pass as an ordinary person.”

“Why?”

“Well, Blue Witch-sama, you are beautiful. Like a crane in a garbage heap, I suppose. I mean, your face alone stands out a great deal.”

“O-Oh.”

Feeling awkward, the Blue Witch gratefully dirtied her face with ash, dust, and a little soil, then had her neatly tied, long and glossy black hair made suitably messy. It did not feel good, but it was necessary.

After thanking the mother who had helped with her disguise, and the boy who cheered, “Beat up the bad guys!” the Blue Witch headed briskly toward the center of Bunkyo Ward again. To hide Cyanos, she borrowed a bamboo sword bag that had belonged to the woman's late husband. She kept her magic power firmly suppressed and took every precaution to pass as an ordinary person.

If Foresight was unconscious and the Eyeball Witch had been defeated, then the Witches' Council had effectively lost its head.

The Witches' Council already lacked coordination, and without a leader it was just a rabble. A dragon mixed in among crows would probably wreak some havoc, but the Dragon Witch was not very trustworthy.

If the violent but organized Arataki Group had seized control of the Witches' Council's center, the Blue Witch could not just rampage with brute force anymore. It was not as though she could freeze all of Bunkyo Ward like she had during the giant kaiju incident.

So what the Blue Witch needed to do was guerrilla work. She would move in secret, gather information on the Arataki Group, find the key target, and blow it away in one go.

She did not know how much internal information about the Witches' Council the Arataki Group had.

Judging by how smoothly the attack had gone, the Dragon Witch had probably let a lot of information slip. But the Blue Witch's watcher had been a pathetic small-fry witch, so there were probably plenty of gaps in the Arataki Group's intelligence.

The Blue Witch prayed that Ohinata had managed to escape the Arataki Group and was hiding safely.

If they knew how much the Blue Witch doted on Ohinata, it was easy to imagine them taking her hostage.

Then the Blue Witch would not be able to defy the Arataki Group. Of course, it depended on the details of the situation...

After once again reminding Ori, “Do not leave Okutama under any circumstances,” the Blue Witch dismissed the eyeball familiar. She did not want an incoming call from Ori while she was sneaking around.

Given Ori's weak sense of danger, even if she told him not to call, he might do it at the worst possible time for some reason or another. She wanted to eliminate any uncertainty.

Even under the emergency declaration, there was still some foot traffic. The Blue Witch headed for central Bunkyo Ward while casually watching her surroundings, and no one stopped her.

From overhearing the anxious, confused security force members talking, it seemed the Eyeball Witch, Night Witch, and Tobacco Witch really had been defeated.

But the Arataki Group had accepted the three witches' surrender and apparently had not killed them.

And although Foresight had been unable to take command after his initial orders, there was no talk of him being captured or killed. It seemed he had hidden himself well.

The Blue Witch picked up every scrap of information she could while walking quickly, until she reached the gates of Tokyo Magic University, where Ohinata was most likely to be.

There were men and women inside the university who were clearly neither students nor faculty, dressed like obvious thugs. Killing them all at once would be easy, but if she did that, the source of the magic-power fluctuations farther in—a mage or witch—would come flying over.

She was confident she could kill every enemy in the university, including the witches and mages.

But with Ohinata's whereabouts and condition still unknown, she could not carelessly start fighting.

She hid in the bushes behind the statue of Professor Ohinata, the father, and wondered how to start probing when someone suddenly spoke to her in a low voice.

“Witch-sama. You're the Blue Witch-sama, aren't you?”

“!?”

The Blue Witch almost screamed at the presence and voice that had suddenly sprung up beside her.

She reflexively moved to club the newcomer with the bamboo sword bag, then realized just in time that it was Nanase Nanami, associate professor in the Department of Magic Linguistics.

Nanase broke into a cold sweat as the weapon stopped at the tips of her eyelashes and raised both hands.

“S-Sorry...”

“You startled me. I could not sense you at all.”

“I had been hiding the whole time using suspended-animation magic. Then you came close, Witch-sama.”

“I see. That was an excellent decision.”

The Blue Witch was impressed and nodded. Just what one would expect from an associate professor at an elite institution like Magic University.

Suspended-animation magic was one of the Spider Witch's magics, making one difficult for monsters, witches, and mages to detect by sight or smell. The magic broke the moment one spoke or moved, and someone would naturally be noticed if they entered another's field of vision in an obvious way, so it could not be used for surprise attacks. But it was ideal for letting enemies pass by. In a situation like this, it was surely the best way to escape danger.

Even while suspended animation was in effect, one could see and hear normally. Thankfully, Associate Professor Nanase knew almost everything that had happened or been said inside the university. She even knew the full course of the battle at Bunkyo Ward Office. She had been using an eyeball familiar to look into things as well, though it had been destroyed partway through.

While crouching in the bushes behind the statue, the Blue Witch listened to Associate Professor Nanase whisper the full story.

Associate Professor Nanase had been on university grounds when the emergency declaration was issued, so she had not personally seen every part of the battle at Bunkyo Ward Office. But an eyeball familiar had made it in time to see how the battle ended.

According to her, an Arataki Group witch had apparently used a magic-rampage spell.

Three enemies had attacked the ward office. All of them had magic stones. One of them had died before the familiar arrived, which showed how fiercely the three Witches' Council members had fought.

But the moment the Tobacco Witch cast a major spell to catch the other two at once, the enemy witch recited an unknown incantation. At once, the Tobacco Witch's magic-power control broke down badly, and her magic nearly ran out of control.

The Tobacco Witch's wand was a low-performance mass-produced item. In a panic, the Eyeball Witch put her own custom wand made by 0933 into her hands. The Tobacco Witch barely regained control of her magic power, preventing the disaster of the major spell completely escaping her control.

But amid the confusion, the Night Witch lost her wand along with the arm holding it and was knocked unconscious. Her magic vanished.

The Tobacco Witch was exhausted from nearly letting her magic run out of control.

The Eyeball Witch, having lost any chance of winning, obeyed the surrender demand.

The Blue Witch had thought they had lost too quickly, even against that enemy, but now it made sense.

She had heard the Arataki Group might raze Bunkyo Ward at worst. Their trump card was not large-scale, wide-area magic, but a magic-rampage spell.

By disrupting a witch's or mage's magic-power control and making a major spell run out of control, they could practically turn an enemy into an extremely powerful bomb.

![gaiji-0004.png](images/gaiji-0004.png)Katsushika Ward had been razed when a mage's magic ran out of control and he blew himself up, and the Hell Witch had also destroyed her administrative district when her magic ran wild. The power of that bomb had already been proven.

If magic like that existed, she could not carelessly use Cyanos.

Magic abnormally amplified by Cyanos was difficult to control. If it was forced out of control, not just Bunkyo Ward, but half of Tokyo would sink beneath a glacier.

Around the time the three witches were defeated at the ward office, two mages called the “gang boss” and “Young Boss” had attacked Magic University at the head of their troops.

Magic University was divided between the all-out resistance faction and the bloodless surrender faction. The all-out resistance faction pushed past Professor Ohinata's attempts to stop them and intercepted the two mages.

The professors and students, mainly from the Department of Combat Studies, successfully bound the enemy and hit them with a concentrated barrage of magic, despite the setback of having the Monster Trap nullified by magic stones used as ground.

But there was no overcoming their fundamental lack of power.

The Young Boss was covered in bruises and dropped to one knee, but he was not seriously injured. In the end, the gang boss's magic made every member of the all-out resistance faction submit, leaving them defeated.

“What do you mean, submit? Don't tell me it was puppetry magic?”

The Blue Witch tensed. If he used the same magic as Iruma, the collapse of the Witches' Council was starting to look real.

But Associate Professor Nanase shook her head.

“No. It looked more like they became unable to disobey orders. When they tried to defy an order, their mouths and bodies stopped moving. They clearly still had the will to disobey, so their thoughts had not been twisted. There was also a grace period of around 30 seconds between casting the magic and it taking effect. It did not contain the core word of puppetry magic either.”

“If the core word is different, it is not puppetry magic. Good...”

“They cast a different incantation on the three witches brought to the university from the one they used on the professors and students. They also asked them to consent to a contract. It seems the magic they use on witches requires consent. All three refused, and they were talking about preparing torture to get it.”

“That magic has plenty of weak points. It seems safe to think of this gang boss as a lesser Iruma.”

Associate Professor Nanase saw the Blue Witch relax at the comparison and glared at her.

“What are you relieved about? Foresight-sama has collapsed, and the Eyeball Witch-sama has been restrained and is about to be tortured. Blue Witch-sama, you are the only one who can turn this situation around.”

“A-Ah. Sorry. But all I care about is Kei-chan...”

The Blue Witch started to speak, then reflexively dodged the fist flying at her face.

She dodged the second fist as well and stared in shock. Associate Professor Nanase was quietly furious, her voice still low and her fist clenched tight.

“You need to cut this nonsense out, or I really will hit you.

“What do you mean, ‘as long as Kei-chan survives’? Do you think Professor Ohinata would be happy to be the only one saved and see Tokyo in this state?

“You are Tokyo's savior, the one Professor Ohinata trusts completely.

“You are the strongest witch, the one who will absolutely beat the Arataki Group and bring back peace, aren't you?

“And what have you been doing?

“You did this. Because you were off protecting some ruins, Professor Ohinata tried to kill herself with poison!”

“...What did you say?”

“See! You did not even know! If I may say so, you are only playing at being a guardian. You have not protected anything at all.”

As the blood drained from the Blue Witch's face, Associate Professor Nanase clicked her tongue and laid out the situation for her.

“Professor Ohinata is smart. She knew that if she fell into enemy hands, she would be used as a hostage against you. So she tried to negotiate with the Arataki Group until the last moment, and the instant negotiations broke down, she drank poison.

“The gang boss noticed and made her throw up the poison, so she survived, but she is unconscious.

“You made Professor Ohinata, a girl who is only fifteen, go that far!

“Because you dote on her, she became valuable as a hostage.

“And yet, instead of staying by her side to protect her, you clung to an empty city. Because you were not beside her, she is now in enemy hands, with her life in danger.

“If you had been beside the Professor. If you had been in Bunkyo Ward from the beginning. The university would not have fallen, and the Eyeball Witch-sama, Night Witch-sama, and Tobacco Witch-sama would not have had to surrender to the enemy. Foresight-sama surely would not have needed to push himself until he collapsed, either.

“I know. Your trauma and grief must be beyond what I can imagine. You were still a high school senior then.

“But how long are you going to wallow in sentimentality and shut yourself away in Ome?

“Because you keep clinging to something that is over! You keep letting the present slip away!”

The Blue Witch did not dodge Nanase's third punch, thrown in a rage. The fist slammed into her face, but she did not move an inch.

Harshly confronted by an outsider in the middle of a clear crisis, the Blue Witch suffered heartache and intense self-blame.

The Hell Witch had lost what she was meant to protect, just as the Blue Witch had, yet she had still looked forward and kept going.

The Blue Witch should have done the same. Much sooner.

Of course, the Arataki Group, who had invaded and were spreading violence, were most at fault.

But the Blue Witch blamed herself, deciding she was next.

The Blue Witch had let the past hold her back until she nearly lost something precious again.

Seeing the Blue Witch's expression change, Associate Professor Nanase lowered her raised fist.

Though anger still remained in her expression, she gave the Blue Witch more valuable information.

Nanase had watched allies fall and be captured, endured the urge to rush out, and focused on gathering information. Thanks to her, the Blue Witch now knew enough.

“The Arataki Group is gathering influential people in the courtyard, making them submit with magic, and extracting information. They have hidden Professor Ohinata somewhere, so I do not know where she is. But rather than search blindly, it would be better to look into the courtyard first.”

“All right. Got it.”

“I do not even have 1 K of magic power left. I am no use to you, so I leave the rest to you, Blue Witch-sama. Do you understand? Even if you die, save Professor Ohinata. That is the bare minimum responsibility you must fulfill. And if you have any human conscience left, save Tokyo.”

Nanase all but kicked the Blue Witch out of the bushes where she had been hiding.

Under these extreme circumstances, Nanase had clearly lost her usual calm and grown hysterical. But what she had said was right.

The Blue Witch did not think preserving memories of the past had been wrong. But she could not let that make her lose Ohinata, lose the future.

The Blue Witch immediately started for the courtyard, as she had been told. But thugs who seemed to be Arataki Group men wandered along the route from the gate, so she leaped onto a roof from the cover of a building, then moved across the rooftops to a position where she could see the courtyard.

If she was discovered and they used Ohinata's safety against her, she was confident she would give in easily. For everyone's sake, she could not be found.

Luckily, no one spotted her, and the Blue Witch reached a good position to look into the courtyard.

In the courtyard, professors and influential people from nearby—excluding witches—knelt in a row with their hands tied behind their backs, like a lineup before an execution by firing squad.

A tough-looking man around fifty went down the line, putting a hand on each person's shoulder and casting magic on them. Once he had finished, he began the interrogation. When she listened closely, she could faintly hear the conversation.

“I'll ask you again. Tell me the truth. Where is the Wand Maker?”

“I don't know. I heard they are in Ome City.”

“Their name? Not 0933. Their real name.”

“I don't know.”

“Their sex? Their age?”

“Male or female. Most likely between 5 and 150 years old.”

Hearing that utterly vague answer, the mage who seemed to be the gang boss grunted.

“Damn it, you're done. Next, you. Where is the Wand Maker?”

“I don't know. I believe the story that the Wand Maker is currently being sent to Hokkaido in secret aboard a trade ship to provide technical assistance.”

“Name? Sex? Age?”

“I don't know any of it.”

Yet another empty answer came back, and the mage scratched his head irritably.

“You bastard...! Next, you! Where is the Wand Maker!? Who are they!?”

“I don't know. I support the theory that the Wand Maker and Foresight-sama are the same person.”

“Are you fucking with me...? Every one of you is saying something different. What the hell is going on?”

The Blue Witch smiled as she overheard the exchange. She had never been more grateful for Ori's poor social skills than she was right then.

Because he had kept refusing to show his face in public, the Arataki Group had no clue what was going on.

If Ori had been sociable, respectable, and actively involved in society, they would have found out where he was long ago, and Ori would have been in danger.

The situation was still serious.

But seeing the enemy confused by Ori's stubborn social anxiety let her relax.

Ori was so spaced-out, yet just hearing his name somehow made her feel that everything would work out.

The Blue Witch gripped the amulet Ori had given her over her clothes and swore to herself that she would find Ohinata and rescue her no matter what.
