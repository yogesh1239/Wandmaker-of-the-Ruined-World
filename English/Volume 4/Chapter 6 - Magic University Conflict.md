Nanase Nanami, associate professor in Tokyo Magic University's Department of Magic Linguistics, dearly respected Ohinata Kei.

Very few students who had received Ohinata's guidance did not respect her. Even as Nanase advanced from student to research student to associate professor, that respect only grew stronger instead of fading.

The wise, kind, helpful, bright, cute university president ended up being liked even by the most stubborn people. Nanase was no exception. After the stalker incident, she had secretly organized a President Fan Club and worked to keep suspicious people away from Ohinata and the fans in line.

When the emergency declaration was issued early that morning, Nanase had been in the hallway outside the document room with Ohinata.

“Emergency declaration! An enemy force will attack Tokyo in two hours! Remain where you are and wait for rescue!

Emergency declaration! An enemy force will attack Tokyo in two hours! Remain where you are and wait for rescue!

Emergency declaration—”

An eyeball familiar from the Bunkyo Ward Office flew around the campus, repeating the warning in every corner.

Only seconds earlier, Ohinata had been chatting with a gentle smile. In an instant, her expression tightened.

“...This is a problem. Nanase-san, we will close the university and strengthen its defenses now. Please help me. The enemy is probably a group of humans. It is likely the Arataki Group. If it were an attack by a Class A monster, they would not use the words ‘enemy force.’ Build anti-personnel barricades at the entrances and set traps. Go to the Department of Combat Studies and tell them this. That is all. Do you have any questions?”

Ohinata grasped the situation at superhuman speed, made her deductions, and laid out a response without hesitation.

Seeing how composed the girl so much younger than herself was, Nanase, who had nearly panicked, calmed down too. She took a deep breath and asked only one question.

“What will you do, Professor Ohinata?”

“I will go to Professor Sendo. We need the Department of Monster Studies to release its magic beasts and place them at key points around the university. You have no other questions, correct?”

“Y-Yes.”

“Come on, hurry. You are allowed to run in the halls today, you know?”

After giving a mischievous wink, Ohinata ran down the hallway like the wind.

“Be careful, Professor Ohinata!”

After calling out to Ohinata's small but dependable figure, Nanase turned on her heel and started running the other way down the hallway too.

Her heart pounded. This was different from the cold fear that had crept up and closed around her during the mushroom pandemic. This time, it wasn't an epidemic. A clearly hostile force was coming to attack them.

Memories of the coup caused by the Iruma Mage flashed through her mind, making her nauseous. Fights between Transcendents were terrifying, and there was no knowing what might happen. One unknown spell could easily turn the tide of battle.

Could the Tokyo Witches' Council, which had lost more than ten people since its peak, really defend Tokyo from the Arataki Group even with the Blue Witch there...?

When Nanase rushed into the Department of Combat Studies' laboratory, Professor Onigawara was already giving orders while students brought out magic wands and Monster Traps. Red marks covered key points on a campus map scrawled across the whiteboard. They were right in the middle of making a plan.

“Professor Onigawara! A message from the President. The enemy is believed to be the Arataki Group, a group of Transcendents. She said to put up anti-personnel barricades at the entrances and set traps.”

“We have already started.”

As Nanase caught her breath, Onigawara answered curtly, his already stern face twisted into an even more frightening scowl.

“The plan is to place magic snipers on the roof and shoot down enemies caught in the Monster Traps. But we need something to serve as a wall. We have sent people to the Department of Monster Studies to borrow magic beasts.”

“Professor Ohinata is at the Department of Monster Studies right now... Wait, don't tell me you intend to fight them?”

Nanase sensed something off and asked. Onigawara nodded as though it were obvious.

“If we are attacked, we fight back.”

“Please wait. The instruction that came with the emergency declaration was ‘Remain where you are and wait for rescue’! The President only ordered us to strengthen the university's defenses. She did not order a counterattack. Half-baked attack magic will not work on Transcendents.”

“You cannot say that for certain. Even mosquito bites will make you bleed to death if you take a million of them.”

“Th-That's such a meathead way of thinking...”

Onigawara shoved a stab-resistant vest from a locker into the speechless Nanase's arms, then hurried off with the armed students.

His quick response to the emergency was admirable. But President Ohinata and the professor were already taking different approaches.

Nanase held her head.

There was no way they could beat Transcendents. And yet Professor Onigawara was raring to fight.

Was there any chance of victory? Or was he just hot-blooded? The former would have been good, but the latter would have been the worst. If they attacked the enemy and only ended up making them angry, there was no knowing what the angry Transcendents might do to them.

Normally, an emergency like this would bring more detailed instructions from the Foresight Mage. That had been the case during the mushroom pandemic.

There should have been specific instructions as though he had seen the future—or rather, because he really had seen the future. Instructions like, “Take that and go here,” “Tell this to that person,” or “Do this by night.” That kind of instruction.

And yet this time, the instructions lacked any specifics at all.

Because they lacked specifics, the people on the ground had to make more decisions for themselves, and the chain of command was falling into disorder.

Nanase feared that the Foresight Mage was already in a situation where he could no longer give specific instructions.

At the same time, Onigawara's arbitrary judgment irritated her.

Fine, forget his defiance of the Foresight Mage's instructions for now. But why won't he follow the President's instructions? Why do something unnecessary? Ohinata had turned the Foresight Mage's vague instructions into something concrete. That should be enough. Does he really think his judgment is better than Ohinata Kei's?

The chain of command needed to be unified.

Rather than everyone sending messengers back and forth, all the important people at the university needed to gather and coordinate their response.

Nanase sorted out her thoughts, then ran back into the hallway crowded with panicked people to gather everyone.

---

The emergency declaration, issued two hours before the attack, had given the university a fair amount of time, if not all it needed. Barricades blocked the school gates, potted turret balsam stood in place, and barbed wire had been hidden in the hedges.

They were hardly reassuring defenses against Transcendents. One great spell could easily blow them all away.

But the Foresight Mage had instructed them to wait, not flee. If he had seen a future where the enemy slaughtered all the students of Tokyo Magic University, he should have told them to flee. If the enemy did not mean to slaughter them all—if it intended to hold back—then there was meaning in showing the will to resist.

In the large lecture hall where the noncombatants had gathered, Professor Ohinata paced around, her tail drooping anxiously and her ears constantly twitching. She held her beloved wand Aleister in her arms and could not stop fidgeting with it.

“Maybe negotiate... No, hit them once first... Make it a duel... Give us a common enemy... Spread false information... But then it won't be credible... Ugh...”

The anxious students watched Ohinata mutter. Nanase wanted to knock flat the all-out resistance faction that had pushed past Ohinata's attempts to stop them.

After the emergency faculty meeting, Tokyo Magic University had split in two. The all-out resistance faction and the bloodless surrender faction.

The all-out resistance faction was made up of the hot-blooded Department of Combat Studies and Department of Monster Studies, and it argued for all-out resistance against the Arataki Group.

Even if they couldn't kill a Transcendent, seriously injuring one might force a temporary retreat. Even if they were brushed aside without effort, making the enemy think, These guys bite, would make the enemy handle them more carefully.

The bloodless surrender faction feared that resistance would backfire and argued for submitting to the Arataki Group without resistance.

If they seriously injured a Transcendent, it might fly into a rage, stop holding back, and blow away the university. If they made the enemy think, These guys bite, it might decide to kill anyone who bit.

All-out resistance and bloodless surrender each had one good point.

And since Ohinata had said the best option was for her to negotiate surrender terms across the barricade, her plan had a hundred good points.

The President's policy should naturally have been adopted with a hundred votes to one in favor, but the all-out resistance faction would not yield, and volunteers ended up going ahead with the resistance. It was an unforgivable outrage.

Driven by anxiety, Nanase quietly approached the troubled-looking Ohinata and whispered in her ear.

“Professor Ohinata. Please, at least save yourself. It isn't too late. I will clear a path for you.”

“I cannot. If the head of an organization runs away after things have come this far, we will lose trust and morale will collapse. Whether we surrender or resist, I have to be here.”

Ohinata smiled faintly and whispered back. Nanase's chest tightened.

Why did she have to bear such a heavy responsibility?

It was always like this. Ever since the Gremlin Disaster, the people Nanase wanted to have an easy life were the ones who suffered, and the people she wanted to live were the ones who died.

Nanase grabbed Ohinata's sleeve and pulled it hard.

“If you give the word, Professor, there is nothing we cannot do. If those people catch you, there is no knowing what they will do to you.”

Through a gap in the boards nailed over the windows, she could see the Arataki Group gathered outside the blocked school gates. Every last one had either dyed their hair flashy colors or shaved it close, and they wore suits sloppily over flashy patterned shirts. Their ears and lips were covered in tasteless piercings, and they laughed like idiots while shouting vulgar abuse and waving swords and nail-studded bats around.

The Arataki Group members surrounding the university were painfully obvious <ruby>yakuza<rt>outlaws</rt></ruby>.[^1]

Of course, having piercings did not necessarily make someone a bad person, and Tokyo's security forces used close-combat weapons too. If a rumpled suit made someone the enemy, every salaryman coming off a night shift would qualify.

But every part of the Arataki Group oozed so much ugliness that Nanase had no desire to look for their good points or defend them.

Ohinata glanced out the window beside Nanase and sighed.

“They don't look like the kind of people who would go home if we asked, do they?”

“How can you be so carefree...!?”

“Foresight-san said to wait. If it would be better to run, he would say to run. And besides.”

Ohinata paused, then put on a bright smile.

“Ao-san, the Blue Witch, is in Tokyo. If we hang on, she will beat up all the bad guys! The whole Arataki Group will end up like this. And this!”

As she spoke, Ohinata wagged her tail fiercely, put on a sharp expression, and shadowboxed.

Nanase nodded vaguely.

Nanase knew well how close the Blue Witch and Ohinata Kei were. Tokyo's strongest witch openly declared, “I will protect Kei-chan. I will not go easy on anyone who lays a hand on her.”

She probably meant every word.

But in fact, the Blue Witch was not here right now.

The Blue Witch was not where Ohinata Kei was.

Strength and dependability were separate matters.

During the Iruma coup, the Blue Witch had attacked without concern for catching allies in her attacks, and every resident of her home territory of Ome had died. No matter how much she boasted that she would absolutely protect Ohinata Kei, Nanase could not completely believe her.

Even now, the Arataki Group that had attacked Tokyo and surrounded the university wore flippant, vulgar smiles, and there was no sign at all of the Blue Witch's hammer coming down.

“If the Arataki Group uses me as a hostage, Ao-san will have trouble too, so that is the only thing I am worried about. But I have countermeasures in place, so please do not worry. It will be fine!”

Right after Ohinata gave that assurance and pumped her fist, there was movement in front of the gates.

The crowd of punks split apart, and two men appeared.

One was a tough-looking, blue-eyed man who looked to be in his fifties. Following him was a bald man with tattoos on his arms and sunglasses. Both were gripping raw magic stones.

“We're the Arataki Group! Starting today, Tokyo's ours! Got that? Then bow your heads!!”

At the bald man's roar, Professor Onigawara stepped forward from Tokyo Magic University's battle line and shouted back.

“We refuse!”

“I can't hear anything but yes! Want me to twist that mouth of yours until you can only say yes?”

“…………”

Professor Onigawara stuck up his middle finger, silently taunting the bald man.

A vein bulged on the bald man's forehead as he cleared the barricade with an inhuman leap and landed inside.

The instant his foot touched the ground, a ring of light appeared beneath it, then vanished into the magic stone he held.

Professor Onigawara gasped, and Nanase and Ohinata, who had been watching, were shocked too.

“No way. The Monster Trap failed!?”

“!! I see, it's ground! The magic stone served as ground! Come to think of it, we have never caught someone carrying a magic stone in a Monster Trap...!”

Ohinata leaned forward and spoke as she watched intently through the gap in the window.

A Monster Trap was a time-stasis magic tool made by linking together crushed magic stones of several kinds. It could slow time to an incredible crawl for anyone who stepped on it.

But if another magic stone entered the trap, it apparently distorted and disrupted the flow of magic power through the Monster Trap.

Nothing had happened, but the bald man seemed to realize the trap had tried to do something to him.

“W-What was that? Some weird thing—”

Before the enemy's shock turned into the will to fight, Professor Onigawara raised his wand-holding right hand as a signal.

At the signal to begin the battle, the seven wizards waiting behind him quickly readied their magic tools. They formed a fan-shaped formation, synchronized their notes through the Seven Wind-Instrument Ritual Implements, and rang out a ritual-magic incantation.

“<ruby>Naetaku Ji-shio Ontaruku Ea××× Mabuinai××<rt>The warrior held fast by chains relied on his master</rt></ruby>”

The Seven Wind-Instrument Ritual Implements were harmonica-shaped magic tools originally made by Wand Maker 0933 for research into unpronounceable sounds.

Their amplification ratio was negative. They had not been made for combat in the first place.

But ritual magic let several people join forces to perform magic that ordinary people could not cast, and that was more than effective enough on a battlefield.

As the ritual-magic implements shattered from the forced use of magic, chain magic, which the now-dead Hachioji Witch had excelled at, activated at the same time.

Dull-gray chains burst from empty space and attacked the enemy like living things. They wrapped around the arm he tried to shake them off with, then wound around his whole body.

“You... bastard!”

“Well, well. Walked right into that one, huh? Need help?”

The blue-eyed mage who seemed to be his boss laughed in amusement.

Perhaps he found that humiliating. The bald man's face turned bright red as he growled.

“No, sir! Boss, just watch from there! This... This kind of magic ain't even got proper magic power in it...!”

His defiant boast was cut off by chains winding around his mouth like a gag.

Now that the Monster Trap had failed, they had been forced to use their prized ritual incantation for chain magic right at the start.

Even those magic chains were being yanked with brute force and could snap at any moment.

The witch's magic that people had recreated by pooling their wisdom and magic power was much weaker than when used by the witch herself.

Even so, it had held him up.

Nanase, who had opposed resistance, watched while holding her breath. Would the fangs of a human wizard unit reach a Transcendent?

“Begin the volley! <ruby>Do Vaa-ra<rt>Freezing Javelin</rt></ruby>!”

Professor Onigawara stepped back, aimed with his wand, and fired ice-spear magic at the mage bound by magic chains. That set off the magic barrage.

“<ruby>Do Vaa-ra<rt>Freezing Javelin</rt></ruby>!”

“<ruby>De-nitsu Fuetosu Iento-dotsuto Hia-zui<rt>The blood pact brought about an evil revolution</rt></ruby>!”

“<ruby>Do Vaa-ra<rt>Freezing Javelin</rt></ruby>!”

“<ruby>![gaiji-0002.png](images/gaiji-0002.png)-<rt>Fire</rt></ruby>!”

“<ruby>Do Vaa-ra<rt>Freezing Javelin</rt></ruby>!”

“<ruby>Do Vaa-ra<rt>Freezing Javelin</rt></ruby>!”

The repertoire of magic ordinary people could use was limited. The wizards Tokyo Magic University was proud of pointed their wands at the enemy in perfectly coordinated stances and unleashed the most powerful magic each of them could cast.

Ice spears filled the air.

Light bullets and beams hammered the enemy.

A swarm of bats made of fresh blood attacked.

Those who had used up their magic power immediately fell back and were replaced by the next wizards. In less than ten seconds, the number of spells fired without interruption must have reached one hundred.

The storm of magic striking the enemy kicked up a cloud of dust, and the poor visibility made it impossible to aim.

“Stop! Spread out!”

Once the dust cloud had spread and they could no longer aim, Professor Onigawara quickly gave the order, turned his back on the enemy, and fled. The wizard unit scattered cleanly in every direction too.

“Gaaaah!! I'll fucking kill you!!”

The bald man tore through the chains and burst from the dust cloud. His suit was in tatters. His sunglasses were broken too.

But what about the injuries that mattered—

“He's bleeding! It's working, Professor Ohinata!”

“Not yet. A mage would naturally heal that much in a few hours. It does not even count as an injury.”

Ohinata calmly corrected the delighted Nanase.

As the two watched, the battle shifted again.

As the bald man tried to use some kind of magic on the wizard unit fleeing in all directions, a rain of magic from diagonally above slammed him into the ground. A group of wizards waiting on the roof had begun sniping.

Each time the man who had been forced to the ground tried to stand, the falling ice spears and light bullets pounded him and made him drop to one knee again.

But that was the end of their advantage.

In a single instant, the mage who had been taking one-sided attacks completely regained his footing.

Then the next instant, he started running at an unbelievable speed.

The bald mage reached top speed in no time, and the magic stopped hitting him entirely. Every spell fired down at him landed three or four steps behind him.

It was simple. A moving target was hard to hit. Especially one moving that fast.

With a long enough run-up, the mage raced up the outer wall of the south building without using magic and reached the roof.

He easily dodged all the murderous seed bullets fired by the turret balsam lined up on the roof, then used that momentum to send every wizard on the roof flying with his punches.

Nanase gasped and covered Ohinata's eyes with her hand so she would not see the grisly sight.

Blood sprayed, and human bodies were ripped apart and thrown through the air.

Even the lucky wizards who avoided instant death despite being sent flying by a Transcendent's attack fell from the roof and made grotesque flowers of blood bloom on the ground below.

Without even using magic. The enemy mage easily crippled half of the university's wizard unit.

Nanase went pale. She wanted to slap herself for thinking, even for a moment, that they might have a chance. After all that, he had only suffered scratches and bruises all over his body.

The difference in fundamental strength between humans and Transcendents was far too great.

A Transcendent was someone who had transcended the human realm.

Humans could not defeat them after all.

“...The outcome is decided. Nanase-san, thank you for covering my eyes. I am all right now.”

Ohinata's trembling voice did not sound all right at all. She gently moved Nanase's hand away.

The victorious mage's booming laughter echoed throughout the university. The students taking shelter in the large lecture hall trembled and shrank in on themselves. Cold fear filled the room.

Ohinata had to be terrified and surely wanted to cry, but she bravely kept her composure and gave Nanase firm instructions.

“I will request a ceasefire from the Arataki Group. Somehow, I will negotiate for everyone's lives—”

“I understand. Leave it to me. Even if it costs me my life, I will stop this fighting from going any further.”

As Nanase tried to dash out of the lecture hall in Ohinata's place, Ohinata tugged her sleeve and stopped her.

“This is my job as the president of Tokyo Magic University.”

“No, this is an adult's job! Professor Ohinata, you are still a child!”

Furious, Nanase shook her hand free and tried to leave, but the adorable girl with small beast ears would not let her go.

After taking a deep breath, Ohinata spoke in a calm, soft voice, like a professor advising a student about their future.

“Nanase-san. You have always treated me as an adult. You have done so much for me behind the scenes, respecting what I do and supporting me. The respect in your eyes when you see me as a researcher in my own right has made me very happy. So I do not want you to treat me like a child only at a time like this.”

The first president of Tokyo Magic University, Ohinata Kei, gently wrapped both hands around the speechless Nanase's hand and softly ordered her.

“Nanase-san. Please hide somewhere. If everyone surrenders and the Arataki Group decides to execute us all, we will all be wiped out. We need someone to tell whoever comes to rescue us what happened here.

No. I will not hear any objections. So that we do not all go down together, so that you can help me later, endure for now. No matter what happens. All right?”

Ohinata's eyes were firm. She would never yield.

Nanase could not force a sound from her throat. She only nodded.

The Foresight Mage had said, “Wait for rescue.”

All she could do was believe those words.

Rescue would surely, definitely come.

In agony, Nanase turned her back on the professor she respected and disappeared into the darkness of the school building to carry out the professor's instructions.
