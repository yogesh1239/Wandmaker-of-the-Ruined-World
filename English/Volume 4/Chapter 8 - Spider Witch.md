It had been more than five years since the Gremlin Disaster, and five winters had come and gone without a single electric drainage pump running. The air, cooler than aboveground, smelled of animal droppings and dust. It was creepy, but it had an exciting, otherworldly atmosphere. I could kind of see why people got into exploring ruins.
