Today, like every day, the Blue Witch woke up in her quiet house, washed her face, combed her hair, and cleaned the grave in the backyard. It was the same routine every morning.

Back when people still called her Aoyama Hiyori instead of the Blue Witch, she had always liked peaceful mornings.

Her ideal morning started with her little sister gently shaking her awake. Or her sister would crawl under the covers, and she'd hug her and drift back to sleep. That was nice too.

Then she'd get up at her own pace and ease into the day with her family over thickly buttered toast, sausages, and coffee with milk.

But that would never happen again.

After a tasteless breakfast out of a can, the Blue Witch checked that the handgun she'd stripped from a dead police officer still worked. It was her backup weapon in case her magic power ever ran out, and she kept it hidden in the holster on her thigh. She made sure there were bandages in her pouch and went to grab a magic stone for patrolling her territory. It threw her for a second to find a magic wand, not a magic stone, sitting in the usual spot.

The magic wand Cyanos had arrived a few days earlier, but she still wasn't used to the sight of it.

Ori Kenshi, a Wand Maker with a terror of people and a freakish level of dexterity, was the first person she'd been able to trust in a long time.

His personality had its problems, but there wasn't any malice in him. Or any sense of danger, either. She was almost impressed that someone like that had survived this long.

The magic wand sales deal with Okutama's strange creature suited the Blue Witch just fine, too.

After all, she'd practically been handed exclusive distribution rights to the strongest weapon around, by a mile.

From now on, skirmishes with other witches and mages would be easy to settle. If she wanted, she could even dangle magic wands as bait to get people moving and control the balance of power all over the region.

The Blue Witch was based in Ome, in northwestern Tokyo.

She had once lost every last citizen she was supposed to protect. Now she hunted the monsters that wrecked the city, determined to protect at least what was left of her memories, hollow as it was.

What was the point of protecting houses no one would ever come home to?

It led nowhere.

Even so, she stayed put in Ome, like a mother wolf standing watch forever over a den with no pups left in it. She couldn't help it.

With a magic wand, Ome's defenses would be that much stronger.

She owed Ori. With everything breaking, rotting, and slipping away day after day, one memory of the peaceful days, broken like all the rest, had come back to her. How happy that had made her!

Just as her benefactor had asked, the Blue Witch had started vetting potential magic wand buyers.

She'd sell to friendly factions and turn away hostile ones. That alone would let her redraw the balance of power however it suited her.

The Blue Witch meant to get every bit of use out of the broker role that had dropped into her lap. Politics wasn't her strong suit, but it was worth a thought if the payoff was big enough.

If magic wands made a name for themselves as the weapon that changed the balance of power, Ori would be happy. If the balance of power shifted and Ome's defenses got stronger, the Blue Witch would be happy too.

A textbook win-win.

Gripping the magic wand Cyanos tight, the Blue Witch headed out on her daily patrol of the city.

Her job was to hunt monsters that had wandered in from other districts and ones that had spawned naturally inside her own.

Sometimes she also hunted thieves who ignored the warning signs to steal Ome's supplies, or lowlifes who tried to move in without permission. She usually beat them half to death and threw them out of the district, but if they were brazen and showed no remorse, she showed no mercy.

She fought three times that morning, all against small fry.

None of them was a good enough test of how much the magic wand Cyanos amplified her magic.

In her first monster fight after the delivery, she'd fired her weakest ice-spear magic at a carnivorous plant that had grown to the size of a compact car. The magic came out so absurdly amplified that she couldn't control it, and it punched straight through five houses in a row and brought them all down. Overkill.

She couldn't pitch a magic wand without knowing how the product actually performed.

Ori's spec sheet did list the catalog specs. But a line like "Major amplification of magic via a seven-layer core" didn't tell her how much it amplified anything. She'd have to try it to find out.

She wanted a decent target for a test shot, but naturally, no powerful monsters turned up just when she wanted one.

Magic wand in hand, the Blue Witch leaped from roof to roof and spent several hours checking every corner of the city from above to make sure no intruders had gotten in. Then she went home and had an early lunch. When she'd disposed of the Iruma Mage a while back, she'd cleaned out every scrap of food in his territory while she was at it, so her stockpile still had plenty to spare.

Once she's done eating, she'll take more food to Okutama again, and throw in a few pieces of fruit from the tree at Nakamura-san's house next door, too... She was thinking it over when something hit the window with a dull thud.

She turned around to find a floating eyeball ramming the living-room window.

It was the Eyeball Witch's familiar.

Figuring it had come for another round of pointless small talk, she grudgingly opened the window. The eyeball floated inside and started relaying the Eyeball Witch's voice from wherever its mouth was.

"It's an emergency."

The voice was tense and skipped the greeting entirely. The Blue Witch raised an eyebrow.

The usually easygoing Eyeball Witch seemed pretty desperate.

"What?"

"A giant monster came ashore from Tokyo Bay. Coastal Tokyo is a sea of fire right now. I need help."

"Don't come to me with this. Whoever runs that area can deal with it."

The Blue Witch protected Ome, well inland within Tokyo. The coast was outside her territory, and she had no interest in it.

She'd brushed it off coldly, but the eyeball rolled its pupil around in a panic and pressed on.

"When I say giant monster, I mean a kaiju. An actual kaiju. It's easily 100 m long."

"Oh? That's big. Then Bloodsucking's probably running a joint operation."

"The Bloodsucking Mage was killed in the fighting. The Setagaya Witch ran. Right now, the Flame Witch and the Hachioji Witch are holding it off."

Up to then, the Blue Witch had tuned it out as someone else's problem, but the unexpected news made her grip her magic wand hard without meaning to. No matter how powerful the monster was, she couldn't readily believe Bloodsucking was dead.

"Wait. Bloodsucking's dead? Really?"

"Really. But in exchange, every civilian in Minato Ward, where the kaiju came ashore, got out safely."

"I figured you couldn't kill that guy if you tried... I see. So that's how he went."

Unlike her, he'd protected every innocent person in his care right up until he died. She envied him a little for that.

He'd been an unlikable man, forever wrapped up in his little political games, but he deserved respect.

The Blue Witch offered a brief moment of silence, then nodded.

"All right. Call it a parting gift for Bloodsucking. If the kaiju comes near Ome, I'll kill it."

"I know you don't want to leave Ome, but this is really bad. Who knows how long the Flame Witch and the Hachioji Witch can hold out on their own? The Foresight Mage is about to burn out, and the other witches won't lift a finger... Ao-chan, could you come over here? Please. We need every fighter we can get!"

For her part, the Blue Witch would welcome a chance to test Cyanos on a giant monster.

But not badly enough to leave Ome unguarded and head off on an expedition.

Once, out of the goodness of her heart, she'd gone to Kanagawa to kill a monstrous man-eating bird that flew at the speed of sound. The Iruma Mage had used her absence to attack Ome and carry off every last resident.

It was a bitter memory she'd never forget.

Dropping by a neighboring district was one thing. Going anywhere too far to get back from quickly was out of the question.

"I'm not leaving here. If it gets near Ome, pull back the Flame Witch and the Hachioji Witch. I'll hunt it alone."

The two of them had told her they'd been her fans since her magazine-model days. With their faces in mind, the Blue Witch gave the warning out of kindness.

The eyeball went literally wide-eyed and argued back.

"What are you talking about? You can't! You're strong, sure, but you're about on the Iruma Mage's level, right? I'm not even asking you to coordinate with them. Just give them some covering fire—"

"I said I'll do it alone. Anyone who wants to get caught in the crossfire is free to join in."

"...Honestly! Run if it gets dangerous, okay?"

The kaiju's roar came through the floating eyeball, and as if it were hurrying her along, the Eyeball Witch rattled off her last words and cut the connection.

The familiar blinked goodbye at the Blue Witch and bobbed back out the window.

She watched it go, then climbed up onto her roof. Far off, a giant kaiju was advancing, mowing down whole blocks of buildings as it came.

"Huh? It's hu—...!"

It was even bigger than she'd heard. She was speechless.

It looked like a black Tyrannosaurus standing more upright, and it was so enormous she could only think her sense of perspective was off.

Something that big could wreck Tokyo just by going for a stroll. Yet the kaiju was clutching a huge structure that looked like a radio tower and swinging it like a kid throwing a tantrum, smashing everything around it to pieces.

As she stared, stunned, a pillar of fire shot up from the ground. The crimson flames were probably the Flame Witch's.

It was powerful magic, with the scale and firepower to burn an entire skyscraper to a crisp, but it only licked at the knees of that absurdly huge body. The kaiju kept walking as if it didn't even notice the fire at its feet.

Next, a mass of chains shot up from the ground and wrapped around the kaiju. From this distance, they looked thin and flimsy, but these were the Hachioji Witch's sealing chains, famous for their incredible toughness and binding power.

But the kaiju just shook itself irritably, whipped its tail around, and fired a beam from the tip that melted the chains like they were nothing.

Her head started to spin.

She'd seen plenty of monsters beyond human understanding before.

And she'd hunted them down, too, in fights to the death.

In the early days of the Gremlin Disaster, doomsayers had never stopped shouting that humanity was finished. But she'd believed it would never happen, not as long as there were witches like her, Transcendents who'd gained powers beyond human understanding.

There would be sacrifices, but humanity would never die out. She'd believed that.

But watching that living embodiment of violence march in, shaking the ground as if it were strolling across an empty field, she felt her certainty waver. A body that huge, smart enough to swing a weapon, and it could even use magic.

She couldn't believe her eyes. Could a super-creature like that survive even a direct hit from a nuclear bomb?

The Blue Witch was starting to lose her nerve when she remembered the feel of the magic wand in her grip and came back to her senses.

Right.

She can't run.

Ome is right in the monster's path.

Whatever kind of monster this is, she'll protect the streets that still hold her memories of peaceful days. If she can't even do that, something inside her will break.

There's hope.

The old her wouldn't have stood a chance, but now she has the magic wand Cyanos.

If she pours every drop of her magic power into her strongest magic and amplifies it with Cyanos, she's bound to do some damage. At the very least, she can hope it'll hate that enough to change course.

Monsters started out as ordinary animals. She has no idea what this one used to be, but it can't be an exception.

They don't live just to destroy things.

Just like wild animals, monsters run away when they're hurt or feel threatened.

She probably can't beat it.

But if all she has to do is hurt it and drive it off...

As long as she can hold Ome no matter what, it's not her problem if the kaiju veers off and goes on a rampage somewhere else.

The Blue Witch took a deep breath and leveled Cyanos from the rooftop.

The kaiju had seemed far away, but with strides that long, it closed the distance faster than she'd expected.

Right around when it came within range of her magic, the pillar of fire and the chains wrapped around it disappeared. It looked like the other two had started pulling back, as she'd told them to.

Her magic was hard to control at full power.

With a magic wand's amplification on top of that, it would be close to uncontrollable.

Still, all she had to do was fire straight ahead. With a target that big, there was no way she could miss.

The Blue Witch gathered the magic power surging through her body and began pouring it into the wand.

The magic power escaping her body spilled out around her as freezing air.

Frost settled at her feet. The sudden chill and the shift in air pressure produced diamond dust, which danced on the swirling wind.

"Ugh... ahh...!"

The Blue Witch groaned as her magic power tried to lash back into her.

She hadn't poured in even half her magic power yet, and she'd already lost control of the spell.

Just the buildup before the spell went off was enough to freeze her house over. Icicles sprouted everywhere, growing thicker and longer by the second.

Heavy, frigid air settled into her lungs and stabbed at her from the inside, even though she was supposed to handle the cold well.

Limiting the area of damage was completely out of the question.

All she could do was point the wand forward and fire in the kaiju's direction.

But that was enough.

Charge complete, the Blue Witch tightened her frost-covered, half-frozen fingers around the wand's grip, let out a white breath, and smiled.

If this doesn't work, nobody can defeat that monster.

So, full power. Every last drop.

Let's put it to the test, shall we?

The skill of the world's one and only Wand Maker!

Then the Blue Witch recited the incantation and unleashed her strongest magic, the spell that had once wiped out the Iruma Mage in a single blow.

"You, sink beneath the glacier. Sleep in the permafrost!"[^1]

A pale blue wave burst from Cyanos's magic stone with a crack like a gunshot.

The wave itself was invisible, but the air in its path froze and liquefied in an instant, then went further still and turned solid white. The result looked like a merciless white beam lunging at its prey.

The kaiju was no pushover, though.

With reflexes far too quick for something its size, it held up the radio tower in its hand as a shield to block the magic.

But the white wave punched straight through the tower and the kaiju behind it, and a moment later, the whole kaiju froze solid.

And that wasn't all.

The runaway magic burst apart against the kaiju and spread outward, sealing even the city at its feet under thick ice.

To top it all off, the magic reached all the way into the sky and swallowed the clouds, and unseasonable snow began to fall.

Quiet white snow fell over Tokyo, a city trampled by the kaiju and moments from being swallowed by fire and destruction.

A calm silence settled over the world like an act of mercy, and the Blue Witch let out a dry laugh at what her own magic had done.

Who asked for this much?

This makes me the monster that destroys the world.

"Okay, this really is overtechnology, Ori," the Blue Witch murmured weakly. Then her magic power ran dry, and she passed out.
