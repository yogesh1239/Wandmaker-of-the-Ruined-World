## Exact English

### 8 — Night Ward

Kiyose and Higashikurume. Territory of the Night Witch. Monster control and governance are normally sound, but family trouble can make the witch lose all restraint and take it out on everyone nearby. Admirable family devotion; troublesome for the residents.

**Standout:** Thriving agriculture.

### 9 — Flower Ward

Arakawa and Taito. Territory of the Flower Witch. She refuses no arrivals but pursues anyone who leaves. Residents untroubled by her cannibalism—and with no plans to relocate—find the area comfortable.

**Standout:** Premium plant-based luxuries: sugar, honey, vegetable oils, and more.

### 10 — Mermaid Ward

Koto and Chuo. Territory of the Mermaid Witch, covering all Tokyo Bay. Coastal waters remain unusually safe because she hunts nearly every monster in the sea, though exceptionally powerful ones may slip through. Governance is lax.

**Standout:** Thriving fishing industry.

### 11 — Dragon Ward

Musashimurayama, Higashiyamato, and Higashimurayama. Territory of the Dragon Witch. Excellent interim administrators keep affairs moving and cleverly direct the witch’s tremendous power. Mistakes happen, and outsiders judge the region harshly, but residents rate it surprisingly well.

**Standout:** District-wide precious-metal and gemstone mining; many jewelers.

### 12 — Tobacco Ward

Mizuho and Hamura. Territory of the Tobacco Witch. Public safety and morals are poor, but the district is lively. It ranks first in births—and first among places people do not want to raise children. Residents either love it or hate it.

**Standout:** Tobacco, medicinal hemp, horse breeding, and nightlife.

### 13 — Minato Ward

Skilled former residents have returned from Bunkyo and Eyeball Ward, driving rapid reconstruction. Its security could justify a higher rank, but anxiety persists because no Transcendent lives there.

**Standout:** Harbor facilities being rebuilt as Tokyo’s gateway.

### 14 — Suginami Ward

Territory of the Pebble Witch. A golem stands watch at the edge of town, though it is less useful than it looks. Witch and golem alike tend to neglect their duties. The witch claims to protect manga artists, illustrators, idols, and voice actors, but few come.

**Standout:** Self-proclaimed cultural preservation district.

### 15 — Setagaya Ward

Setagaya and Komae. Territory of the Setagaya Witch. Her incompetent, childish, voter-minded “brilliant governance” is widely mocked. Arbitrary decrees are usually ignored. Monster hunting alone preserves minimum order.

**Standout:** No notable industry; heavy emigration and a shrinking population.

### 16 — Zombie Ward

Fuchu, Kunitachi, and Kokubunji. Territory of the Zombie Witch: a city of dead people crawling with countless zombies. Rot hangs in the air, and the few survivors are required to serve the dead.

**Standout:** No industry. A ward filled with the dead produces nothing.

### 17 — Ome Ward

Territory of the Blue Witch. A terrifying place said to release no one who enters alive. Anyone asking to move to Ome is treated as suicidal—unless they were originally an Ome resident.

**Standout:** No industry, but it has the Blue Witch.
