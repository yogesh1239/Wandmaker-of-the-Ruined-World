## Source-transcription and decision record

This section records the reasoning behind the English above, which the shorter pass notes do not capture.

- **Page numbers were read from a dedicated crop, not from the page at full-page scale.** The number column was cropped and upscaled 3×, then read digit by digit: 11, 25, 44, 54, 67, 79, 95, 109, 142, 163, 180, 199, 213, 241, 261, 277, 284. The entry count is seventeen, with no extra or missing line.
- **Titles are not independently translated here.** All seventeen are taken verbatim from the Volume 2 chapter-title map in `novel.config.md` (lines 58–74), which is the project's declared single source of truth for chapter titles and already governs the filed chapter filenames and the EPUB TOC. Retranslating on this page would risk desynchronising the contents page from the book it indexes. This includes leaving the locked-but-stiff `Melt-Recast Gremlin` and `Backlash-Prevention Mechanism` exactly as filed. Any change to a title belongs in that map first, after which this page should be re-rendered.
- **The `番外編` badge is deliberately not folded into the title.** Verified at 3× that the source sets 番外編 noticeably smaller than the title that follows it, so it is a badge rather than part of the title. The config map's `Side Story - I Raised This Protagonist` uses a dash only to produce a valid single-line filename; the source page has no such punctuation, so the badge is kept as its own smaller element.
- **Credit line.** Verified at 4× as 口絵・本文イラスト●かやはら, separated by a solid round bullet rather than a middle dot. 口絵 → `Frontispiece` and 本文イラスト → `Interior Illustrations` are the terms already used for these two classes of art in the project's other image specs; they are joined with `&` to hold the credit to one short line as the source does. The role-bullet-name shape was kept rather than recast as `Illustrations by Kayahara`, because the bullet is part of the page's visual character. かやはら → `Kayahara` follows the romanization already filed across Volumes 1 and 3.
- **The heading `Contents` is already Latin in the source** and was carried through unchanged in position, size, weight, and letterspacing.

Rather than shrinking the body type, the leader-and-number column was widened: the text block keeps the source's left edge at about x = 265 and the page numbers right-align near x = 1230.
