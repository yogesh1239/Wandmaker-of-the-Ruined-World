**Exact English**

Handa Sakunosuke is Tokyo Magic University’s professor of Gremlin engineering, originally from Saitama City—a country of ruin where monsters ran rampant.

He lost his entire family immediately after the Gremlin Disaster, and surviving became his only purpose. For a year he lived by looting, sacrificing strangers so he could continue breathing. Every theft left his heart raw. He never grew used to it, nor could he persuade himself that he was blameless.

Whenever he found a quiet moment, Handa wondered whether his guilt proved he was merely drunk on his own virtue. If he truly hated hurting others, perhaps he should have died before taking so many lives to preserve his own. Exhausted by these thoughts, he abandoned Saitama and followed a faint rumor of a paradise south to Tokyo.

Under the Tokyo Witches’ Council, civilized life slowly healed him. People respected one another, and he could form relationships without keeping a knife behind his back. Having escaped a world of slaughter, Handa understood just how precious peace and trust were.

Now a respected professor of Gremlin engineering, he likes the days when hands that once became stained with blood and betrayal are dirtied only by polishing compound.
