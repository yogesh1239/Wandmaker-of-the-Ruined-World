### Dragon-Furnace-Carved Seven-Layer Blue Wand Cyanos Ver. 2.1

**Owner:** Blue Witch — Aoyama Hiyori  
**Manufactured:** July 10, 2027  
**Core:** Magic stone — the Blue Witch’s  
**Length:** 152 cm

A masterpiece by Wand Maker Ori Kenshi. Continual improvements incorporating the latest technology have kept it his finest work.

The seven-layer, dragon-furnace-carved core’s extraordinary amplification once made the wand a double-edged sword by increasing the danger of magic going berserk. The backlash-prevention mechanism introduced in Ver. 2.0 drastically reduced that backlash.

Ver. 2.1 advanced further with a detachable backlash-prevention mechanism housed inside the handle, lowering the danger of magical runaway to roughly the level of casting with no amplification at all.

This beautiful and terrifying wand explosively increases power while keeping backlash under control. It is becoming synonymous with the Blue Witch herself.
