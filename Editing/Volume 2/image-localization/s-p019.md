## Exact English

### Page Header

Magic Item Catalog

### Moebius-Linked Khakkhara Hariti

**Owner:** Hell Witch  
**Manufactured:** September 23, 2027  
**Core:** Magic stone — the Hell Witch’s  
**Maximum Diameter:** 90 mm  
**Color:** Amber  
**Length:** 160 cm

The tenth creation of Wand Maker Ori Kenshi. This prayer staff was crafted with a blessing for the Hell Witch’s difficult journey. Its motif is the khakkhara, and every choice of material and inscription expresses the hope that she will escape her former livelihood.

Casting magic through the Moebius-ring core stabilizes its power at exactly a one-to-one ratio. It is a reliable companion for the Hell Witch, whose spellcasting tends to be unstable. Whenever magic passes through the core, the inside of the Moebius ring glows gold. The glow neither changes power nor suppresses backlash, but it is delightful all the same—according to the maker.

Ori could have made a higher-performance wand. He believes, however, that no wand could suit the Hell Witch better.

### Thirteen Ritual Implements

**Owner:** Minato Ward Defense Force  
**Manufactured:** November 5, 2027  
**Core:** Giant-kaiju Gremlin  
**Twelve Cores:** 30 mm each  
**Focus Core:** 40 mm  
**Color:** Pale blue  
**Length:** 92 cm

The eleventh creation of Wand Maker Ori Kenshi. This set of thirteen wands contains ring-shaped Moebius cores carved from one large Gremlin so that every core has an identical form.

Thirteen subsidiary wands and one focus wand divide the magic-power cost among their users, enabling a group to cast great magic that no individual could wield alone: ritual magic.

Because the core shapes must match perfectly, even slight damage or deformation causes the set to lose its special function. These delicate wands must be handled with care. The Gremlin’s sickly pale-blue coloring may remind some viewers of Cherenkov radiation.

The printed page number `19` remains unchanged.
