### 07

ハッキリ言って生涯を捧げ魔法杖職人の道に打ち込んだところで、彼らは到底大利に敵わない。それでも、努力をしない理由にはならない。大量生産路線と高級路線の棲み分けは大利にとって必要な事だが、品質で決して大利に勝てない大利以外の職人にとっても必要な事である。

### 06 — The Gremlin Engineering Professor’s Career (continued)

—a comparison. Without one, the intended impression cannot be achieved, and the danger of misjudgment grows. A quality or excellence becomes visible only when compared with something else.

Handa Sakunosuke appears as a foil for Ori Kenshi. Handa is far from mediocre, yet he is still useful as a point of comparison. Even a man of his caliber can barely enter Ori’s field of view. That is how far removed Ori stands as a Wand Maker.

### 07 — The White Fingers

Continuing from the previous chapter, this story concerns ordinary artisans who pale beside Ori. They are nameless craftsmen whom Ori does not even recognize, yet they remain ambitious and continue refining their work.

They devote their lives to the path of the Wand Maker and still cannot rival Ori. Their efforts are not meaningless, however. The gap between mass-production and luxury-grade quality is significant. Their work matters to Ori and to artisans beyond him.

### 08 — The Hell Witch

How can a cannibal monster live among human beings?

If someone burdened with a profession that human society could never accept tried to adapt to that society, what methods would make it possible? This is that kind of story. The author likes watching troublesome people bargain with society and somehow survive.

### 09 — The Bad Oni Who Attacked the Village

An episode from one day on the Hell Witch’s journey. The idea existed from the outline stage, but I omitted it because the pacing suffered. I finally wrote it after all.

It becomes an old tale passed down through a rural village.

### 10 — The Thirteen Ritual Implements

This novel often follows familiar fantasy conventions. Witches exist, magic wands appear, and spells have incantations.

Ritual magic was introduced according to this novel’s internal logic without sacrificing the mysterious appeal of ancient ceremony.

### 11 — Minato Ward Recapture Operation

The operation was a trial: could a human unit with no Transcendent independently reclaim Minato Ward, and could other wards eventually expect the same?

Ori, the witches, and the mages are this novel’s stars, but that does not mean everyone else achieves nothing. I want the story to keep showing that. Two overwhelming Transcendents sweeping everything aside is exhilarating; ordinary people desperately building toward a shared achievement is wonderful too.

I honestly did not expect the death-curse incantation to be so well received. This chapter convinced me to include more incantations in the future.
