## Exact English

### Body

“...”

“And your hair’s had some effort put into it, hasn’t it? It’s looking better than the last time I saw you.”

“...”

Held under that stare, the Blue Witch was stuck for a reply. The person she had wanted to notice the change was not this eccentric woman.

Everything she had noticed, however, was correct.

Choosing silence had apparently been a bad move. The Tobacco Witch had been studying the Blue Witch as she clammed up, and she snapped her fingers at once.

“Ohhh? So it’s a man!”

A grin spread over the Tobacco Witch’s whole face, and her voice brimmed with the delight of lining up 777 on a slot machine.

She had handed a troublesome witch a troublesome piece of bait. When she turned her face away, the woman simply came around in front of her and threw a familiar arm across her shoulders.

“Who? Who is he? What man, from where?”

“There is no man, from anywhere. Just go home. You are not welcome here.”

“Older? Younger? Come on, let me hear it. I’ll teach you how to drink a man you’ve got your eye on under the table at a party.”

“I don’t need it.”

“Oho, so you’re the come-and-get-me type? More the sort to fake being drunk, then. What a late bloomer.”

“Teach me how to shut you up instead...”

“Telling me to keep quiet about a story this good—don’t go saying such heartless things~”

The Tobacco Witch clung on relentlessly, her mood shot straight through the roof and her mouth unstoppable.

Resorting to force felt like admitting defeat. But she stood no chance of winning with words however she struggled, so the Blue Witch took the starry-eyed Tobacco Witch roughly around the waist with both hands and hurled her.

Laughing, the Tobacco Witch twisted her body in midair as neatly as a cat, vanished out of sight, and landed with a light sound beyond the garden wall.

“Scary, scary. I’ll leave it here for today, shall I. Seee you laaater, Ao-chan! Bye now!”

“Never come back.”

She flung the words back at the ones thrown over the wall, and once again all that returned was laughter. The Blue Witch let out a deep, deep sigh and let her shoulders drop.

The Tobacco Witch never changed. Rumor had it she had lost comrades in the pandemic and had gone badly to pieces, but she was completely unbothered.

The Tobacco Witch was quick to get back on her feet. That much, at least, might be worth learning from.

Thinking as much, the Blue Witch went back into the house to finish the rest of a breakfast that had gone cold.

The printed page number `34` remains unchanged.
