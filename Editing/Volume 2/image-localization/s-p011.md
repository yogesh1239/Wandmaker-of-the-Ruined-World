**Exact English**

Her real name is Himori Fumika. She was in her second year of middle school when the Gremlin Disaster struck: the serious eldest daughter of a serious family, consisting of her parents and younger sister. As a human, she had above-average natural intelligence and decent physical ability. Earnest to a fault, she approached everything sincerely and maintained excellent grades. Her gentle nature and respect for rules made her both liked and relied upon by her teachers. Give her control of the purse strings and she would keep the books clear while managing the money sensibly.

She now manages Shinagawa Ward with her family's help. Drawing on her abilities and instincts as a witch, she has built up heat-processing industries centered on metallurgy. The district is steadily rebuilding as a stable, distinctive community. She has never missed or arrived late to a regular Tokyo Witches' Council meeting. The Eyeball Witch and Foresight Mage value her as one of their few reliably stable combat assets.

She has been a devoted fan of Aoyama Hiyori—the Blue Witch—since Hiyori's modeling days. She bought every magazine featuring Hiyori and followed every piece of information about her. Learning that Hiyori had become a witch like her, then joined the same Tokyo Witches' Council, felt like fate and left her walking on air.

She desperately wants to pour out her feelings to the Blue Witch, but understands the boundaries expected of an ordinary fan. She restrains herself and expresses her affection modestly, never going far enough to cause trouble. The Blue Witch, in turn, recognizes that the Flame Witch is not a bad person.

Recently, the Flame Witch's body, once 150 cm tall, has been shrinking smaller and smaller. Her magic power, physical strength, and heat have all declined. What in the world is going to happen to her? The Flame Witch is deeply afraid.
