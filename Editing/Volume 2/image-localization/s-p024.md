### 6位　調布区

### 7位　三鷹区（小金井・三鷹・武蔵野）

## Exact English

### Header

Tokyo Residential Popularity Ranking

### 1 — Bunkyo Ward

Territory of the Foresight Mage. Tokyo’s most desired place to live: safe, pleasant, and backed by strong social services. It offers the highest standard of living an ordinary person can obtain in the ruined world, so hopeful migrants arrive constantly. Home of Tokyo Magic University.

**Standout:** Tokyo Magic University.

### 2 — Shinagawa Ward

Territory of the Flame Witch. Monster suppression is swift and careful, working conditions are good, and industrial growth looks promising. A serious, responsible witch is reassuring as a ruler.

**Standout:** Thriving metallurgy.

### 3 — Nerima Ward

Territory of the Spider Witch. A curfew is enforced; dense fog rolls in nightly and sweeps away monsters throughout the area. The witch is cautious about accepting migrants. Governance is sound, though some distrust a ruler who never shows herself.

**Standout:** No signature industry, but every sector is growing steadily.

### 4 — Kita Ward

Territory of the Northern Witch. Monsters are eliminated quickly, and the witch’s father directs policy competently. The witch herself is still young and sometimes suffers breakdowns that leave her withdrawn, which can make the territory feel unreliable.

**Standout:** Exceptionally well-trained guard force.

### 5 — Eyeball Ward

Toshima, Nakano, Meguro, Shibuya, Shinjuku, and Chiyoda. The Eyeball Witch governs this vast territory through numerous familiars. Simultaneous monster attacks can delay response. Decisions seek consensus, so meetings and negotiations take time. Fair, but often indecisive.

**Standout:** No special strengths and no special weaknesses—a safe, dependable region.

### 6 — Chofu Ward

Territory of the Chofu Witch. Residents are concentrated in the center; abandoned buildings are demolished and converted into farmland. Water from the Tama River is plentiful, though poor soil limits harvests. Yields rise each year.

**Standout:** Thriving agriculture.

### 7 — Mitaka Ward

Koganei, Mitaka, and Musashino. The Mitaka Witch abandoned personal governance from the beginning and relies on a political machine of surviving metropolitan assembly members. They are corrupt but capable; she prefers competent crooks to honest incompetents, a judgment unpopular with residents.

**Standout:** Many industries are reasonably developed, and goods are plentiful.
