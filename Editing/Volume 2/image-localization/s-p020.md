## Exact English

### Blood Wand Vampir

**Owner:** Kyogoku Yamato  
**Manufactured:** November 11, 2027  
**Core:** Blood Moon magic stone  
**Diameter:** 105 mm  
**Color:** Red, with inclusions  
**Length:** 92 cm

Manufactured by Professor Handa’s Department of Gremlin Engineering team at Tokyo Magic University and finish-polished by Wand Maker Ori Kenshi.

The core once belonged to the Bloodsucking Mage. It was taken by the Dragon Witch, recovered by the Blue Witch, and—following the Bloodsucking Mage’s last wishes—put to use for Minato Ward. The core was left without internal multilayer processing to preserve room for future upgrades.

Because the magic stone is enormous, a counterweight was fitted into the staff’s butt to balance it. Its 92-centimeter length suits Kyogoku Yamato, a former Self-Defense Forces member trained in rifle bayonet techniques. Matching the wand’s length, weight, and center of gravity to an assault rifle lets him adapt those techniques for close combat. Its heavy steel body is sturdy enough for melee: a pure combat wand.

### Lower Technical Note

This magic item was developed from what Ori learned by dissecting the parasitic mushroom responsible for the mushroom pandemic. It accelerates magic-power recovery.

Milky-white Gremlin created with electricity is mixed with personal-color Gremlin refined from the wearer’s blood, producing a marbled Gremlin. The effect is specific to its owner.

When the personal-color ratio is high, recovery increases for the personal-color owner but falls for a wearer associated with the white Gremlin. When the white ratio is high, the reverse occurs. Effectiveness is unrelated to particle size once the marbling is at least 0.1 mm; the mixing ratio is what matters.

The material supports a wide variety of designs and can be made into necklaces, earrings, rings, bracelets, hair ornaments, and other accessories. Its current effect is somewhat modest, but its performance should improve in the future.
