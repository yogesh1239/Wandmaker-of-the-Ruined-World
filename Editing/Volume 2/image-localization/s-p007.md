### Biography

**Verbatim Japanese**

本名は佐々杜愛。専業主婦。夫婦で子供を持たないと決めていて、悠々自適の有閑マダムをやっていた。多趣味で、夫婦仲は良好。夫婦というより趣味友に近かった。魔女になってから、人生は激変した。変異した妻を恐れ、化け物とまで呼んだ夫を、花の魔女は自らの領地から追い出した。以来顔を思い出す事すらしていない。生死にも興味はない。花の魔女は魔女になってから芽生えた「もっと己の一族を増やしたい」という欲求に戸惑ったが、不快とも思わなかったので、そのように動きはじめた。花の魔女は以前と比べ冷酷非情になった事を自覚している。自分とその一族以外が駒にしか見えない。利用価値でしか人を量れない。しかしこのような変貌を打ち明けても弱みになり損をするだけなので、密かに気に病みながらもまるで普通の人間の感性を持っているかのように振る舞っている。それを見抜き寄り添ってくれた吸血の魔法使いには一定の敬意と畏れを抱いていた。大利賢師の事は、家族と他人の中間のように認識している。一族繁栄を助けた優秀で貴重な駒に対する好意なのか？それともただの「大利賢師」に対する好意なのか？自分でも分からないが、とても好ましく思っているのは確かだ。

**Exact English**

Her real name is Sasamori Ai. She was a full-time housewife. She and her husband had decided not to have children, and she lived a leisurely life of comfort. She had many hobbies, and the couple got along well—more like hobby partners than husband and wife.

After she became a witch, her life changed completely. Her husband feared his transformed wife and even called her a monster, so the Flower Witch drove him from her territory. She has not so much as recalled his face since, and has no interest in whether he lives or dies. The Flower Witch was bewildered by the desire that arose after her transformation—“I want to expand my clan”—but she did not find it unpleasant, so she began acting on it.

The Flower Witch knows that she has become cold and ruthless. Everyone outside herself and her clan looks like a game piece to her, and she can judge people only by their usefulness. Revealing that change would only expose a weakness and cost her, so although it secretly troubles her, she behaves as though she still possesses ordinary human sensibilities. She held a measure of respect and fear for the Bloodsucking Mage, who saw through her and stayed by her side.

She regards Ori Kenshi as something between family and an outsider. Is her affection for a talented, precious piece who helped her clan prosper? Or is it simply affection for “Ori Kenshi”? Even she does not know, but she is certain that she likes him very much.
