### Body

Carrying the argument further clearly wasn’t going to make her leave, so the Blue Witch gave up and opened the door. The Tobacco Witch, smirking away, gave her a breezy wave. She had a metal bat slung over one shoulder with a baseball glove capped over the end, dressed casually enough for a quick run to a convenience store.

The Tobacco Witch spoke smoothly, without taking the cigarette out of her mouth.

“Sorry about that~, making you open up. Any tea going?”

“Of course there isn’t. What do you want?”

“There you go getting all prickly again. Come on, I brought you good news.”

“Your good news has never once turned out to be good news. Grab your ball and go home.”

“Mm-hmm, so you’ll let me get the ball? Say what you like, Ao-chan, you were raised right. Aww, you’re too cuuute.”

“I’ll ram Cyanos into you.”

She brandished her magic wand in threat, and the Tobacco Witch cackled, raised both hands in a surrender pose, and headed for the back garden to fetch the ball.

The witch adored using little tricks and props to do strange things, and she really had thrown a baseball into the garden. She picked the ball up, screwed it into her own pocket, and set a hard candy and a manga volume gently down by the grave in its place.

The Blue Witch watched the Tobacco Witch put her hands together and offer a short silent prayer, and felt something she couldn’t put a name to. She wasn’t just a pushy nuisance. She was a woman who could be thoughtful about things like this, and that made her impossible to cut off entirely.

She hadn’t been a stranger to the little sister either... The Blue Witch was remembering that when a thought struck her. She picked up the manga left as an offering and slid off the dust jacket—and sure enough, the contents didn’t match. Inside the jacket was no ordinary manga, but a shojo manga far too racy for the cover.

She was speechless for a second, then flung the indecent offering at the Tobacco Witch.

“Idiot! What kind of thing are you leaving as an offering?! You idiot!”

“Ah, busted. Well, well—if she were alive she’d be right at the age to get curious about this stuff, wouldn’t she? Nothing but stiff, educationally approved books, candy for little kids, and a pat on the head? That’d be boring.”

“Shut up!”

“Now, now. Truth is, I knew about her. Sweet little face on her, and she used to trade dirty talk behind her big sister’s back.”

“Huh...”

“No, sorry, I’m kidding. Still, I don’t think you should polish your memories up too much. That’s just my opinion.”

“Y-You—!”
