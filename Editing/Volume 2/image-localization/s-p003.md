### Biography

**Verbatim Japanese**

本名は具志堅小雀。フリーターを名乗っているが、競馬、パチンコ、麻雀などで生計を立てていた。変異に伴う昏睡から目覚め、グレムリン災害によって大混乱状態の東京を目の当たりにした時、煙草の魔女は真っ先に「借金をうやむやにできる！」と喜んだ。しかし一拍遅れてそれどころではない事に気付き、パチンコ仲間の足腰が悪いジジイや麻雀仲間の手癖が悪いババアを助けるために慌ててボロアパートを飛び出した。趣味が趣味なのでグレーゾーンやブラックゾーンに理解があり、大麻の違法栽培をしていた犯罪者を仲間に引き入れ医療用麻薬の生産を開始したり、水商売の人々に継続的な労働の場を作りガス抜きに気を配ったりなど、独特の仲間たちを引き連れ独特な領地管理を行っている。魔女になってから広い家に住むのと飯を奢るのが好きになり、怪我や病気、精神疾患で働けない者達への炊き出しなどもやっている。ところが細かい部分に気を配る割に足元が疎かで、保護や食べ物を求めて流入する人口を養いきるほどの食料確保ができず、領地の食糧事情や治安は悪い。奇行に走りがちだがいつも笑顔を絶やさない煙草の魔女は魔女集会内でも交友関係が広く、同じ麻雀好きの三鷹の魔女とは特に仲が良い。粗製ではあるものの貴重な痛み止めや巻き煙草、酒を東京各所に融通する代わりに食料を流してもらい、ギリギリなんとかやっている。仲間からは本名別読みの「コガラちゃん」「ガラやん」などと呼ばれ親しまれていた。そんな仲間たちをキノコパンデミックにより根こそぎ失った煙草の魔女は大泣きして大荒れした。だが、墓石に一つ一つ丁寧に酒瓶を叩きつけ、山積みの煙草に火をつけ盛大な送り火をした後、煙草の魔女はケロリと日常に戻った。少なくとも、表面上はそう見える。楽しければ豪快に笑い、辛くとも笑ってみせるのが、煙草の魔女という女である。

**Exact English**

Her real name is Gushiken Kosuzume. She called herself a freeter, but made her living from horse racing, pachinko, mahjong, and the like. When she awoke from the coma caused by her mutation and saw Tokyo in chaos after the Gremlin Disaster, her first reaction was delight: “Now I can make my debts disappear!” A beat later, she realized that was hardly the issue and rushed out of her run-down apartment to help an old pachinko buddy with bad legs and an old mahjong friend with sticky fingers.

Given her interests, she understands the gray and black sides of society. She brought criminals who had been illegally growing marijuana into her group and started producing medical narcotics, while also giving people from the nightlife trade steady work and ways to blow off steam. With her unusual companions in tow, she runs her territory in her own unusual way. After becoming a witch, she developed a taste for living in a large house and treating people to meals, and she also runs soup kitchens for those unable to work because of injury, illness, or mental disorders.

For all her attention to small details, however, she neglects the fundamentals. She cannot secure enough food to support everyone seeking protection and meals, leaving her territory short on food and poor in public safety. Though prone to bizarre behavior, she never stops smiling. She is broadly connected within the Tokyo Witches' Council and is especially close to the Mitaka Witch, who shares her love of mahjong. In exchange for sending crude but valuable painkillers, hand-rolled cigarettes, and alcohol across Tokyo, she receives food and just barely keeps things running.

Her friends affectionately called her “Kogara-chan” and “Gara-yan,” alternate readings of her real name. When the mushroom pandemic took every one of those friends from her, she wept and raged. But after carefully smashing a bottle of alcohol against each gravestone and lighting a great farewell bonfire from a mountain of cigarettes, the Tobacco Witch returned to daily life as if nothing had happened. At least, that was how it looked on the surface. She laughs heartily when life is fun, and makes herself smile even when it hurts. That is the kind of woman the Tobacco Witch is.
