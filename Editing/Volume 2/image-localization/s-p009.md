**Exact English**

The Iruma Mage concealed his real name because it carried the stench of infamy in certain circles. He had been evil even while he was still human—ever since he was old enough to understand the world. He was simply born that way; there was no extraordinary tragedy in his past. By the time he entered middle school, he had already learned to manipulate his parents with words, turning them into virtual slaves who served him gladly.

As an adult, he founded a company and built his own kingdom, controlling his employees by every means available: words, violence, contracts, and more. The company produced excellent results, yet whenever misconduct came to light, the employee least useful to the company invariably proved to bear full responsibility and was punished. On the surface, it was a wonderful, rapidly growing company with healthy finances. A very small number of sharp-eyed investors, however, smelled something rotten.

When the Gremlin Disaster awakened him as a mage, he felt an evil impulse identical to the one he had always possessed. Many people had their personalities warped when they transformed into Transcendents. The Iruma Mage had been warped from the beginning, so nothing about him changed.

It was the Iruma Mage who proposed the Tokyo Witches' Council rule against revealing real names. His stated concern was that an enemy might use hostile curse-like magic triggered by a person's name, but in truth, he merely wanted to hide his own notoriety. He told convincing lies without hesitation, but whenever he could achieve the same result without lying, he did so to avoid even the slightest risk of losing trust if a lie was exposed. Fortunately, his transformation completely changed both his appearance and apparent age. The precaution worked, and nobody connected the Iruma Mage with the president of a once-prominent company.

While playing the part of a refreshing, personable, and capable boy mage, he plotted in secret and moved outside the Bloodsucking Mage's notice. He regarded her as special—and remained wary of her—because she alone could converse with him smoothly without needing his ideas simplified.

After arranging everything beneath the surface and becoming certain of victory, the Iruma Mage launched his coup. Events proceeded smoothly, and it seemed he would seize the entire Tokyo Witches' Council. Then the enraged Blue Witch awakened as a cold-blooded combat machine. Forced into single combat with that terrifying witch, the Iruma Mage was defeated and killed. His ultimate objective had been complete domination of the entire world. With the outrageous capabilities of puppetry magic and his intelligence from another dimension, he might even have achieved it.
