### Biography

**Verbatim Japanese**

本名は聖代橋ユイ。女子大生。聖代橋家は放任主義だった。母は栄養価を計算した手料理を食べさせる以外何も強制しなかったし、父は毎年必ず健康診断に連れて行き体調不良の自己申告をさせる以外何も強制しなかった。普通の家庭の温かさとは随分違っていたが、確かな愛情を感じながら、ユイはすくすくと育った。グレムリン災害が起きた時、ユイは変異に伴う昏睡状態に陥った。身動きできないユイを、ある女性が命懸けで三日三晩魔物から護り抜いた。朦朧とする意識の中で見たその大きな背中を、ひるがえる特攻服を、身の丈の倍ほどもある魔物を金属バットで打ち据えながら吠えるド根性の叫びを、ユイは死んでも忘れない。放任主義の家庭で育ち、自己責任を覚えてきたユイにとって、見ず知らずの他人が（それもいかにも素行が悪そうな恰好をした他人が）命懸けで自分を護り抜いてくれたという体験は、人生の根底を揺るがす衝撃的なものだった。昏睡から回復し、ようやくまともに動けるようになった時、ユイは真っ先に恩人に礼を言った。返事はなかった。ユイを背中に庇って仁王立ちしたまま、恩人は事切れていた。かくしてユイは名前も分からない恩人の服を貰い受け、地獄の魔女となった。

**Exact English**

Her real name is Miyohashi Yui. She was a college student. The Miyohashi family believed in a hands-off approach to parenting. Her mother never forced her to do anything except eat home-cooked meals with carefully calculated nutrition, and her father never forced her to do anything except attend an annual physical and report whenever she felt unwell. It was quite different from the warmth of an ordinary family, but Yui grew up healthy and secure in their unmistakable love.

When the Gremlin Disaster struck, Yui fell into the coma that accompanied her mutation. As she lay unable to move, a woman risked her life to protect her from monsters for three days and three nights. Yui would never forget the broad back she saw through her haze, the long delinquent coat whipping through the air, or the gutsy roar as the woman beat down monsters twice her size with a metal bat.

Yui had grown up in a hands-off household and learned to take responsibility for herself. For a complete stranger—one dressed like an obvious delinquent, no less—to risk her life protecting Yui was an experience so shocking that it shook the foundations of her life.

When Yui finally recovered from the coma and could move properly again, the first thing she did was thank her savior. There was no reply. Her benefactor had died on her feet, still standing guard with Yui sheltered behind her. And so Yui inherited the clothes of the woman whose name she never learned and became the Hell Witch.
