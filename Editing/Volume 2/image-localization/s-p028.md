### 地獄の魔女を助けた女性

変異に伴う昏睡状態で動けない地獄の魔女を助けた女性は、暴走族だった。昭和さながらの服装で夜な夜なバイクをふかしていた彼女は、グレムリン災害の混乱に乗じて泥棒をしようと入った民家で昏睡状態の女子大生を発見。その姿は幼い頃に病気で死んだ、優しかった姉に似ていた。

姉の死後は両親に甘やかされ、好き放題をして生きてきた。高校をサボり、煙草を吸い、酒を飲んで、無免許運転を繰り返し、斜に構えて世の中を冷笑し、人助けを偽善と蔑んできた。

だが、昏睡する女子大生を前に、足が根を張ったように動かなくなった。罵倒も皮肉も、どうしても口から出てこなかった。窓を割って侵入してくる怪物たちにバットを振り回し、遮二無二戦った。骨が折れても血を吐いても、三日三晩、戦い続けた。

あのとき姉を助ける事はできなかったが、最後に姉に似た一人の女性を助ける事はできた。

### 椋井有琉（むくい　ある）

地獄の魔女が訪ねた小村、世見池村に住む少女。都会の生まれだが、自然派両親は化学物質に汚染されていない大自然の中で娘を育てようと決意。物心ついた時にはもう田舎の村で暮らしていた。両親はグレムリン災害後間もなく、魔物を手なづけようとして喰い殺された。以後は隣家の老夫婦に引き取られる。

村を支配していた池上の死後しばらくして村一番のまじない師（魔法を使う本物）になり、村をよく治めた。成人してから池上の莫大な埋蔵金や帳簿、黄金郷計画などが記されたメモ書きを発見。池上を喰い殺した鬼が本当に人食いのバケモノだったのか疑問を覚える。歳を重ねるにつれ確信を深め、やがて村を襲った悪い鬼の無罪を確信するに至った。だが、本人に謝る機会はついぞ訪れなかった。

### Bigfoot & White Wolf

Bigfoot is a wild Japanese macaque that mutated, while White Wolf is an Akita dog once kept at an old farmhouse. The two monsters were already close before their mutations. Though both changed beyond recognition, they remained lifelong friends.

Bigfoot is omnivorous and possesses a special stomach capable of digesting magical ore, though it has no opportunity to eat any. White Wolf is exceptionally long-lived and grows larger with age. If it survives for a century, it will exceed five meters in total length.

### The Woman Who Helped the Hell Witch

The woman who saved the Hell Witch while she lay helpless in her mutation coma had been a biker-gang delinquent. Dressed like a throwback to the Showa era, she roared around on her motorcycle at night. During the chaos of the Gremlin Disaster, she broke into a house to steal and discovered an unconscious university student who resembled the gentle older sister she had lost to illness as a child.

After her sister’s death, her parents indulged her. She skipped school, smoked, drank, repeatedly drove without a license, sneered at the world, and dismissed helping others as hypocrisy.

Yet before the sleeping young woman, her feet seemed rooted to the floor. No insult or sarcastic remark would come. When monsters smashed through the windows, she swung a bat and fought with everything she had. Even with broken bones and blood in her mouth, she battled for three days and nights.

She could not save her sister then. In the end, however, she saved one woman who looked like her.

### Mukui Aru

Mukui Aru lived in the tiny mountain village visited by the Hell Witch. Born in the city, she was raised in the countryside by nature-loving parents who wanted her far from chemical pollution. Not long after the Gremlin Disaster, they tried to tame a monster and were eaten. An elderly neighboring couple took her in.

After the death of Ikegami, who had ruled the village, Aru eventually became its foremost spellcaster—a genuine magic user—and governed well.

As an adult, she discovered Ikegami’s vast hidden fortune, account books, and notes describing his El Dorado scheme. She began to question whether the oni who killed him had truly been a cannibalistic monster. Her certainty grew with age until she became convinced the “evil oni” who attacked the village had been innocent. She never had the chance to apologize in person.

### Koganei Witch

A witch who lived in Koganei City and wielded demonic magic. She cornered a powerful monster and tormented it at length—mercifully, by her own reckoning—before trying to kill it. Its final counterattack struck her head-on, and she died instantly.

### Itabashi Witch

The witch who governed Itabashi Ward had fluffy white hair like a sheep. In raw strength she ranked near the bottom. Yet she needed no sleep, recovered magic power quickly, and healed injuries rapidly by eating enormous meals.

Restless and unable to sit still, she constantly raced across Tokyo hunting monsters. Her attendance at regular Tokyo Witches’ Council meetings was poor because she could not remain quietly in a chair.

She used household magic and died in the mushroom pandemic.
