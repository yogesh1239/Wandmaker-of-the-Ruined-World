### 進路

卒業後の進路は学科によって異なる。魔法言語学科は例年八割前後が農林水産業、製造業、建設業、公務員など様々な業種に就職する。グレムリン工学科は約半数が魔法杖関連産業に就く。魔物学科は年度によってバラつきが大きい。就職先は農林水産業、警備隊、魔物解体処分業など。変異学科はほぼ全員が進学（研究生）を希望し、研究生になれなかった者も就職先で独自研究を続ける例が多い。戦闘学科はほぼ全員が各区警備隊に就職する。魔法医学科は新設されたばかりで卒業生を出していない。

卒業論文の評価が充分に高ければ、就職ではなく研究生として大学に在籍し続ける道が拓ける。研究生は教授陣のアシスタント業務を行いながら、自分のテーマについての研究を行う。研究生の研究が一つの学問として扱うに足ると評価されれば、准教授に昇格できる。准教授は研究室を持ち、研究のかたわら、学生に講義を行う。教授は学科を統括する主査。通常、その学科について最も卓越する。学科を卒業した後、研究生になり、准教授にならず就職する者も多い。

### Curriculum — continued

—graduating itself is not especially difficult.

Once serious study begins, enrolled students are exempt from all ordinary labor regardless of home district. Crimes are punished under Bunkyo Ward law. Anyone judged to be remaining enrolled deliberately to avoid work is expelled.

When the university opened, one year was enough to teach the limited body of known magic. As knowledge and technology accumulate, however, a single year no longer provides sufficient instruction. A transition to a two-year system is under consideration.

### Career Paths

Career outcomes vary by department.

**Magic Linguistics:** Around 80% enter agriculture, forestry, fisheries, manufacturing, construction, or public service.  
**Gremlin Engineering:** Roughly half enter wand-related industries.  
**Monster Studies:** Outcomes vary widely; common destinations include primary industries, security forces, and monster-dismantling work.  
**Mutation Studies:** Nearly all hope to become researchers. Those not placed may continue independent research while working elsewhere; many join ward security forces.  
**Magic Medicine:** Newly established and has produced no graduates.

Students with highly rated theses may remain at the university as researchers. Research assistants support professors while pursuing their own work. Strong independent scholarship can lead to promotion as an associate professor, with a laboratory and teaching duties. Professors oversee their departments. Many graduates become researchers without seeking faculty rank.

### Student Life

As long as they remain Tokyo Magic University students, minimum food, clothing, and housing are guaranteed. Academic evaluation points can also purchase luxuries and better meals and provide other privileges.

Major groups include the Student Council, the President Fan Club, and the Magic Enthusiasts’ Society. The Magic Enthusiasts are drawing closer to a split between a playful faction that wants to enjoy magic and a serious faction that wants to pursue every possibility of magic.

### Facilities

**Main Building:** Reuses a preserved former university building; unused rooms still contain covered machinery and dust.  
**Cafeteria:** Open 24 hours, including warm late-night meals for researchers. Its miso soup is popular.  
**Campus Store:** Accepts evaluation points and sells magic items.  
**Administrative Office:** Handles examinations, student identification, career support, supplies, and general inquiries.  
**National Diet Library Branch:** Collects and supplies information needed by the university.  
**Katsushika Ward Magic Test Site:** A broad-range testing ground for magic that may damage the surrounding area.

### External Reputation

The university, its students, and its graduates all enjoy excellent reputations. Graduates may receive high-quality magic wands with their diplomas and are actively recruited across the wards.

Admission to Tokyo Magic University is regarded as the first step toward an elite career in the ruined world.
