## Verbatim Japanese

### Title

キノコ病

### Body

正式名称をジンメンタナと名付けられたこのキノコは、生活環の全てにおいて非常に高い拡散力を持つ菌類魔物である。

ジンメンタナは成長段階によって様相が大きく変化する。

まず微細な胞子が魔力を持つ動物（寄生主）に付着すると、発芽し菌糸を体内に広げる。自覚症状は全くなく、発見は困難。菌糸が呼吸器系に到達すると、感染者は呼気を通じて無自覚に胞子を拡散する感染源となる。この時代が菌糸体期。

数年かけて充分に菌糸を広げると、寄生主の頭部に栄養を集中させキノコを生やす（発症。子実体期）。このキノコは鳴き声を上げ、鳴き声の感知範囲にいた感染者を連鎖的に発症させる。

キノコは魔力と栄養を急激に吸い上げ、急成長する。寄生主が死亡すると寄生主から分離独立して歩きはじめ、自分より小さな生き物を攻撃・捕食するようになる（放浪期）。攻撃方法は腕部による殴打。２ｍほどにまで育ち切った成体による強力な殴打は、超越者に膝をつかせるほど。

放浪期のキノコは死亡すると爆散し、溜め込んでいた胞子を周囲にばら撒く。

菌糸体期から子実体期にかけて寄生主が一度でも魔力欠乏失神に陥ると、菌糸が頭部の不可知魔力器官に接続し、劇症化する。劇症化さえしなければ軽症で済み、命に別状はない。

ジンメンタナは魔力を持つ全ての動物に寄生する。

普通の動物は魔法を使わず、魔力欠乏失神を経験しない。

魔物は魔力欠乏を本能的に避けるため、魔力欠乏失神を経験しない。

どちらも必ず軽症であり、生えたキノコも何かに擦り付けて容易に削ぎ落とす。

劇症化により阿鼻叫喚に陥るのは人類だけである。

## Exact English

### Title

Mushroom Disease

### Body

Officially named Zinnmentana, this mushroom is a fungal monster with an extraordinarily high capacity to spread at every stage of its life cycle.

Zinnmentana changes drastically as it matures.

First, microscopic spores attach themselves to an animal with magic power—the host. They germinate and spread mycelium through the host’s body. There are no perceptible symptoms, making detection extremely difficult. Once the mycelium reaches the respiratory system, the infected host unknowingly disperses spores through its breath and becomes a new source of infection. This is the mycelial phase.

After several years of spreading sufficiently, the mycelium concentrates nutrients in the host’s head and sprouts a mushroom. Symptoms then appear: the fruiting-body phase. The mushroom emits a cry that triggers the same onset in every infected host within hearing range, producing a chain reaction.

The mushroom rapidly drains magic power and nutrients, growing at tremendous speed. When the host dies, the mushroom separates from the body, begins walking independently, and attacks and consumes creatures smaller than itself. This is the wandering phase.

It attacks by striking with its arms. A fully grown specimen, approximately two meters tall, can deliver a blow powerful enough to bring even a Transcendent to one knee.

When a mushroom in the wandering phase dies, it explodes and scatters its stored spores throughout the surrounding area.

If the host suffers magic-power-depletion fainting even once between the mycelial and fruiting-body phases, the mycelium connects to an unknown magic organ in the head and the disease becomes fulminant. Without that escalation, the illness remains mild and is not life-threatening.

Zinnmentana parasitizes every animal that possesses magic power.

Ordinary animals do not use magic and never experience magic-power-depletion fainting. Monsters instinctively avoid magic-power depletion and likewise never faint from it. Both therefore develop only mild cases, and any mushroom that sprouts can easily be scraped off against another object.

Only humanity is driven into screaming catastrophe by the fulminant form.
