ピアノが弾ければピアニストと考える者もいれば、ピアノで生計を立ててやっとピアニストと考える者もいる。それと同じで、魔術師もピンキリである。

### グレムリン焼却時の紫電

グレムリン災害以降、人類は放電現象を見た事が無く、全ての電気機器は沈黙し続けている。

### 改定モース硬度

より正確な指標であるビッカース硬度を公的な硬度基準とする案もあったが、分かりやすさが優先された。グレムリン工学科ではビッカース硬度換算でのグレムリン硬度測定も行われている。

### 2型アミュレット

大利が東京魔法大学に送ったアミュレット（1型と呼ばれる）とその設計図を基に、半田教授が改良を行ったもの。魔力回復促進効率6％。

アミュレットに用いる融解再凝固グレムリンは、製造時に融解温度を適切に保つ事で品質が僅かに向上・安定する。融解温度が低すぎればそもそも融けないが、高すぎても品質が落ちるのだ。

### Wizard

The word was originally used for graduates of the Department of Magic Linguistics at Tokyo Magic University, but its meaning shifted as it spread through the public. These days it means roughly “someone who isn’t a Transcendent, carries a magic wand, and casts impressive magic.”

Some people count anyone who can play the piano as a pianist; others say you are only a pianist once you make a living at it. Wizards are the same. They run the whole range, from brilliant to hopeless.

### Violet Lightning from Burning Gremlins

Heat a Gremlin with powerful fire magic and it does not melt. It gives off violet lightning, crumbles to dust, and disappears. That violet lightning looks like electricity, but it has been confirmed to be something else—alike in appearance, different in nature.

Since the Gremlin Disaster, no human being has seen an electrical discharge, and every electrical device has stayed silent.

### Revised Mohs Hardness

With the founding of the Department of Gremlin Engineering at Tokyo Magic University, the Mohs hardness of 11 that Ori had privately assigned to Gremlins and magic stones was adopted as the official hardness standard. (On the old Mohs scale, diamond’s 10 was the ceiling.) The formal name is Revised Mohs hardness, but nobody calls it that. Everyone just says Mohs hardness. Unless the conversation turns historical, that is precise enough.

There was a proposal to make Vickers hardness, a more accurate measure, the official standard, but ease of understanding took priority. The Department of Gremlin Engineering also measures Gremlin hardness in Vickers-converted terms.

### Type 2 Amulet

Professor Handa’s improved version, built from the amulet Ori sent to Tokyo Magic University—now called the Type 1—and its design drawings. Magic-power-recovery acceleration efficiency: 6%.

The melt-recast Gremlin used in an amulet gains a little quality and stability when the melting temperature is held at the right level during manufacture. Too low and it will not melt at all; too high and the quality drops.
