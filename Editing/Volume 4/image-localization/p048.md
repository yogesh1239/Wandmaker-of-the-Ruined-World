# p048 — interior illustration (no text)

- **Source:** `Source/Volume 4/images/p048.jpg` — JPEG, RGB, 1439 x 2048 (portrait)
- **Output:** none. This plate is not localized.

## Finding

**The plate carries no text of any kind.** It is a full-bleed monochrome illustration: a smiling
long-haired girl in a torn, vine-wrapped dress leaning over an unconscious dark-clad figure in the
lower left, set against a pale ground scattered with hand-drawn hearts, ink spatter and trailing
vines. The hearts and vines are drawn ornament, not lettering.

## How that was established

Searched the same three ways as p022, since a blank-looking plate still has to be checked:

1. **Roughness scan.** Mean absolute gradient over every 32 px block. The ten roughest blocks are
   scattered across the eyes, hair edges, the dark cloak boundary and the face at lower left — no
   rectangular run anywhere, which is the signature set type would leave.
2. **Split-range contrast boost.** The light range (200-255) and the dark range (0-70) were each
   stretched to full scale, so pale lettering on light ground and dark lettering inside the black
   cloak would both be forced visible. The light pass resolves the heart doodles and vine work
   sharply and shows no glyphs; the dark pass reduces the page to line art and is empty of text.
3. **Edge inspection.** The margins were read at magnification where a caption or folio would sit.
   Artwork throughout.

## Decision

No localization is performed and nothing is written to `English/Volume 4/localized-images/`.

Consistent with the previous volume, where 73 source images produced 47 localized ones — textless
interior plates are left out of `localized-images/` rather than copied through, and the EPUB build
takes them from the source directory.
