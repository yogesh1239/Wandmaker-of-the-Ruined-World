## Layout decision

**The incantation is the whole problem on this page.** In the source it is three huge vertical brush columns fanning across the left page, each with its magic-language reading set small alongside, and they read right-to-left: `氷河に沈み` → `永久凍土` → `眠れ`.

Keeping one English phrase per source column does not work. Placed in source positions the English would read, left to right, "SLEEP / IN THE PERMAFROST / SINK BENEATH THE GLACIER" — backwards. Mirroring the assignment fixes the order but puts the longest English phrase in the shortest column's space.

**Decision: set the incantation as two stacked horizontal display lines** across the same area the columns occupy, reading top to bottom:

```
SINK BENEATH THE GLACIER.        Torika To-ruka
SLEEP IN THE PERMAFROST!         E Nashieka Vaa-ra
```

This is the same vertical→horizontal conversion applied to every balloon in this volume, extended to display type. It reads correctly, keeps each magic-language form paired with its own clause, and preserves the "huge brush display over ice artwork" look the page is built on. `眠れ` and `永久凍土` merge into one English clause because English says "sleep in the permafrost" as a unit; the `E Nashieka Vaa-ra` reading spans exactly that clause in the source, so the pairing stays honest.

`君よ` / `YOU` stays where it is on the right page as its own display element, with `Zei` set small beside it, because the source treats it as separate from the left page's three columns.

**Label boxes and band** read as one sentence across the spread: `The original's` / `most striking` / `scenes, too—` then `RECREATED` / `IN FULL FORCE!!`. The band halves are swapped relative to the source (`大迫力で` on the left, `再現!!` on the right) so the English reads naturally left to right; the source's order is a Japanese reading-order artifact with no visual significance, since both halves are white knockout on the same black band.

## Editing and refinement record

**Pass A — source and continuity edit: complete.**
- Every region was read at 2.6×–5× magnification from the source before drafting.
- The incantation was **not** freshly translated. `glossary.md` line 243 already fixes it as "You, sink beneath the glacier. Sleep in the permafrost!", and `English/Volume 1/Chapter 4 - Overtechnology.md` line 249 files that exact sentence in prose. The page's English is taken from there, not invented.
- The three magic-language spoken forms were matched to the Volume 1 Chapter 4 footnote, which records `ゼイ`, `トリカ・トールカ`, `エ・ナシエカ・ヴアアラー` for these three clauses.
- `ヴアアラー` → `Vaa-ra` is a **LOCK** in `glossary.md` line 233. Long vowels use a hyphen; macrons are forbidden project-wide and are checked by `normalize_romaji.py`.
- `トールカ` → `To-ruka` follows the same locked convention rather than being romanized ad hoc.
- `魔法杖職人` again carries the ruby `ワンドメーカー`; collapsed to the glossary term **Wand Maker**, consistent with p290-291.
- R2 `試させてもらおうか？` is a challenge, not a request — the volitional `もらおうか` is the speaker deciding, so the English is an invitation with an edge, not a polite ask.

**Pass B — publication-English refinement: complete.**
- R2: literal `Shall I have you let me try it?` is unusable English. Rewritten to `Shall we put it to the test?` — keeps the challenge and the shared-stage feel of `もらおうか`.
- R3: `Let me see the skill of the world's only one Wand Maker!` was a calque with a broken superlative. Rewritten to `The world's one and only Wand Maker — let's see what you can do!`, which keeps `ただ一人の` emphatic and turns `その腕前を！` into the natural English challenge it is.
- L1–L3: `Impressive scenes from the original work too` was flat and mistranslated `印象的` as "impressive" rather than "striking/memorable". Rewritten as the three-box sequence `The original's` / `most striking` / `scenes, too—`.
- L7/L8: `Reproduced with great force!!` was stiff and buried the verb. Rewritten to `RECREATED` / `IN FULL FORCE!!`, and the two halves were swapped so the English reads left to right.
- Incantation: kept verbatim from the glossary. Capitalized for display, and `沈め`/`眠れ` remain imperatives. The only change from the filed prose is the clause split across two display lines, which the source's own clause structure supports.
- All edited wording was written back into both the exact-English table and every quoted occurrence in the production prompt; the two match verbatim.

## Notes and uncertainties

- **No illegible text.** All 11 regions resolved cleanly. Nothing is guessed.
- **The furigana glyph shapes are not fully separable at source resolution** — `ェ` vs `エ` and `ァ` vs `ア` in the magic-language readings cannot be distinguished with certainty at this raster size. This does not affect the output: the English uses the project's already-locked romanizations (`Vaa-ra`, and `Nashieka` from the Volume 1 footnote), so the ambiguity never reaches the page. Recorded rather than guessed away.
- **`眠れ` has no English region of its own.** It is absorbed into `SLEEP IN THE PERMAFROST!`. This is a deliberate layout decision, documented above, not an omission.
- **This is promotional copy for a manga adaptation**, not story prose; only the incantation has a filed-prose authority, and that authority was used.

## Accepted deviations

- **The incantation block is reconstructed artwork.** The source sets that clause as three vertical
  brush columns; the English sets it as two horizontal lines. There is no correspondence between where
  the Japanese ink sits and where the English lands, so the whole block (x0-978, y152-1204) comes from
  the render and the ice texture inside it is rebuilt. This is the same accepted deviation as the p285
  side columns and the p290-291 display line, and it is the direct cost of the layout decision above.
- **The bottom band is taken whole** (x0-1706, y1136-1428) rather than as two lettering rects. Per-column
  dark profiling could not find the band's edges because the white lettering breaks the dark runs, and
  the band is a flat black shape, so reconstructing all of it costs nothing and avoids a seam through
  the middle of the display type.
- **The witch, her face and hands, the beast head, the wand and the whole right page are source pixels.**
  Those are the page's focal art and were the reason for merging back at all.
