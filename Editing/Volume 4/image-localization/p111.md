# p111 — interior illustration (no text)

- **Source:** `Source/Volume 4/images/p111.jpg` — JPEG, RGB, 1439 x 2048 (portrait)
- **Output:** none. This plate is not localized.

## Finding

**The plate carries no text of any kind.** It is a full-bleed monochrome illustration: a close
profile of a long-haired figure in the foreground, head bowed, tears running off the chin, with two
uniformed students at a desk in the pale backlit distance behind.

## How that was established

Searched the same three ways as the other textless plates in this volume:

1. **Roughness scan.** Mean absolute gradient over every 32 px block. The ten roughest blocks all
   fall inside the foreground hair and the eye — no rectangular run, which is the signature set type
   would leave.
2. **Split-range contrast boost.** The light range (200-255) and the dark range (0-70) were each
   stretched to full scale, so pale lettering on the blown-out background and dark lettering inside
   the black hair mass would both be forced visible. Neither pass reveals a glyph; the light pass
   resolves only brushwork and the dark pass reduces the page to bare line art.
3. **Edge inspection.** The margins were read at magnification where a caption or folio would sit.
   Artwork throughout.

## Decision

No localization is performed and nothing is written to `English/Volume 4/localized-images/`.

Consistent with the previous volume, where 73 source images produced 47 localized ones — textless
interior plates are left out of `localized-images/` rather than copied through, and the EPUB build
takes them from the source directory.
