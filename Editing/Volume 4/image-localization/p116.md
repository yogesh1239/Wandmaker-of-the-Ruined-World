# p116 — interior illustration (no text)

- **Source:** `Source/Volume 4/images/p116.jpg` — JPEG, RGB, 1439 x 2048 (portrait)
- **Output:** none. This plate is not localized.

## Finding

**The plate carries no text of any kind.** It is a full-bleed monochrome action illustration, split
by diagonal panel rules into stacked bands: a snarling face at top, a sword-wielding figure at right
resting a blade over one shoulder, and large scaled beasts lunging across the left and lower thirds.

The diagonal white rules that divide the page are drawn panel borders, not text underlines, and no
band carries a caption.

## How that was established

Searched the same three ways as the other textless plates in this volume:

1. **Roughness scan.** Mean absolute gradient over every 32 px block. The ten roughest blocks
   cluster on the upper figure's eye and hair and along the beast's jaw — no rectangular run, which
   is the signature set type would leave.
2. **Split-range contrast boost.** The light range (200-255) and the dark range (0-70) were each
   stretched to full scale, so pale lettering on the bright panels and dark lettering inside the deep
   shadow at top left would both be forced visible. Neither pass reveals a glyph.
3. **Edge inspection.** The margins were read at magnification where a caption or folio would sit.
   Artwork throughout.

## Decision

No localization is performed and nothing is written to `English/Volume 4/localized-images/`.

Consistent with the previous volume, where 73 source images produced 47 localized ones — textless
interior plates are left out of `localized-images/` rather than copied through, and the EPUB build
takes them from the source directory.
