# p271 — interior illustration (no text)

- **Source:** `Source/Volume 4/images/p271.jpg` — JPEG, RGB, 1439 x 2048 (portrait)
- **Output:** none. This plate is not localized.

## Finding

**The plate carries no text of any kind.** It is a full-bleed monochrome illustration seen from a
high angle: a long-haired figure in a dark coat and harness stepping forward, hair streaming to the
left, holding a tall staff with a glowing orb in the raised right hand, against a pale interior with
soft geometric light shapes.

## How that was established

Searched four ways, the extra midtone pass included because the figure and background sit largely in
the greys where the standard windows are least sensitive:

1. **Roughness scan.** Mean absolute gradient over every 32 px block. The ten roughest blocks all
   fall on the face, the fringe and the harness buckles — no rectangular run, which is the signature
   set type would leave.
2. **Split-range contrast boost, light and dark.** The light range (200-255) and the dark range
   (0-70) were each stretched to full scale. Neither reveals a glyph.
3. **Midtone stretch (130-200).** Resolves the background light shapes, the staff and the costume
   detail at high contrast, and is likewise empty of text.
4. **Targeted crop.** The pale area to the right of the orb showed faint structure on the midtone
   stretch, so it was cropped and enlarged 4x on its own stretch. It resolves as staff and orb
   highlights over background blur — no glyphs. Checking rather than assuming, since faint marks in
   open space are exactly where a small credit line would sit.
5. **Edge inspection.** The margins were read at magnification where a caption or folio would sit.
   Artwork throughout.

## Decision

No localization is performed and nothing is written to `English/Volume 4/localized-images/`.

Consistent with the previous volume, where 73 source images produced 47 localized ones — textless
interior plates are left out of `localized-images/` rather than copied through, and the EPUB build
takes them from the source directory.
