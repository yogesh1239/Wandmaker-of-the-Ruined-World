## Localization decision — no render, original retained

Two independent reasons, either sufficient on its own:

1. **Nothing to translate.** The page carries no Japanese. `BOOK☆WALKER` is already Latin, and it
   is the store's own spelling, including the star.
2. **It is a trademark.** `novel.config.md:122` sets the policy as *"retain the Japanese cover and
   colophon as-is; retain original publisher/legal matter in Japanese."* A retailer's registered
   logomark is exactly that class of matter. Redrawing it would misrepresent a third party's mark,
   and the ten-colour bar/letter correspondence is part of the mark's design, not typesetting.

Filed precedent: Volume 3 localized 47 images and left its retailer and publisher marks
unrendered. The `MEDIA FACTORY` mark on `allcover-001` is retained verbatim on the same grounds.
