## Terminology decisions

| Japanese | English | Authority |
|-|-|-|
| 崩壊世界の魔法杖職人 | Wand Maker of the Ruined World | `novel.config.md` series title |
| 大利賢師 | Ori Kenshi | `glossary.md` — family name first, long vowel unmarked |
| 青の魔女 | Blue Witch | `glossary.md` |
| 魔法杖 | magic wand | `glossary.md` |
| 反社会勢力 | organized crime | new; sibling of 指定暴力団 "designated crime syndicate" |
| モードチェンジ | mode-change | loanword, kept as the compound modifier |
| トップレアアイテム | top-rare item | loanword; game-rarity register preserved |
| アンロック | unlocks | loanword — deserves the literal "unlock" |
| 解放 | released | distinct word from アンロック in the source; kept distinct in English |

**On ② vs ④.** The source uses two different words for the same idea — the loanword `アンロック` in
② and the native `解放` in ④. English keeps them distinct for the same reason: "unlocks" for the
loanword, "released" for `解放`. Collapsing both to "unlocked" would flatten a deliberate variation.

**On the proverb.** `井の中の蛙大海を知らず` is a well-known Japanese saying. It is rendered as a
plain English clause rather than left in romaji or footnoted, because this is display copy on a
promotional page — a footnote marker would be wrong here, and the image cannot carry one.

**On 予定 twice.** `予定されています` in the left column becomes "is scheduled to"; the small `予定`
in the bottom band becomes a trailing "(scheduled)". Both preserve the hedge; neither is dropped.

## Layout decision

Two structural changes, both forced by the switch to English:

1. **The vertical columns become horizontal blocks.** The right column's five vertical bars and the
   left column's four become one horizontal block each, keeping their page positions and their
   white-on-black bar treatment. Line counts change (5→3 and 4→2) because English lines hold more.
2. **The heading pairs differently.** The source layers a Japanese heading over a decorative English
   `Next episode` wordmark. Repeating "Next episode" in the crisp white heading would read as a
   stutter, so the heading becomes `NEXT VOLUME` and the ghosted wordmark stays untouched. The page
   keeps its two-layer look and reads as though it were designed in English.

The circled numerals ①②③④ are kept — they are language-neutral and carry the list structure.

**Pass B — publication-English refinement: complete.**

Reread as finished promotional copy rather than as a translation.

- The four item headings were made grammatically parallel — noun phrase plus past participle
  ("developed / acquired / obtained / released") — which the Japanese achieves with its
  `の開発 / の習得 / の獲得 / 解放` pattern and which a literal rendering would have lost.
- `実装されます` ("is implemented") became "adds ... to", because "a mode-change function is
  implemented in the magic wand" is engineering-speak, not cover copy.
- `手がかりとなる` was turned round into "the clue that unravels", so the English leads with the item
  and lands on the payoff, as the Japanese sentence does.
- `油断は禁物です` became "there is no room for complacency" rather than the literal "carelessness is
  forbidden".
- The proverb was rendered as a natural English clause and folded into the same sentence with a dash,
  matching the source's conversational aside.
- `お待ち下さい` became "look forward to" rather than the literal "please wait" — English promotional
  copy does not tell a reader to wait.
- Register held to the source's ceiling throughout: this is publisher preview copy in a light,
  game-patch-notes voice, and the English keeps that voice without elevating it.

## Render record

**Accepted.** `English/Volume 4/localized-images/p285.jpg` — real JPEG, RGB, 1439 x 2048, quality 95,
4:4:4, 1,066,769 bytes. Source SHA-256 `3cc2a1a0285f37b20257a113f1a46479c8c0e8ae6ce5964f80e8fbb4203ec820`
verified unchanged after the run.

**Render.** One `imagegen` edit-mode call via Codex headless (`gpt-5.6-sol`, medium reasoning). It
returned a 1051 x 1497 PNG which was scaled to the 1439 x 2048 canvas, so the render is softer than
the source (mean-abs-gradient 14.63 vs 17.13). The merge-back removes that cost everywhere except
inside the text regions.

**Text QA — every string read back at native resolution, not taken on trust.** All six blocks were
cropped and magnified individually. Every string matches the spec exactly: both side columns are
horizontal and left-to-right, all four circled numerals survived as circled forms, both em dashes
survived as em dashes, and no Japanese remains anywhere. No typo was found — worth stating plainly,
because three earlier pages in this volume were reported correct by the renderer while containing
defects, so the read-back is done here regardless of what the renderer claims.

**Merge-back.** Nine rects, `merge_p285.py`, GROW=8 FEATHER=6. Deliberately small, because the text
sits on opaque boxes with hard edges — there is no soft gutter to blend across and a wide dilation
would only drag drifted artwork in. Final check: **0 changed pixels outside any merge rect**, so all
untouched artwork is byte-identical to the source; a clean strip at x1400-1439 measures MAD 0.66,
which is JPEG re-encode noise alone.

### Three corrections made during the merge

1. **Colour offsets abandoned.** The usual ring-sampling trick assumes the ring shows the same
   picture in both images, so any difference is a tone shift. Here `imagegen` redrew the artwork, so
   the ring difference is *drift*, not tone — sampling it produced offsets up to 36 levels, which
   would have greyed out boxes that must stay pure white or pure black. Offsets set to zero; the
   render's global tone already matches (mean 126.35 vs source 127.07).
2. **Item 1's rect started 34px too low**, because the box extents were measured in a window
   beginning at y885 while the source's box actually starts at y843. Item 1's Japanese heading
   survived on the page as a result. Fixed by measuring the true source runs (843-987, 1024-1165,
   1208-1304, 1339-1484) and making the four item rects **contiguous**, so no gap of that kind can
   reopen.
3. **Item 3's rect was 30px too narrow.** Its source box reaches x1365, and the rect stopped at
   x1335, leaving a white tail of the source box standing beside the figure. Column statistics could
   not find the true edge because each item is two stacked sub-boxes of different widths, so no
   single column is white down the whole band; the edges were read off representative rows instead
   (776, 834, 1365, 1274).

All three are the same underlying error class — a measurement window clipped to less than the thing
being measured — which also cost two passes on p011. The lesson recorded there holds: **measure with
a window wider than the feature, then verify coverage, before trusting any rect.**

## Accepted deviations

- **The side columns carry reconstructed artwork.** This is unavoidable, not a defect. The source
  set those blocks vertically; the English sets them horizontally. Wherever a Japanese bar stood and
  no English line lands, the render's rebuilt art has to fill in — taking source pixels there would
  bring the Japanese back with them. The rebuilt areas are the pale upper-right sky and the region
  around the hooded figure's cloak; both blend acceptably.
- **Straight quotation marks** in item 4's `"Blue Witch"` rather than typographic curly quotes. This
  matches the spec as written. Typographic quotes would be marginally more polished; noted rather
  than re-rendered, since a full re-render for one glyph pair is not worth the artwork risk.
- **The ghosted `Next episode` wordmark comes through the merge**, since the whole top band is one
  rect. It was checked against the source first and matches at MAD 2.95, so nothing was lost.
