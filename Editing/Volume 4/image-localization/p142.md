# p142 — interior illustration (no text)

- **Source:** `Source/Volume 4/images/p142.jpg` — JPEG, RGB, 1439 x 2048 (portrait)
- **Output:** none. This plate is not localized.

## Finding

**The plate carries no text of any kind.** It is a full-bleed monochrome illustration: a
bespectacled long-haired girl in the right foreground looking down, two more figures behind her with
hands joined at centre-left, and a pair of clasped hands in the lower foreground, all in a soft
backlit interior.

## The one glyph-bearing object, and why it stays

The pocket watch hanging at the foreground girl's chest (around x920-1050, y1090-1220) has a legible
dial. Enlarged four times, the dial is marked in **Roman numerals** — no kana, no kanji, no Latin
wordmark. Roman numerals read identically in an English edition, so there is nothing to translate and
no reason to touch the watch. It stays exactly as drawn.

## How that was established

Searched the same three ways as the other textless plates in this volume, plus a targeted crop:

1. **Roughness scan.** Mean absolute gradient over every 32 px block. The ten roughest blocks fall
   on the two visible faces, the hair clips and the clasped hands — no rectangular run, which is the
   signature set type would leave.
2. **Split-range contrast boost.** The light range (200-255) and the dark range (0-70) were each
   stretched to full scale, so pale lettering on the blown-out background and dark lettering inside
   the shadowed upper region would both be forced visible. Neither pass reveals a glyph.
3. **Targeted crop.** The pocket watch was cropped and enlarged 4x specifically because a dial is the
   one place on this page a reader would expect characters. Roman numerals, as above.
4. **Edge inspection.** The margins were read at magnification where a caption or folio would sit.
   Artwork throughout.

## Decision

No localization is performed and nothing is written to `English/Volume 4/localized-images/`.

Consistent with the previous volume, where 73 source images produced 47 localized ones — textless
interior plates are left out of `localized-images/` rather than copied through, and the EPUB build
takes them from the source directory.
