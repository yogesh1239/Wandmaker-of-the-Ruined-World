# p196-197 — interior double-page spread (no text)

- **Source:** `Source/Volume 4/images/p196-197.jpg` — JPEG, RGB, **2048 x 1456 (landscape)**
- **Output:** none. This spread is not localized.

Note the orientation: this is the volume's one landscape interior file, a double-page spread, unlike
every other interior plate in the set.

## Finding

**The spread carries no text of any kind.** It is monochrome throughout: a burst of ink spatter and
debris at the far left, a scarred face in a tall narrow band beside it, a small bordered inset panel
at upper right showing a figure lying on the ground between two standing pairs of legs, and a large
close-up at lower right of a bespectacled figure lying against rubble.

The white rule bordering the inset panel at upper right is a drawn panel frame, not a caption box —
its interior is artwork edge to edge.

## How that was established

Searched four ways. The extra pass is because this spread sits mostly in the midtones, where the
usual light and dark stretches are least sensitive:

1. **Roughness scan.** Mean absolute gradient over every 32 px block. The ten roughest blocks all
   fall inside the lower-right close-up — the glasses frame, the hair and the eye — with no
   rectangular run, which is the signature set type would leave.
2. **Split-range contrast boost, light and dark.** The light range (200-255) and the dark range
   (0-70) were each stretched to full scale. Neither reveals a glyph.
3. **Midtone stretch (110-170).** Added specifically for this spread, since a caption laid over the
   grey field would have low contrast against both of the above windows. It resolves the spatter,
   the inset panel's contents and the rubble crisply, and is likewise empty of text.
4. **Edge inspection.** The margins and the inset panel's border were read at magnification where a
   caption or folio would sit. Artwork throughout.

## Decision

No localization is performed and nothing is written to `English/Volume 4/localized-images/`.

Consistent with the previous volume, where 73 source images produced 47 localized ones — textless
interior plates are left out of `localized-images/` rather than copied through, and the EPUB build
takes them from the source directory.

One build note carried forward: because this file is landscape and the rest of the volume is
portrait, the spread still needs whatever spine and page-fit handling the build spec gives spreads,
even though no localization work happens here.
