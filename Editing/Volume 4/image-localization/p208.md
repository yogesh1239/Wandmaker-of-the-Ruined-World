# p208 — interior illustration (no text)

- **Source:** `Source/Volume 4/images/p208.jpg` — JPEG, RGB, 1439 x 2048 (portrait)
- **Output:** none. This plate is not localized.

## Finding

**The plate carries no text of any kind.** It is a full-bleed monochrome illustration seen from a
low angle: a long-haired figure viewed from behind, one arm raised to hold a staff aloft against a
bright sky, standing in tall grass with a building and a dark swirling mass in the distance.

The small dark rectangles scattered near the building at lower right are its windows, drawn in
perspective — not lettering. They resolve as window openings on the midtone stretch.

## How that was established

Searched four ways, the extra midtone pass included because most of this page sits in a narrow
bright band where the standard light window has little separation:

1. **Roughness scan.** Mean absolute gradient over every 32 px block. The ten roughest blocks all
   fall on the figure's raised arm, hair and jacket — no rectangular run, which is the signature set
   type would leave.
2. **Split-range contrast boost, light and dark.** The light range (200-255) and the dark range
   (0-70) were each stretched to full scale. Neither reveals a glyph; the dark pass is nearly empty,
   confirming the page holds almost no true black.
3. **Midtone stretch (140-210).** Resolves the sky, the distant building and the grass at high
   contrast, and is likewise empty of text.
4. **Edge inspection.** The margins were read at magnification where a caption or folio would sit.
   Artwork throughout.

## Decision

No localization is performed and nothing is written to `English/Volume 4/localized-images/`.

Consistent with the previous volume, where 73 source images produced 47 localized ones — textless
interior plates are left out of `localized-images/` rather than copied through, and the EPUB build
takes them from the source directory.
