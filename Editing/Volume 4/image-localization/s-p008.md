- **`オコジョ` is `stoat`, not `ermine`.** The glossary already locks it through

  earlier draft used `blew a fuse`; see the Pass C record for why that had to go.
- **`ＰＴＡ` stays `PTA`.** Full-width Latin in the source; set as ordinary English capitals.
- **`くるぶしソックス` → `ankle socks`**, the ordinary English name for the garment.
- **The furigana is dropped**, as on every profile page so far.

off that feat, came down with burnout syndrome. She had met the students' expectations magnificently
and won their acclaim, but she had taken on too much: she would not cut a single corner on her
studies or her club activities while carrying out her council duties, and in the end she hit her

¶3 `Because she has been through that, she worries about whether the Ohinata she so admires is taking

### Pass C record

Sent to Codex headless (`gpt-5.6-sol`, medium reasoning, text only, imagegen explicitly forbidden):
the verbatim Japanese by region, the proposed English, and the locked constraints. Regions 1, 2, 3
and 6 came back clean.

**Taken — the important one:**

- **`パンク` → `blew a fuse` was wrong.** In English, *blow a fuse* means to lose one's temper. The
  Japanese says nothing about anger; `パンク` is reaching overload and breaking down. The idiom
  would have handed her an angry outburst the source never gives her. Corrected to
  `hit her breaking point` / `hits her breaking point`, which keeps the deliberate repetition intact.

**Taken — meaning corrections:**

- **`一般成人女性。` mis-scoped.** `An ordinary adult woman` reads as a description of Nanase
  herself; it is a rating of her physical ability *against* that of an ordinary adult woman. →
  `That of an average adult woman.`
- **`大切に守るべき幼い子供` — invented intensifier.** I had `a small child to be protected at all
  costs`; `at all costs` is nowhere in the source, and `大切` — the child being *precious to her* —
  was lost. → `a precious young child she has to protect`.
- **`概ね…一番大きい` overstated.** `概ね` is "broadly, generally"; my `by far` is an emphasis the
  Japanese does not make. → `generally the strongest`.
- **`歴任していた` overstated.** `one term after another` claims repeated terms the source does not.
- **`話は…まで広がり` mis-toned.** `escalated` imports worsening conflict; `広がり` is simply that
  the matter spread that far. → `went as far as`.
- **`喝采された` understated.** `been applauded for it` is weaker than winning acclaim.
- **`気にかけている` flattened.** `keeps an eye on whether` is a surveillance idiom; she is
  *concerned about* it. → `worries about whether`.
- **`頑張りたい気持ち` narrowed.** Not an `urge to push hard` but the wish to give something your
  all. Likewise `心置きなく` is *without reservation or worry*, which my `with a clear mind` missed.
- **`Ａクラスを下回る` translationese.** `below the A class` → `an Intelligence grade below A`.
- **`指導教授`** → `academic advisor`, the ordinary English title.
- The two clipped opening statements were joined with `and` in my draft; the source sets them as two
  separate fragments and the English now does too.

**Overridden:**

- The reviewer called `the Ohinata she so admires` unnatural, then reproduced it verbatim in its own
  corrected block. No alternative was actually offered, so mine stands.
- `力を抜くのが苦手` — both `is no good at easing off` and the reviewer's `does not know how to ease
  up` are fine; took the reviewer's as marginally more idiomatic, not as a correction of an error.
