## Terminology conflict resolved — `作品設定`

The glossary carries **two contradictory rows** for this term:

- `glossary.md:436` — `作品設定` → `Setting Notes`, marked *booklet section heading*
- `glossary.md:493` — `作品設定` → `Story Settings`, marked *booklet running head for the
  setting-notes pages*, and explicitly listing `Setting Notes` as a form to avoid

They cannot both stand: the TOC entry and the running head name the same section, so different
English for each would make the booklet contradict itself between page 3 and page 19.

Across volumes the term has in fact shipped three ways — Volume 2's TOC used `Setting Notes`,
Volume 3's TOC used `Series Background`, and both volumes' setting pages used `Story Settings`.

Volume 4 takes **`Story Settings`**, and `glossary.md:436` should be retired into `:493`. Reasons:
it is the form the actual section pages carry in two volumes, so it is what a reader meets on the
page rather than only in a list; `:493` is the more specific row and names the avoided alternative,
which reads as the later and more considered decision; and it keeps this volume internally
consistent, which matters more than matching an earlier volume's TOC that already disagrees with
its own section pages.

## Terminology decisions — the new headings

- **`４巻時点での年表` — Timeline as of Volume 4.** Follows the locked pattern at `glossary.md:437`
  and `:438`. Volume 3's shipped image reads `Timeline Through Volume 3`; the glossary form wins.
- **`魔法道具紹介` — Magic Item Profiles.** Parallel to `キャラクター紹介` → `Character Profiles`;
  the same `紹介` in the same list should not become `Introductions` in one line and `Profiles` in
  another.
- **`４巻の各話解説` — Volume 4 Chapter Commentary.** Pattern from `glossary.md:454`.
- **`東京警備隊対抗戦` — Tokyo Guard Tournament.** `警備隊` takes `Guard`, consistent with
  `glossary.md:658` (`港区自治警備隊` → `Minato Ward Civilian Guard`). Volume 4's filed prose uses
  the lowercase common noun `security guards` for the same word where it is not a unit name; here
  it is a unit name and takes capitals. `対抗戦` is a contest between opposing teams, which as an
  English booklet heading is a tournament.
- **`日本の生存者コミュニティ` — Survivor Communities of Japan.** `survivor community` is the
  standing phrase in Volume 4's filed prose. The plural is right: the section surveys several,
  including the two already in the glossary.
- **`東京魔法大学論文アーカイブ` — Tokyo Magic University Paper Archive.** `論文` here is an
  academic paper, not a degree thesis, so `Paper Archive` rather than `Thesis Archive`.
- **`奥多摩防衛担当大臣業務指令書` — Okutama Minister of Defense: Operational Directive.**
  `防衛担当大臣` is a minister *in charge of* defense; `業務指令書` is a written directive issued in
  the course of duty. The colon carries the source's two-line split — office above, document below
  — which is exactly how the Japanese is set.
- **`奥付` — Colophon.**

### Finishing — `finish_page.py`

This page carries no artwork, so the render is taken whole rather than rect by rect. The finisher
was generalized here from `finish_h2.py`, because every remaining text-only booklet page needs the
same two corrections:

1. **Resample** 1051 × 1496 → 1440 × 2048, LANCZOS.
2. **Re-ink the navy.** The render's type came back at (5.3, 24.4, 80.6) against the source's
   (1.3, 46.2, 98.8) — deeper and greener, the same shift `s-h2` showed. Page by page it would pass
   unnoticed; across a forty-page booklet, navy that drifts from page to page reads as a printing
   fault. Each page is pinned to its own source navy, measured from the same region in both images.

   Gated on blue-dominance (`B − R > 30`), which cannot reach black body text or the paper.
   Coverage runs continuously from 0 at paper to 1 at the render's own densest stroke, so
   antialiased edges land partway between navy and paper instead of banding.
