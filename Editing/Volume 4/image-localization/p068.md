# p068 — interior illustration (no text)

- **Source:** `Source/Volume 4/images/p068.jpg` — JPEG, RGB, 1439 x 2048 (portrait)
- **Output:** none. This plate is not localized.

## Finding

**The plate carries no text of any kind.** It is a full-bleed monochrome illustration: three large
geckos rearing up in the foreground, one of them reaching a clawed hand toward a thin taut line, with
a dark-haired boy in a black tee at lower right shading his eyes and grinning, against speed-lines
and a soft bokeh background.

## Two marks that are drawn symbols, not text

- **The small heart** floating at roughly (620, 300) is an emote drawn into the art.
- **The four tapered emphasis strokes** at roughly (690-880, 610-760) are manga impact marks.

Both are language-neutral pictorial marks with no reading, so there is nothing to translate and no
reason to touch them. They stay exactly as drawn.

## How that was established

Searched the same three ways as the other textless plates in this volume:

1. **Roughness scan.** Mean absolute gradient over every 32 px block. The ten roughest blocks all
   fall on the boy's hair and face and on the foliage at lower left — no rectangular run, which is
   the signature set type would leave.
2. **Split-range contrast boost.** The light range (200-255) and the dark range (0-70) were each
   stretched to full scale, so pale lettering on the bright background and dark lettering inside the
   black shirt and shadowed foliage would both be forced visible. The light pass resolves the speed
   lines, spatter and scale texture crisply and shows no glyphs; the dark pass reduces the page to
   bare line art and is empty of text.
3. **Edge inspection.** The margins were read at magnification where a caption or folio would sit.
   Artwork throughout.

## Decision

No localization is performed and nothing is written to `English/Volume 4/localized-images/`.

Consistent with the previous volume, where 73 source images produced 47 localized ones — textless
interior plates are left out of `localized-images/` rather than copied through, and the EPUB build
takes them from the source directory.
