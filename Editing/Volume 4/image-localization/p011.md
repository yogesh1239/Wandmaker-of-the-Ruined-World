## Layout decision

- **Drop every kana reading**, upper dossiers and lower list alike, exactly as on p010 and on
  Volume 3's p011. In the lower list the reading sits beside the name on the same line, so removing
  it leaves the name alone above its rule.
- **Keep the two block shapes distinct.** Upper dossiers are name / rule / description beside a
  portrait; lower entries are name / rule / single description line. They are different visual
  classes and the page's hierarchy depends on the contrast.
- **Keep the group dossier indented** to `x231`, where the source sets it — it is deliberately offset
  from the lower list, and Volume 3's page keeps that offset too.
- **Nothing else moves.** The nine portraits, the fire-salamander illustration, the hand-drawn rules,
  column x-positions, entry pitch and margins all stay.

Dossier 6 carries the longest description on the page and 4 the shortest; body size and leading stay
uniform throughout, with longer entries taking a third line rather than being set smaller.

## Editing and refinement record

**Pass A — source and continuity edit: complete.**

- All nineteen Japanese blocks were read back at magnification from native-resolution crops.
- The nine dossiers shared with Volume 3's p011 were transcribed from the **shipped** Volume 3 image
  and carried over unchanged.
- The ten new entries were checked against `glossary.md`, `character-reference.md` and the filed
  Volume 4 prose. `Nanase Nanami`, `Professor Onigawara`, `Arataki Group`, `centaur witch`,
  `university president` and `President Fan Club` are all already established in the filed chapters.
- `荒瀧虎次郎` is **Arataki Kojiro**, the third-generation boss — deliberately not `Arataki Kotaro`,
  the second-generation boss, whom `character-reference.md:382` records as Murakumo's victim. The two
  names differ by one character in the source and are separate people.
- `和泉渚` appears in no filed prose; the Foresight Mage's unnamed "secretary" recurs throughout
  Volume 4, and this page is where she is named.
- `領主魔法` occurs nowhere in either volume's source text — this dossier is its only appearance.
- Name order is family-first throughout; no honorific appears on this page.

**Pass B — publication-English refinement: complete.**

Read as finished copy. `半人半馬《ケンタウロス》の魔女` was set as `A centaur witch`, not the literal
`a half-human, half-horse «centaur» witch`, because the filed prose already collapses the gloss the
same way and the doubled construction is a Japanese typographic habit, not information. `引き籠って
いる` became `Holed up in a manga café` rather than the flat `shut in`, matching the wry tone the
Volume 3 prose uses for the same character. `荒瀧組若頭の魔法使い` became `A mage who serves as the
Arataki Group's Young Boss`, preserving the source's "a mage who is X" shape while reading as English.

Living characters take present tense for current traits; the page has no deceased entries.

The exact-English tables and every quoted string in the production prompt are identical verbatim.

## Notes and uncertainties

- **One source defect.** Dossier 8, Kiwada Yoko, ends `荒瀧虎次郎（組長）の愛人` with **no closing
  `。`** — every other description on the page is punctuated. This is a typesetting slip in the
  original. The English supplies the missing period, since reproducing a source typo in an English
  edition serves no reader.
- Nineteen dossiers is more verbatim text than p010's seventeen, and the page has two different block
  shapes plus a staggered lower list. The likeliest failures are a dropped entry, a slot swap between
  the two lower columns, and the renderer "helpfully" aligning the indented group dossier to the
  lower list.
- The renderer will regenerate the whole canvas, so its output is merged back over the source. Merge
  rectangles: `285,100-680,1075` · `872,100-1265,895` · `225,1185-705,1300` · `108,1340-700,1900` ·
  `718,1560-1200,1900`. Alpha must be hard-zeroed over the art at `105,100-287,1098` (left
  portraits), `692,100-874,896` (right portraits) and `674,995-1228,1381` (fire salamanders),
  because the 4 px and 8 px gutters are far narrower than the merge dilation.
- Background is pure 255, as on p010, so paper inside each merged rectangle must be measured against
  the untouched margins and snapped flat if the re-encode leaves a dither.

### Method — one render, merged back with four art guards

`imagegen` was driven through Codex headless, two calls. As on every plate in this volume the model
regenerated the whole page, so its work was merged back over the source.

The merge rectangles in the spec above were **wrong on first attempt** and had to be re-derived; the
accepted geometry is:

| Region | Rectangle |
|-|-|
| upper-left text | `285,100-700,1100` |
| upper-right text | `872,100-1265,900` |
| group dossier | `215,1195-703,1305` |
| lower-left list | `100,1330-710,1950` |
| lower-right list | `700,1400-1240,1950` |

with alpha hard-zeroed over four art guards: `105,100-287,1098` (left portraits),
`692,100-874,896` (right portraits), and the salamanders as three stacked rectangles —
`680,950-1235,1070`, `660,1070-1235,1213` and `703,1213-1235,1395`.

The salamander needs three rectangles rather than one because its outline interleaves with type in
two places. Its foot reaches x669 by y1180, but the group dossier's name runs to x701 on the rows
just below, and the left column's own text runs to x664 on the rows just above — so a single
left edge would either shield type or expose art. The guard steps in x at y1070 and again at y1213.

### Two geometry errors caught before acceptance

1. **The lower-right list was measured with a clipped window.** Its bounding box was taken with a
   window starting at y1550, so the reported top of y1572 was the window's edge, not the list's. The
   list actually starts at **y1422** — the entire Hakata Denjiro entry sat outside the merge
   rectangle, and its Japanese survived under the English. Visible immediately at 4x contrast boost.
2. **The salamander bounding box was clipped the same way**, taken from a window starting at y1000
   and so missing the flame tips that reach y963. The first repair then over-corrected, setting the
   guard's left edge at x660 and shielding the tail of the Pebble Witch's Japanese description, which
   runs to x664 — a second, smaller survival at the same boundary.

Both were found by a coverage test rather than by eye: every source ink pixel lying outside the art
guards was checked for membership in some merge rectangle, which reported the uncovered regions
directly. That test is the reliable check here and should precede the visual pass on any page whose
text and artwork interleave.
