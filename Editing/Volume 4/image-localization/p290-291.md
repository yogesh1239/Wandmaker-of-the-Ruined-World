## Layout decision

The whole page is vertical Japanese; all English is set **horizontal, upright, left-to-right**. Consequences:

- **Balloons (R2, R5–R9, R20–R22, R25, R26)** — vertical bars become horizontal blocks of 2–3 short lines, centred in the balloon. The balloons are wider than one horizontal line needs, so most gain a little air; that is correct for an English edition and must not be filled with enlarged type.
- **R27 `青の魔女、推参!!`** — the source sets this along a descending left-to-right diagonal already (青 lower-left through 推参!! at right), so the English follows the same diagonal axis and the same white knockout treatment. This is the one region whose source direction and English direction agree.
- **R3/R4 and R10/R11** — two-tier headlines: a thinner setup line above, a heavy brush payoff line below. Keep the tier contrast; the payoff line stays visibly larger and heavier.
- **R12–R17 narration boxes** — six white caption boxes laid across the process strip in right-to-left order. In English they read **R12 → R17 left-to-right is wrong**; the boxes stay in their source positions and the English reads them in the source's spatial order, which for a Western reader flows right-to-left across the strip. Accepted: the strip is a manga excerpt and preserves manga panel order. Do not reflow or renumber the boxes.
- **Ruby** — every ruby annotation on this page is a pronunciation gloss for Japanese readers. All of it is dropped in English except where the ruby carries a distinct term (R7 ワンドメーカー), which is absorbed into the base rendering.

## Notes and uncertainties

- **No illegible text.** All 26 regions resolved cleanly at magnification. Nothing on this page is guessed.
- **Ruby is dropped throughout.** Every ruby on the page is a reading gloss except R7's `ワンドメーカー`, which is absorbed into the glossary term. No English ruby is set.
- **The narration boxes keep manga order.** R12–R17 read right-to-left across the strip because they are an excerpt from a right-to-left comic, and the drawn panels they caption cannot be reordered. Reflowing the boxes left-to-right would desynchronize caption and art.
- **This is a promotional page, not story prose.** There is no filed English chapter to draw wording from; the glossary and character voices are the only authorities, and both were applied.
- **Balloon fit is the main render risk.** 26 regions of small type is the densest text load in the volume, and `imagegen` garbles small type more often than large. Expect correction renders.

### Two of Codex's four reported defects did not apply

Codex reported render A as having unlocalized Japanese SFX and regenerated artwork. Both were checked
and neither is a defect for this pipeline:

- The "unlocalized Japanese SFX" in the second panel strip are the punctuation marks `!?`, `!!` and
  `!!!`. They are language-neutral, carry no Japanese text, and are correctly left untouched. They were
  never listed as regions.
- The regenerated artwork is expected. `imagegen` redraws the whole canvas; the merge-back exists
  precisely to discard that and keep the illustrator's pixels everywhere outside the text rects.

The one real defect was the dropped caption box, which render B fixed.

### How the merge rects were measured

The first rect table was estimated off a half-scale overview and was wrong on about ten of twenty-five
rects — R22 was ~100px off in x, R25 stopped at y208 when its text runs to y306, R26 stopped at y1172
when its balloon runs to y1378. Those three alone left Japanese standing on the merged page. Three
automatic methods were tried and each failed for a real reason:

1. **Ink thresholding** — the artwork is as dark as the type, so all seventeen probes returned the
   window edge rather than the text. A clipping guard made this loud instead of silent.
2. **Difference thresholding** — the render regenerates screentone wholesale, so redrawn art differs as
   much as text (art p95 122-170 vs text p95 179-233). No usable separation.
3. **Plain flood fill of white interiors** — leaked through anti-aliased balloon outlines.

Method 3 was then fixed by **eroding the white mask by 2px before filling**, which seals hairline gaps
in thin outlines. That produced measured interiors for every balloon and caption box. The display
headlines and SFX sit on artwork and have no container, so those were read off labelled coordinate
grids at 2-4x magnification. Every rect in the merge script records which method produced it.

### The measurement lesson this page added

A rect must cover the **union of the source Japanese and the rendered English**, not just the source
glyphs. The render routinely sets a line wider, higher or further left than the Japanese bar it
replaces — `Only in the manga adaptation—` starts 50px above and 50px left of its Japanese, and
`all of it in full comic form!!` runs 44px past its right edge. Sizing rects to the Japanese alone
clipped six regions (R3, R4, R7, R10, R11, R89) and two more on a later pass (R15, R24). This is a
distinct failure from the clipped-measurement-window class and is worth carrying to later pages.
