# p022 — interior illustration (no text)

- **Source:** `Source/Volume 4/images/p022.jpg` — JPEG, RGB, 1439 x 2048 (portrait)
- **Output:** none. This plate is not localized.

## Finding

**The plate carries no text of any kind.** It is a full-bleed monochrome illustration: a large
close-up face across the upper half, a woman holding a child at the left, a weeping face at lower
left, and two crying figures at lower right behind a rain-streaked pane.

## How that was established

Not by glance — the page was searched three ways:

1. **Roughness scan.** Mean absolute gradient over every 32 px block. Set type shows up as compact
   high-gradient blocks in rectangular runs; the eight roughest blocks here all fall on hair, faces
   and the crying detail, with no rectangular clustering anywhere.
2. **Split-range contrast boost.** The light range (200-255) and the dark range (0-70) were each
   stretched to full scale separately, so that pale lettering on light ground and dark lettering in
   shadow would both be forced visible. Neither reveals any glyph.
3. **Edge inspection.** The top and bottom margins and the busiest interior region were read at
   magnification, where a caption, credit or page folio would sit. All artwork.

## Decision

No localization is performed and nothing is written to `English/Volume 4/localized-images/`.

This follows the convention already set by the previous volume, where 73 source images produced 47
localized ones — textless interior plates are left out of `localized-images/` entirely rather than
copied through, and the EPUB build takes them from the source directory.
