## Terminology decisions

| Japanese | English | Source |
|-|-|-|
| `蜘蛛の魔女` | `Spider Witch` | glossary line 27 |
| `魔力` | `Magic Power` (stat label) | established on `s-p004` |
| `魔力コントロール` | `magic-power control` | glossary line 108 |
| `戦闘センス` / `身体能力` / `知力` | `Combat Sense` / `Physical Ability` / `Intelligence` | glossary lines 500–502 |
| `全長` | `Total Length` | new; the animal-measurement sense, parallel to the `Height` label on the human pages |

- **The furigana is dropped**, as on `s-p004` and `s-p005`. It is a reading aid for Japanese
  readers; `SPIDER WITCH` already carries its own pronunciation, and a phonetic line under English
  display type would read as a design error.
- **The name is a title, not a personal name**, so the family-name-first convention does not apply.
  It is set in caps as display type, matching the two earlier pages.
- **`馬力` is rendered `raw power`, not `horsepower`.** The literal reading would import a machine
  metaphor the English page does not want on a living creature.

### Pass C record

Sent to Codex headless (`gpt-5.6-sol`, medium reasoning, text only, imagegen explicitly forbidden):
the verbatim Japanese by region, the proposed English by region, and the locked constraints
(register ceiling, honorific policy, name order, romanization rules, and the glossary forms that
appear on this page).

Regions 1, 2, 3 and 6 came back clean — the title, the quote's `怖がらせる`/`怖い` wordplay, the
`Total Length` distinction, and `Average.` were all confirmed. Four substantive corrections in the
other three regions, all taken:

**Taken:**

- **`苦手` mis-cast.** `magic-power control is her weak point` recasts a lack of skill as a
  vulnerability. Corrected to `She is not good at magic-power control.`
- **The two `戦闘センス` sentences should not have been joined.** The source delivers them as two
  clipped statements; merging them with `and` softens the profile-entry cadence. Split restored.
  `does not much care for` was also flagged as fussy for `あまり好きではない` → `does not really like`.
- **`軽やかさ` is nimbleness, not lightness.** `lightness` wrongly suggests low weight on a 660 kg
  creature. Corrected to `agility`. A comma after the introductory `For all her bulk` was also
  missing, and `全身の棘` is `the spines all over her body` rather than `covering`.
- **`失策が少ない` reversed.** `rarely caught out by a mistake` means she is rarely *exposed* by
  one; the Japanese says she rarely *makes* one. Corrected to `she rarely makes mistakes`.
  `とにかく堅実` → `thoroughly dependable`, replacing the translationese `Steady above all else`,
  and `目配りが利き` reduced to `observant`, since `attentive to everything around her` overstates it.

**Overridden:**

- None. The reviewer proposed nothing that conflicts with a locked decision, and explicitly endorsed
  keeping `raw power` for `馬力` over `horsepower`.
- One stylistic reversion of my own: the reviewer's closing sentence for `身体能力`, `The tradeoff
  for that agility is that she does not have much raw power`, is flabbier than the source's terse
  `代償に`. Kept as `The price of that agility is low raw power`, which carries `代償` as a price
  and matches the line lengths the other three bars set.
