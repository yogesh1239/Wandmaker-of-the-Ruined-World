## Layout decision

Follow the Volume 3 page exactly, because the two must read as the same recurring furniture:

- **Drop the kana reading line.** It is a pronunciation aid for Japanese readers and has no function
  in an English edition. Its 19 px band plus leading is handed to the description, which is where
  the English needs the room — English descriptions run longer than the Japanese in every slot.
- **Consolidate the heading.** `Characters` already exists on the page, large and pale gray, bleeding
  off the right edge. `登場人物` is its Japanese twin, not a second heading. Delete the Japanese and
  leave `Characters` alone — position, size, colour, letterspacing and bleed unchanged.
- **Nothing else moves.** Portrait thumbnails, their crops and grid positions, the hand-drawn rules,
  column x-positions, row pitch and page margins all stay.

Profile #14 carries the longest description on the page and #17 the shortest; body size and leading
stay uniform across all seventeen regardless, with #14 allowed a third line.

## Render record

**Status: accepted.**

### Method — two renders, merged back with hard-protected portrait columns

`imagegen` was driven through Codex headless. Render 1 came out correct in sixteen of the seventeen
profiles; render 2 was a targeted correction pass over render 1. As on every plate in this volume the
model regenerated the whole page both times, so its work was merged back over the source.

Merge rectangles, all taken from **render 1**: heading `735,60-1439,300`, left text column
`355,95-755,1915`, right text column `942,300-1341,1915`. Every portrait lies outside all three.

Two departures from the generic merge were needed:

1. **The 32 px dilation had to be fenced off.** The gutter between a portrait and its text is only
   11 px (portraits end at x358, text starts at x369), so the dilated alpha reached 35 px into every
   left portrait and 34 px into every right one. Alpha is therefore hard-zeroed over the two
   portrait bands `x180-364` and `x770-948`. Those bounds still cover the source's Japanese, which
   starts at x369 and x956, so no source lettering ghosts through.
2. **The corrected line was transplanted, not the whole render.** Render 2 fixed the spelling but was
   measurably worse everywhere: paper 251.3 against render 1's 254.0 (source 255.0), and sharpness
   19.7 against render 1's 24.2, where render 1 matches the source's own 24.0. Taking render 2
   wholesale would have traded one typo for a softer, gray-cast page. Instead only Zaizen Kintaro's
   first description line was lifted, as a glyph matte on flat white — the flat-ground method — so
   render 2's cast and softness did not come with it. Render 2 sits 14 px lower than render 1 but
   its x-origin is identical (380 against 380), so the line transplants without re-kerning.

Finally the paper was snapped back to a flat 255 wherever nothing dark lies within 5 px. Re-encoding
had left a 1-level 254/255 dither over 30% of the merged columns, which the source encodes as flat
255; the 5 px exclusion means no glyph's antialiasing was clipped.
