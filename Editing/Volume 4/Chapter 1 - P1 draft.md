When it came to pets, dogs and cats were the big two.

The reason was obvious.

Dogs had been humanity's best friends for tens of thousands of years.

Cats had been incredibly cute for tens of thousands of years.

Of course they'd continued to reign as the two biggest pets.

But about five years after electricity vanished from the world and magic and monsters appeared, a rival force had emerged.

Pouch sparrows.

Pouch sparrows were domesticated monsters—that was to say, magic beasts. They were smart, obedient, cute, and easy to keep, and they even had subspace-storage belly pouches like four-dimensional pockets. Their round forms, plump with thick subcutaneous fat, were downright adorable. They were slow, easy prey for predators, but animal lovers could not get enough of them.

As pets, and for delivery work that made use of their storage belly pouches. Pouch sparrows were rapidly gaining ground as both pets and working animals.

Hiyori was crazy about pouch sparrows too. They had to be seriously cute if they could soften the frozen, sharp eyes of Blue Witch-sama, feared as the strongest of the Tokyo Witches' Council.

Hiyori had set up a feeder in her yard and gone birdwatching, so she'd probably been interested in them already. She liked small, fluffy creatures.

One day, as alternating cold and warm spells brought spring closer, Hiyori summoned me to her house in Ome. She was thinking about getting a pouch sparrow and wanted my opinion.

“You can choose the tail-feather color. This one has blue accents, this one's cherry blossom-colored. This pure-white one would have to wait for breeding, but it's pretty and cute.”

“Ah, they're cute, but isn't an all-white one an albino? A pigment disorder. I've heard albinos are frail, so it's probably safer not to.”

We sat together on the living-room sofa, spread out a pouch-sparrow sales catalog on the table, and went back and forth over it.

People pictured witches' familiars as crows and black cats, but reality was nothing like that. Most witches used eyeball-familiar magic to send flying eyeballs around, or else commanded stone golems like the Pebble Witch did.

“What about red? Not your taste? Still want blue?”

“Hmm. Cyanos and my amulet are both blue, so... going with cool colors might work. What about you, Ori? Do you like red? I'm starting to think matching Ori's fire salamanders could work too.”

I clutched my stomach as it started throbbing and panicked.

What? This stomachache really came out of nowhere.

W-Weird. I don't remember eating anything dangerous.
