“Pouch Sparrow Taming Guide”

The following is a guide to taming pouch sparrows (excerpted from the Hokkaido Magic Beast Farm's secret techniques).

To put a pouch sparrow to work, you must first carry out Gremlin implantation using a pouch sparrow's Gremlin according to the standard procedure, then tame it (get it used to people and make it obey).

With Gremlin implantation, a pouch sparrow recognizes the person who received the implant as one of its own, but that alone does not make it obey.

Pouch sparrows are social animals that live in flocks and instinctively obey their leader. Simply undergoing Gremlin implantation will not make them regard you as the leader. In fact, if you do not obey the current leader sparrow, they will show their disapproval (loud warning cries, barring you from the nest, pecking and pulling out the hair (feathers) on the back of your head, and so on).

For pouch sparrows to recognize you as their leader, you must make the greatest contribution of any individual in the flock during nest building.

When their nest is destroyed for some reason, pouch sparrows dismantle the remains, recover the nesting materials, move to whichever location they consider most suitable at the time, and rebuild the nest.

During this process, up to 20 pouch sparrows pool their nesting materials to form a single sturdy apartment complex (colony). To become their leader, you must provide enough high-quality nesting material during nest building.

The exact requirement depends on the quantity and quality of the materials brought by the other individuals building the nest, but as a general guideline, providing any one of the following as nesting material will earn you recognition as the flock's leader.

Vending machine ...... 1 unit

Mailbox ...... 2 units

Microwave oven ...... 6 units

50 kg rocks ...... 8

Keep in mind that automobiles, container houses, and the like are too large. They are considered unsuitable as nesting material and do not count toward your contribution. Low-density, lightweight materials (such as Styrofoam and urethane) are also considered unsuitable.

Flammable materials such as wood and leather are accepted reluctantly only when no more suitable nesting materials are available (they count as only a minor contribution).

Once you are recognized as having made the greatest contribution, a leader-approval ritual is held when nest building is complete. As proof of approval, every member of the flock gently bites the leader's belly pouch with its beak.

Naturally, humans do not have belly pouches, so wear clothing with a pocket over the abdomen. If the would-be leader does not have a belly pouch (belly pocket) when approval takes place, the pouch sparrows will become confused and, in the worst case, may refuse to recognize them as leader. Once recognized as leader, you can go without a belly pocket until the next nest building.

Pouch sparrows are utterly obedient to their leader, even at the cost of their lives, and show extraordinary loyalty. They will even obey an order to serve as emergency rations, trembling all the while.

Never, ever abuse pouch sparrows' loyalty.
