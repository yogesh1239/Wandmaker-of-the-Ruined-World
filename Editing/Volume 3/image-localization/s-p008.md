`Ever since she was old enough to remember, her family, relatives, and classmates constantly called her cute. When she reached adolescence, jealous girls and boys trying to get her attention bullied her. Main, however, was skilled at handling relationships. She turned cuteness into charm, mediated between girls openly and behind the scenes to balance the social power structure, and gracefully deflected or gently rejected boys' interest without hurting their pride. She completely cleared away hostile atmospheres. Even in a troubled class, bullying stopped and cooperation emerged simply because Main joined it. She was practically an air purifier. She does the same in the Tohoku Hunting Association. She also wants to improve at practical work and never neglects self-training.`

`In the Association, she was drawn to Okyaku, a handsome man close to her age with a shadow over him from still grieving his brother. He made her think, I have to support him.
