### Biography

`His real name is Goke Takuji. His childhood nickname was “Takkun.”`

`He uses wolf magic. His grandfather was a landowner who owned a mountain, so Takuji grew familiar with the mountains from an early age. He liked catching freshwater crabs and was good at skipping stones. He was close to his brother Takuro, who was two years older, and played with him more often than with his friends. Around third grade, the brothers began building a secret base together on their grandfather's mountain. They cut bamboo for the frame and roofed it with a blue tarp they found on the riverbank. Takuji did all the hard labor, such as cutting bamboo and carrying stones. He understood that his brother was frail, but privately resented doing all the hard work on a base they were supposed to be building together. His brother was slacking off. He was part of the secret-base project, but did nothing to help and only watched...`

`Then one day, Takuro installed lighting in the secret base. Takuji was stunned. Takuro had modified a coil and miniature bulb from an elementary-school science lesson, built them into a handmade bamboo waterwheel, and used the stream's current to light the base. That was not all. He replaced its windows with glass, and in autumn he gathered fallen katsura leaves to fill the base with their sweet scent. Their shabby childhood fort seemed to become a proper, grown-up hideout. To young Takuji, his brother looked like the greatest architect who had ever lived. He could never compare. His resentment vanished, replaced by wholehearted respect.`

`Even that brother had to die because of the Gremlin Disaster. If they withheld the enormous supply of medicine needed to prolong one frail boy's life, more than a dozen people could be saved. Takuro calmly accepted that cruel weighing of lives and died. His brother—the brother he respected more than anyone—cast aside personal feelings and gave his life for the greater good. Takuji therefore believed he too had to follow the greater good and live impartially and honorably, just as his brother had. He could never allow the sacrifices made to survive the chaos, foremost among them Takuro's, to be rendered meaningless.`

`Okyaku firmly believes this.`
