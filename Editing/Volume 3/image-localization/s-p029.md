## Verbatim Japanese by region

### Entry 1 — heading `呼び方`

`大日向教授が青の魔女を「お姉ちゃん」と呼べば、青山ヒヨリは喜ぶ。喜ぶ一方で、喜んでしまった自分に（妹の代用にしてしまったと思い）罪悪感を感じるに違いない。だから呼ばない。「青さん」という呼び方は、親しみを持ちつつも一線を引く丁度いいラインだと大日向教授は考えている。`

### Entry 2 — heading `ツバキの杖`

`大利がかけっこ一等賞をとったツバキに与えたミニチュア杖。かけっこ銀メダルのモクタンにはぴかぴかのパチンコ玉が、銅メダルのセキタンには縁がギザギザの十円玉（旧円）が与えられている。なお、三匹とも「自分が一番いいものをもらった！」と思っている。`

### Entry 3 — heading `青の魔女の家の乾燥ハーブ`

`青の魔女の妹が病に倒れた時、電気が消え衰退した医学では手の施しようが無かった。藁にも縋る思いで民間医療を始めた青の魔女は、薬草にも頼った。無駄だったとまでは思っていない。その流れで青の魔女は薬草の薬効に詳しく、薬草の限界にも詳しい。薬草は応急処置。ちゃんとしたお医者さんにかかるのが一番。`

### Entry 4 — heading `大狼の毛皮`

`大狼が羽織っている毛皮は、自らを貫くほど強力な攻撃が迫ると毛が逆立ち教えてくれる。頑丈。背後からの奇襲・強襲への備えとして機能するが、気付いたからといって回避や防御が間に合うとは限らないので、過信は禁物。この毛皮は甲2類魔物から剥ぎ取られた物である。兎が対応に出向いたところ、狩猟に失敗し死にかけた。結局超越者四人がかりで万全を期し仕留めたという曰く付き。`

## Exact English by region

### Entry 1 — `Forms of Address`

> If Professor Ohinata were to call the Blue Witch onee-chan, Aoyama Hiyori would be delighted. But glad as she would be, she would surely also feel guilty about that gladness, taking it as having made Ohinata a stand-in for her little sister. So Ohinata does not call her that. To Professor Ohinata's mind, Ao-san strikes exactly the right line: affectionate, yet still drawing a boundary.

### Entry 2 — `Tsubaki's Wand`

> A miniature wand Ori gave to Tsubaki for taking first place in the footrace. Mokutan, who took silver, got a gleaming pachinko ball, and Sekitan, who took bronze, got a milled-edge ten-yen coin in old yen. All three of them, for the record, are convinced they got the best prize of the lot.

### Entry 3 — `The Dried Herbs at the Blue Witch's House`

> When the Blue Witch's little sister fell ill, the electricity was gone and a diminished medicine could do nothing for her. Clutching at straws, the Blue Witch turned to folk remedies, medicinal herbs among them. She does not go so far as to call it wasted effort. That is how she came to know the healing properties of herbs so well — and their limits just as well. Herbs are first aid. Seeing a proper doctor is best.

### Entry 4 — `Okyaku's Pelt`

> The pelt Okyaku wears bristles to warn him whenever an attack powerful enough to run him through is closing in. It is also tough. It works as a guard against ambush and assault from behind, though noticing does not guarantee there is time to dodge or block, so overconfidence is out of the question. The pelt was stripped from a Class A-2 monster. Iwatsura went out to deal with it, botched the hunt, and nearly died; in the end it took four Transcendents together, leaving nothing to chance, to bring it down. The pelt comes with that history attached.

## Notes and uncertainties

- **`お姉ちゃん` → `onee-chan`.** Honorifics and kinship terms are retained per the project's critical rules. Filed prose uses `onee-san` elsewhere; `-chan` is kept here because the source specifically uses the diminutive, and the difference between the two is the entire point of the entry — the whole passage is about which form of address is appropriate. Flagged for addition to `glossary.md`.
- **Ruby flattened.** `兎[イワツラ]` and `大狼[オキヤク]` are filed with ruby. Ruby cannot be typeset on this page, so both are flattened to their baseline readings `Iwatsura` and `Okyaku`, the same decision recorded on `s-p024.md` and `s-p028.md`.
- `縁がギザギザの十円玉` is the pre-1959 milled-edge ten-yen coin, a minor collector's item in Japan; rendered `milled-edge ten-yen coin` so the English reader understands it is a specific and slightly special coin rather than ordinary change. `旧円` follows the glossary as `old yen`, in contrast to the `new yen` introduced on `s-p024` and `s-p027`.
- `かけっこ` is a children's footrace; rendered `the footrace`, definite, since the source refers to a specific event the three magic beasts ran.
- The entry heading `呼び方` is literally "way of calling / form of address"; rendered `Forms of Address`.
- Band 3 being empty is a property of the source, not an oversight, and is preserved. The English content is shorter than the band-2 boundary allows, which is expected.
