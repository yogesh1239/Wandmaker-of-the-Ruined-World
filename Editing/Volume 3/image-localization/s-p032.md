### 01 — Magic-School Customization
```
Basically it's the sort of thing you see all the time with game gear. Your Intelligence drops but your Strength goes up. Your MP drops but your HP goes up. Gear that comes with a drawback like that is stronger than gear that's all upside, isn't it? Accepting the drawback is exactly what makes the upside bigger. Specializing in freezing magic at the cost of making your other magic harder to use runs on the same logic. Let's build some peaky gear.
```

### 02 — Secret Techniques of the Tohoku Hunting Association
```
This is the chapter where a new town and a new job class unlock. From nothing but "the first village" and "Job: Mage", you unlock "the second village" and "Job: Hunter", and the world opens out. The story is moving in places the protagonist knows nothing about, and plenty is happening there. Tokyo was not the only place that had a hard time getting through the Gremlin Disaster.
```

### 03 — The Flame Witch
```
So far I've written good sorts and bad sorts, sorts drifting through life, sorts having a hard time of it, sorts scheming in the shadows — all kinds. Time for a pinch of pervert. The world may perish, but perverts do not. If anything, a pervert tenacious enough to survive the world's collapse must be a pervert of a very high order.
```

### 04 — Himori Wand
```
A catastrophe struck, and the elder sister, reduced to something no longer human, protected her family and the people around her with everything she had. In time, her life all but spent, she was sealed inside a wand. The younger sister takes that wand her sister is sealed in as her tie to her, and lives on facing forward with strength. What a lovely story.
```

### 05 — A Black Marketeer Talks About 0933
```
The Watarigarasu that Shirokarasu leads are scum. But scum will never disappear from this world. Even if you could wipe them all out once, they would well up again without fail. If there is no curing it anyway, then let the relatively tolerable scum rule the underworld and have them clear out the nastier scum for you, through turf wars and fights over vested interests — that is the logic by which Foresight keeps the Watarigarasu alive. And the Watarigarasu know their place well enough. The ones who couldn't grasp it are dead, so of course they do.
```

### 06 — Let's Put Monster Materials to Use
```
You go to all the trouble of killing a monster and then you can't use what it drops — what a ridiculous world that would be. But wouldn't it be exciting if, after you killed a monster, there were a skinning event or a quality-appraisal event, and raising your butchering skill got you better materials? I'm piling up a lot of fussy reasoning here, but that is what this chapter is about. Only those who know the right method can draw high value out of something worthless.
```

### 07 — Fire Salamander
```
Enter the happy-go-lucky pet with complicated family circumstances. Mimimi! Isn't there something romantic about a magic tool forged in magical fire? That sort of thing brings a sword to mind more than a wand, though. A wand made with fire salamander fire is, if anything—
```

**The `白鴉` / `渡鴉` distinction is the trap the handoff flags.** They are not variants of one name: `白鴉` (Shirokarasu) is the person who leads, `渡鴉` (Watarigarasu) is the Suginami organization she leads. Rendering it as the latter would erase the agent and misstate the plot.
