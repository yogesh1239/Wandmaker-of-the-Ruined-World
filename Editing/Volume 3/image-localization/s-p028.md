### Entry 3 — heading `茂布翔掠（もぶ しょうりゃく）`

### Entry 4 — heading `モエカ`

`渡鴉きっての武闘派構成員。モエカは源氏名。本名は白鴉だけが知っている。可愛らしい童顔と華奢な細身は手弱女に見えるが、治安が完全に崩壊した修羅の国さいたま市であらゆる汚い手を使い生き残っていた猛者。白鴉が与えた抗生物質によって死の淵から救われており、白鴉を絶対に裏切らない。人差し指の指輪のグレムリンを発動媒体に使い、パンチのインパクトに氷槍魔法を重ねて叩き込み敵を滅殺する。火事場の馬鹿力を意図的に出す事ができ、ステゴロだけでもやたらと強い。`

### Entry 5 — heading `山熊`

`北海道魔獣農場が擁する最強の巨大魔獣。手から光線魔法を放てるのだが、この光線は無生物をすり抜け生物にだけ命中する。とても強く、相性次第では弱い超越者を倒すポテンシャルがある。蛇が苦手。`

## Exact English by region

### Header band (right-aligned)

`Story Settings`

### Entry headings and bodies, in reading order

**1 — `Dog (Seta)`**

> A mage of the Tohoku Hunting Association and a user of light magic. He has grown dog ears and a dog tail. He has a nose for magic: when a spell is cast nearby, he gets a rough sense of what it does. He is the mage who gave his assessment of the Monster Trap's effect that Okyaku mentioned. By blood, he is the grandson of Itazu's elder sister.

**2 — `The Northern Witch`**

> The young witch of the Tokyo Witches' Council who governs Kita Ward. She is known for staying in the shape of an adolescent child without aging in the slightest. This is not because her lifespan is long, but because her constitution holds a youthful appearance for a long time; her lifespan itself is much the same as an ordinary human's. She can unfurl small wings of light at both ankles and fly — an innate ability that comes from her constitution rather than from incantation magic. In this and that, she is rather like a magical girl.

**3 — `Mobu Shoryaku`**

> A thug who held Setagaya Ward as his turf. A strong fighter, and sensitive to killing intent — the will to attack. He could tell whether even a monster was about to come at him, which was no small thing, and it saved his life more than once. He kept a junior from his high-school days as his underling and threw his weight around for a while, and then he died. He was no match for a genuine berserker out of Saitama, that land of carnage, who could hide his killing intent.

**4 — `Moeka`**

> Watarigarasu's foremost fighter. Moeka is a working alias; only Shirokarasu knows her real name. Her sweet baby face and slender, delicate build make her look like a gentle thing, but she is a hardened survivor who lived through Saitama City — that land of carnage, where public order had collapsed completely — by every dirty trick there was. Antibiotics Shirokarasu gave her pulled her back from the brink of death, and she will never betray him. She uses the Gremlin in her index-finger ring as an activation medium, layering ice-spear magic onto the impact of her punches to annihilate her enemies. She can call up hysterical strength at will, and is absurdly strong with bare fists alone.

**5 — `Mountain Bear`**

> The strongest giant magic beast the Hokkaido Magic Beast Farm has. It can fire beam magic from its hands, but the beam passes straight through inanimate objects and strikes only living things. It is very strong, and depending on the match-up it has the potential to bring down a weaker Transcendent. It is no good with snakes.

**6 — `Ori's Sugoroku Supremacy`**

> Ori is deft-handed, so he can control the roll of a die at will. Fully manual cheating is within his reach. Facing the Blue Witch, he declared he could beat her standing on his head, then actually won a board game while doing a handstand and was told how creepy that was. Impressive and creepy are perfectly able to coexist.
