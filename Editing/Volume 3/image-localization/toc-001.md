## Production prompt

> Text-localization of the supplied source image. Treat the supplied image as the edit target. This is a text-only front-matter contents page: no illustration to protect, but the restrained typographic design must be preserved and must read as an official English edition's contents page.
>
> **Match the accepted Volume 2 contents page** `English/Volume 2/localized-images/toc-001.jpg` for face, weight, hierarchy and leader treatment.
>
> **Preserve exactly:** the portrait canvas, and the flat pure-white field RGB(255,255,255) edge to edge — no gradient, texture, band, stripe, panel, border, rule, ornament, logo or page number beyond what is listed. Type is dark on white.
>
> **Structure, top to bottom:**
> 1. `Contents` — the heading, already Latin in the source. Keep it letter-spaced in the same serif, at the same position: ink top y 301, left x 272, about 31 px ink height.
> 2. Eighteen entry rows on a uniform 76 px pitch, first row's ink top at y 410, last at y 1729. Each row: the title, left-aligned with its ink starting at x 272; then a single thin horizontal leader rule, vertically centred on the row, **always ending at x 941**; then the page number.
> 3. The credit line at y 1819, left edge x 270.
>
> **Leader rules:** one thin rule per row, starting a consistent gap after the title's last glyph and always ending at x 941. Do not use dot leaders. Do not let any rule touch the title or the number. If a title is long enough that the gap would fall below about 25 px, shorten nothing — reduce that row's title tracking slightly instead, and never shrink the type of one row alone.
>
> **Page numbers:** bold, **LEFT-aligned with the first digit starting at x 978** on every row. Do not right-align them. Three-digit numbers therefore extend further right than two-digit ones — that is correct and matches the source. Set the numbers exactly as given; they are the original print pagination and must not be recalculated, renumbered or reordered.
>
> **Last row:** set `Side Story` as a smaller prefix label, then a clear space, then `Three Red Ones, One Blue One` at the normal entry size. Do not use a hyphen between them, and do not set the prefix at the same size as the title.
>
> **Credit line:** `Frontispiece & Interior Illustrations ● Kayahara`, small, dark, left-aligned at x 270. Keep the filled-circle separator `●` between the label and the name, with a space either side.
>
> Render every English string verbatim with exact capitalization, spacing and punctuation. Do not paraphrase, omit, duplicate, abbreviate, translate, invent or garble text. Do not add a subtitle, section header, part divider, publisher name or any text not listed.
>
> **Typography:** professional kerning, even leading, optically consistent baselines across all 18 rows. No Japanese; no vertical, rotated or stacked lettering; no one-letter-per-line stacking; no automatic hyphenation; no overlap, clipping or watermarks.

## Notes and uncertainties

- The page is entirely typographic; this is a type-reset rather than a text-replacement-over-art edit.
- English titles are substantially longer than the Japanese, so several leader rules will be much shorter than in the source. This is expected and is what the accepted Volume 2 page does; the fixed rule end at x 941 is what keeps the column of numbers aligned.
- The two longest entries — `Secret Techniques of the Tohoku Hunting Association` and `Secret Techniques of the Hokkaido Magic Beast Farm` — are the rows most at risk of crowding their leader rule. Flagged in the prompt with an explicit minimum gap and instructions to adjust tracking rather than type size.
- No illegible regions. Every Japanese string has one certain, source-supported English rendering.

## Render record

- **Rendered:** deterministic Pillow raster composite; no image-generation model. One correction pass.
- **Output:** `English/Volume 3/localized-images/toc-001.jpg` — real JPEG, 1440 × 2048, RGB, quality 95, 4:4:4.
- **Field QA:** pure white RGB(255,255,255) at all four corners and centre.
- **Structure QA:** heading at x 272, ink top y 301, 31 px ink. Eighteen entry rows, uniform 76 px pitch measured (single value across all gaps), first ink top y 410, last y 1702. Credit line at x 270, ink top y 1819, `●` separator present.
- **Anchor QA:** every title's ink starts at x 272–273; every leader ends at x 941; every page number's first digit starts at x 978. All three verified independently on all 18 rows.
- **Numbers:** 13, 23, 40, 61, 70, 91, 104, 119, 136, 149, 164, 180, 190, 201, 213, 230, 250, 273 — as measured from the source, strictly ascending, unchanged.
- **Last row:** `Side Story` at 20 px ink against the title's 30 px, no hyphen between them.
- **Spec arithmetic corrected during the render.** The measured-geometry table gives the entry block as y 410–1729, and the production prompt wrongly restated 1729 as the *last row's ink top*. With 18 rows at a 76 px pitch the last ink top is 410 + 17 × 76 = 1702; 1729 is that row's ink bottom. The renderer flagged the contradiction and kept the measured pitch, which was correct. Corrected here so the figure is not propagated.
- **Correction pass — collapsed leader rules.** The first render left 15 px leaders (x 927–941) on the two longest rows, `Secret Techniques of the Tohoku Hunting Association` and `Secret Techniques of the Hokkaido Magic Beast Farm`, whose titles ran to x 901 and x 900. At that length the rule stops reading as a leader and reads as an en-dash between the title and the page number — "…Hunting Association – 23" — i.e. as punctuation belonging to the text. Every other row measured 76–481 px, and the accepted Volume 2 page never falls below about 108 px, so the stubs were outside anything the series shows.

  The fix was specified as a rule applied to **every** row rather than a patch to two, so the page stays internally consistent: a leader shorter than 60 px gets that row's tracking reduced by up to 1.0 px per character until it reaches 60 px, and if that is still insufficient the leader is omitted entirely in favour of clean whitespace. Omission is the better failure mode — a missing leader reads as the line running out, which is ordinary, whereas a stub reads as content.

  Both rows resolved by tracking alone: row 2 at −0.900 px/character and row 8 at −0.918 px/character, each pulling its title end back to x 856 for a 60 px leader. No type size changed on any row, and visual inspection at 2× confirms neither line reads as condensed beside its neighbours. Post-correction measurement finds no leader between 1 and 59 px anywhere on the page.
